## .what

**`decost`** is one of the three rock categories — the mainquest that drives what
ahbode **keeps** out of what it earns.

it occupies the `{category}` slot of a rock goal uri, and that slot is a **closed
set of three**: `decost` | `ensell` | `entool`
(`rule.require.rock-goal-treestruct`).

```
ahbode/decost/twilio-tune/waste-audit
       ^^^^^^
```

## 🔴 .its scope is WIDER than the word reads — it is KEEP, not merely SPEND

the surface read is *"cut the bill"*, and that read is too narrow. the declared
scope is the wisher's, verbatim:

> **decost = keep what earn**

⇒ so `decost` covers **every leak between revenue earned and revenue kept** —
infra spend, vendor waste, unbilled work, a refund we did not have to issue, a
plan we undercharged for. the test is not *"is it an invoice line?"* but:

> **does this reduce what ahbode keeps out of what it earns?**

yes → `decost`, whatever shape the leak takes.

## .the `en-` / `de-` morphology carries weight

| prefix | direction | categories |
|---|---|---|
| `de-` | **cuts** the noun it names | `decost` — cut the cost |
| `en-` | **builds** the noun it names | `ensell`, `entool` |

⚠️ **the prefix binds the noun, so a coinage must survive that re-read.** a
category is not free to name its own intent — the morphology re-reads it.

## .the boundary — decost vs its two peers

| a row that… | files under |
|---|---|
| reduces what we spend, or plugs a leak in what we keep | `decost` |
| raises what we earn — a new customer, a new plan, a new channel | `ensell` |
| makes the **tools** better, whatever they build | `entool` |

🟡 the org split does independent work: `decost` and `ensell` are **ahbode**
(the product); `entool` is **rhachet** (the tools).
