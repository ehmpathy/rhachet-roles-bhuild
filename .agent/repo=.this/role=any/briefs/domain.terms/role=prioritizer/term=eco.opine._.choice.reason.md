# domain.term.choice.reason: opine

## .etymology

from the verb **to opine** — to state a view as one's own. the noun form is a
back-formation, and it is deliberate: the word carries the **act of assertion**
inside it, which is exactly the property that parts it from a quant.

a quant is true or false about the world. an opine is a person's stated view,
so it is **owned** rather than merely correct. that ownership is what earns it
the rank order.

## .why not the rejected words

| rejected | why |
|---|---|
| `opinion` | the obvious word, and it reads as *"merely an opinion"* — dismissive of the field that sets the rank. `opine` keeps the sense and drops the shrug |
| `judgment` | eight letters beside `quant`'s five, so the render pair loses its symmetry. also overloaded: a judge renders a judgment in the route vocabulary (`route.stone.judge`) |
| `call` | far too general. this repo already says "the call is yours" in a dozen briefs, so `call.sev` would collide with prose everywhere |
| `claim` | close, and it is what the `.what` says an opine IS. rejected because `claim` is the GENUS — a quant of an estimated gain is also a claim (`rule.require.enumerate-before-you-name`: a word that covers both sides of a contrast is not a contrast word) |
| `grade` | implies a scale handed down from outside, and both `sev` and `urg` are chosen by the person in front of the keyboard |
| `assessment` | names a process, not a field |

🔴 **`claim` is the near miss worth the record.** it reads perfectly against
`opine.sev`, and it fails the moment you hold it against the other family: an
estimated `quant.gain` of "about $150/mo" is a claim too. so `claim` names what
both families share and parts them not at all — the same failure `knowledge`
has against a brief, and `tool` against a skill.

## .evidence

### the discovery — the wisher's own split, 2026-09-13

> "and yeah, asks = a quant, the rest = an opine"

one sentence, and it settled both words at once. the rest of the exchange
established the direction between them:

> "urg and sev gets informed from the quants like asks"

⇒ **informed**, not driven. that verb is the whole invariant, and it is why
`ORDER_BY` puts the quants last and `markQuantDisagreement` raises a mark
rather than a rewrite.

### why the direction cannot be reversed

a quant is narrow by construction. `asks` counts a reach for a record;
`gain.cash` counts one cell of the 3x2 matrix in `define.cost-gain-matrix`,
which also declares `gain.time`, `gain.rank`, `cost.cash` and `cost.rank` — four
cells this store does not yet hold.

⇒ so a rank driven by quants alone would rank by **whatever happens to be
countable today**, and would silently re-rank every time a new cell is added.
the opine is where a human puts the four cells the store cannot see, plus
whatever the matrix does not model at all.

### the boundary

`eco`. "opine, of WHAT?" → of an `eco.priority`. one word, so
`rule.require.boundary-qualified-terms` is satisfied at authorship rather than
after a collision.
