## .etymology

both values are ordinary english reached for at face value, and the pair was chosen because the
two words already carry the **temporal** relation the dimension encodes:

| value | the plain sense | what the goal does |
|---|---|---|
| **solve** | to work a problem through to its answer | stands between you and the money, and **creates** it |
| **clamp** | a device that holds a part shut under load | stands behind the money, and **keeps** it |

`clamp` was not coined here. it was already live in this corpus at a narrower grain —
`term=clamp` declares *"a **test** whose job is to hold a repaired defect shut"* — so the goal
dimension **widened a word the domain already spoke** rather than import a new one. that reuse is
deliberate and it is also the cluster's one open boundary question (below).

`effect` names the axis rather than either value: the dimension answers *what a goal does to the
gain*, and "effect" is the plainest word for one part's action upon another.

## .why not the rejected words

the `.choice._.md` forbids five synonyms. the arguments, expanded:

| candidate | why not |
|---|---|
| 🔴 `kind` | ⛔ **TAKEN.** `kind` already names the rock kind on a goal — big / alt / sub. to overload it would make *"a goal's kind"* ambiguous at the exact grain both senses apply to, which is `rule.forbid.domain-term-ambiguity`'s worked failure shape: *"$word, of WHAT?"* returns two answers |
| `type` | ⛔ `rule.prefer.kind-over-type` rejects `type` house-wide — and `kind`, the word it would defer to, is taken anyway, so the substitution has nowhere to land |
| `category` | ⛔ a category is an open set; this dimension is a **closed pair**. the word invites a third value by its own grammar, and the arithmetic below cannot take one |
| `phase` | ⛔ reads as **sequence** — that a goal passes from solve into clamp. it does not. a goal is one or the other for its whole life, and a clamp is often authored before its solve ships |
| `stage` | same defect as `phase`, plus a collision with the deploy-stage sense (`test` / `prep` / `prod`) that the org already speaks |

⇒ the common thread: `kind` and `type` are **occupied**, `category` mis-states the cardinality,
and `phase`/`stage` mis-state the relation as temporal succession when it is a fixed property.

## .the name fulcrum — `solve` is settled by POSITION, never by act

the recoverable half of the promised fulcrum. the wisher's narrow read — *"solves = repairs
immediately"* — leaves the twilio **audit** unclassifiable: it measures where the waste sits and
repairs not one byte of it, yet nobody would call it a clamp.

⇒ so the term was settled **wider than the phrase that prompted it**: *is this work on the path to
the gain?* a scout that finds the defect is on that path as surely as the repair that closes it,
because you cannot fix what you have not found.

🟡 **the open third value.** find / fix / hold is a real three-step, and `scout` is a live candidate
for it. it earns no value today because the arithmetic needs exactly **two** buckets and a scout's
hours sit in the same one as a repair's. left wet (`rule.prefer.wet-over-dry`) until a reader needs
the split for a question the pair cannot answer.

## .the boundary — `goal`, and why it is not a tautology

*"an effect, of WHAT?"* → **of a goal.** the boundary is what the dimension is a property OF, so
`goal` is what it attaches to rather than a genus that says naught.

⇒ this is the same resolution its peer reached. `term=goal.surgoal._.choice.reason.md` records a
prior argument that `goal` was *"the genus, not the boundary"* and then overturns it: a word that
names a **position or property relative to a goal** is bounded by `goal`, and only a word that
names a **kind of goal** would find `goal` redundant. `effect` is the former.

## .evidence — the concept was found in prose before it had a word

the dimension was not coined in the abstract. the distinction appears, unnamed, in the dispatch
that later became the clamp half of the worked pair:

> *"the parent line carries **USD 100.00/mo**, estimated. this row does not ADD to it — it
> **holds it**"*

source: `nheuron:src/stream/2026.Q3/2026-09-13.dispatch.repo=svc-notifications.behavior=twilio-waste-alert.wish.md:73`

⇒ that sentence is the whole term, written before anyone reached for a word. the row beside it in
the same file states the consequence the dimension exists to serve: *"a row that protects a monthly
cut and a row that creates one are worth different amounts."*

the measured case that forced it into a **stored** column rather than a note is carried in the
`.choice._.md`: `twilio-tune`, 2026-09-13, where the store's first ever ⚖️ flag was a false
positive because its denominator charged the wrong bucket.

## ⚠️ .the open boundary dispute — genus or species against `term=clamp`

`term=clamp` (flat, supervisor-homed) declares a **test** that holds a repaired defect shut. this
cluster uses the same word at the **goal** grain, and wider: the twilio alert delivers a CI check
(a `test.clamp`) **and** a runtime raise, which that cluster explicitly rejects as *"a canary."*

⇒ one concept at two boundaries, which is a legal split rather than an overload. the genus/species
question stays open and is filed as a dated dispute in `term=clamp._.choice.reason.md`, 2026-09-13.

🔴 **and the lift widened the gap.** `term=clamp` stays with the supervisor while this cluster homes
under the prioritizer, so the two now sit in **different roles** and no single boot loads both. a
reader who meets one will not meet the other by accident. the dispute was cheap to hold while the
words shared a directory; it is no longer.
