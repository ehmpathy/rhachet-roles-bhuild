# domain.term: sprout

term.chosen   = sprout
term.kind     = verb
term.synonyms.forbidden:
- pull
- boot
- spin up
- kick off
- start
- claim

## .what

to **dispatch a tree** — a worktree, its ducts, and a bound behavior. work STARTS now.

it is the **only** word that authorizes a tree boot. its absence forbids one.

```
sprout  →  rhx git.tree.behavior --into $org/$repo --name beav/… --size … --wish …
```

## .the two properties the word carries

| property | what it means |
|---|---|
| **the act begins** | a sprout breaks ground; a seed waits |
| **it is expensive and hard to unwind** | a branch, a worktree, two ducts, a terminal, an install, a bound route — and `git.tree.del` REFUSES where the tree holds work |

the second is why it is gated behind one literal token rather than behind a judgment.

## .a sprout is NOT

- **a promise a tree now EXISTS** — it names the ACT of dispatch. a sprout may boot a worktree,
  be answered by work that needs no tree, or fail to take and be felled. verify from
  `rhx duct.list` and the filesystem, per `rule.require.verify-after-send`
- **a relaxation of any worktree rule** — a sprout still needs a bound behavior
  (`rule.require.behaviors-over-adhoc`) and still needs a human ok above nano
  (`rule.require.confirm-behavior-size-above-nano`)
- **a seed** — see `term=seed._.choice._.md`, its exact counterpart
