## .what round 11 genuinely ADDS — and the first item is a correction

1. 🔴 **it refutes the prior frame.** round 10 defined the class as *"bytes in the **scrollback**
   that retain the shape of a live state."* too narrow: `⎿ Login successful` sits on the **visible**
   pane and is the costliest instance measured. ⇒ **location is not the discriminator**, and a word
   keyed to scrollback would have encoded the wrong test
2. **two instances round 10 did not hold** — the leg-2 login line, and the spent-oauth-url case
   that `__url_best` fights
3. **the axis result** below: a relic cannot be *ordered* against a live marker at all

## .etymology

**relic** — an object that survives from a past era, after the world that produced it moved on.

the fit is **survival past its moment**, never the veneration a sacred relic carries. a relic line
does no work and changes no state; it merely **occupies the surface a reader scans**, and a reader
who cannot part it from a live marker will misread the pane.

| **`stale line`** | implies the line was true and has decayed. it has not: a record of a past event stays exactly as true as it ever was. what expired is the **reading**, and the word must point at the reader rather than the text |
| **`old output`** | age is not the property. a relic is a hazard from the instant it is printed; a line printed a second ago is as dangerous as one from an hour back |
| **`leftover`** | too weak — implies surplus rather than a claim that will be actively mis-parsed |
| **`echo`** | TAKEN, and doubly. `echo` is a shell builtin the skills call constantly, and a duct's own command echo (`❯ /login`) is one instance of relic rather than the class |
| **`history`** | names the whole record, deliberately kept. relic names the subset a live reader will trip over |
| **`husk`** | the near miss, and rejected on the subject test — a husk is a whole PANE whose process died. a relic is one LINE in a pane whose process thrives. one is a container, the other a claim inside it |

## .the evidence

### 1. the enumeration — four instances, and it followed `rule.require.enumerate-before-you-name`

the word was **not** coined against the instance in hand. the instance in hand was `Login
successful`; three more were enumerated before a word was chosen, and two of them changed the
outcome:

- `cap` and `plea` already carry a `SEEN` render suffix, adopted for precisely this reason
- the spent-oauth-url case is fought by `__url_best`, whose own comment names the hazard

⇒ so the concept was **twice-solved and unnamed**. that is the strongest case a term can have:
the repo had already paid for it, in two places, without a word to hold it.

⚠️ had the enumeration stopped at one instance, `scrollback` would have read as an acceptable
word — the `__url_best` case genuinely IS scrollback. it is instance three that refutes it.

### 2. the measurement — 2026-09-08, `grove-ahbode-v20260811` and `grove-ahbode-v20260901`

`git.grove.auth` leg 2 keyed its screen `case` on `Login successful` as a peer arm beside the
live-repl marker. because a repl retains that line on its visible pane, the arm matched on every
pass and the repl arm — placed last on purpose — was **unreachable by construction**.

the cost, in order:

| round | what happened |
|---|---|
| 1–4 | four swaps printed `⚠️ the repl never came up, though the credential DID land`. harmless, because the credential terminus was the real verdict. **survived precisely because it blocked no run** |
| 5 | a supervisor claimed the repl marker was fixed, saw the warning again, and hypothesized **scrollback staleness** |
| 6 | a live-pane read refuted that in one call. the truth was relic on the visible pane |

🔴 **the wrong hypothesis was reasonable and would have shipped.** narrowing `capture-pane -S -200`
is a plausible-sounding cure that repairs no defect. what caught it was `rule.require.trust-but-verify`
— reading the pane rather than reasoning about it.

### 3. the structural finding — a relic cannot be ordered against a live marker

this is the part worth more than the incident. the repair was **not** a reorder, and could not be:

- an **unanswered select** must outrank the repl — a status line renders beneath a live modal
- a **dead record** must not outrank the repl — else the repl is unreachable

both are true, and one ordered `case` has exactly one slot. ⇒ the two claims are **different in
kind**, so they belong on different axes: `$kind` ranks screens by answered state, `$repl` reads
the terminus independently.

> a relic is not a lower-priority marker. it is a marker of another TYPE, and a reader that
> ranks the types together will always sacrifice one of them.

## .disputes

### dispute: what parts a relic from a husk — raised 2026-09-08 — status: OPEN

the two rounds that enumerated this class disagree, and the disagreement is **internal to round
10** as well as between them.

| | how it parts relic from husk | so a husk's chrome is… |
|---|---|---|
| **round 10** | the **alive/dead** axis — *"husk: process dead, retains a live ARTIFACT's shape · relic: process ALIVE, retains a past STATE's shape"* | ⛔ excluded by that axis — yet round 10 **lists it as instance 4** |
| **round 11** | the **grain** — a husk is a whole PANE, a relic one LINE within it | ⛔ excluded, since a husk's process is dead |

- **claim** — they are one class over two grains: a husk is a relic *of the pane grain*, every
  other instance is a relic *of the line grain*. liveness then falls out as incidental rather than
  definitional, and round 10's list becomes self-consistent
- **counter** — round 10 chose the alive/dead axis deliberately, citing `wedge` as precedent for a
  third term on it. to demote that axis is to weaken a parting the repo already uses twice, and a
  relic that spans dead processes overlaps `husk` enough to invite the ambiguity
  `rule.forbid.domain-term-ambiguity` forbids
- **resolution** — unsettled. ⚠️ **the honest state is that neither round noticed the conflict**,
  because each enumerated alone. **do NOT decide it from the desk** — the discriminator that
  survives is the one a live classifier can compute, so the sweep named in `.the arrears` (which
  detectors key on retained lines?) should run **first** and settle this with instances

⚠️ `scrollback` needed no dispute — instance three refuted it outright rather than argued it.
