# domain.term: braid

term.chosen   = braid
term.kind     = noun
term.boundary = repo
term.synonyms.forbidden:
- duplicate
- parallel path
- fork

## .what

a **braid** is a second path laid beside a serviceable one, **because its author did not look**.

the word is adopted verbatim from `philosophy.pavement-saves-nature`, where hikers step off a
muddy trail to keep their boots clean, each detour becomes a faint new path, and within a season
one tread is five parallel scars.

⇒ **no single step is unreasonable. the damage is entirely emergent.** that is the whole
difficulty of the term: every braid-step is locally rational, so a braid is never caught by the
author who lays it — only by a later reader who sees all the scars at once.

## .the shapes it covers

| the braid | the serviceable path beside it |
|---|---|
| a `duct.*` call, after a crew verb errored | the crew verb, **mended** |
| a defect seeded a second time | the open seed that already names it |
| a term paved here that a peer repo already declares | the extant cluster, cited |
| a hand-rolled command where a skill exists | the skill |
| a brief re-derived because no one grepped | the brief |

## 🔴 .the discriminator is the LOOK, never the count

two paths are not a braid. **two paths where the second author did not know the first existed**
are a braid.

| two paths, and… | verdict |
|---|---|
| the author never looked | 🔴 **a braid** |
| the author looked, found the first, and **routed around** it rather than mend it | 🔴 **a braid**, said out loud |
| the author looked, found the first, and states why a second is owed | ✅ **not a braid** — a documented pair |
| the second path serves a different need, and the split is recorded | ✅ not a braid |

⇒ so a braid is diagnosed from the **provenance**, never from the artifact. two files that look
identical may be one braid and one deliberate pair, and only the record parts them.

## .what it is NOT

| not a braid | why |
|---|---|
| a **rut** | conformity to a found path with **no test** that it leads anywhere. a gap of skepticism; a braid is a gap of attention |
| a **workaround**, recorded | one pass around a defect, named as such. it becomes a braid once it hardens into a second path that persists |
| a **fork** | a deliberate divergence, declared |
