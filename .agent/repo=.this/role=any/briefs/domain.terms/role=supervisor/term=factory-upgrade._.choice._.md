# term = factory upgrade

## .the choice

**`factory upgrade`** — a change to the **machinery we operate** (a skill, a hook, a brief, a
route, a role) that raises **safety**, lowers **defects**, or raises **speed** for every future
run.

## .kind

**domain object** — `[...noun]`, shape `factory upgrade`.

it names a *record*: an observation captured so it outlives the session that produced it. the
observation and the eventual repair are distinct — a factory upgrade is the former.

## .the three gains

every factory upgrade claims at least one, and states which:

| gain | what it raises | example |
|------|----------------|---------|
| **safety** | the floor under a wrong move | a skill that exits before its own safety gate |
| **defects** | what gets caught, so less ships | a poll that misreports a stalled duct |
| **speed** | throughput per operator-hour | a hook gap that stalls a clone on every run |

## .the boundary that makes it a distinct term

a factory upgrade is **not** the work we were dispatched to do. it is a repair to the tools,
found while the tools were in use. dispatched work has a wish, a route, and a deadline that
compels it; a factory upgrade has none of those, which is precisely why it needs a mechanism.

## .forbidden synonyms

do not reach for these — each one distorts a different edge:

| ⛔ synonym | why it distorts |
|-----------|-----------------|
| `tech debt` | implies a shortcut once taken and now owed. most of these were never owed — they are discoveries, not deferrals |
| `chore` | understates. a safety upgrade is not upkeep |
| `tool fix` | narrows to repair, and drops the case where a tool that already works gets raised |
| `meta-work` | defines by what it is not; carries no gain axis |
| `infra fix` | collides with deployment infrastructure, a different domain |
| `improvement` | too broad to bound; every change claims to be one |
