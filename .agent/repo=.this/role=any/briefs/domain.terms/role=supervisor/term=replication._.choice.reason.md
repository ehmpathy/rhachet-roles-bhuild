# domain.term.choice.reason: replication

## .etymology

`replication` comes from metascience, where it is set against **reproduction** in a split this repo
had already half-adopted without notice:

| the word | the canon's sense |
|---|---|
| **reproduction** | the same data, the same method, re-run — does the analysis hold up? |
| **replication** | new data, an independent party — does the RESULT hold up? |

`term=false-report._.choice.reason.md` uses `reproduction` in exactly the canonical sense — *"the
reproduction — single variable, isolated"*, and *"four reproductions, two months, one hypothesis,
never once tested."* that section's whole lesson is that a reproduction proves the observation and
never the diagnosis.

so half the pair was already in use, at exactly the job the canon gives it. **the other half was the
unnamed one**, and it carried the heaviest load in the glossary.

## .the discovery — a word three clusters call their best evidence and none can name

this term was not proposed from taste. a grep over `domain.terms/` for the phrase the files actually
use returned it already load-bearing in three places:

```
term=false-report._.choice._.md:442        "…a party that had never read this file. that is the
                                            term's strongest validation to date"
term=partial-audit._.choice.reason.md:625  "…camp-grove had never read this file, and calls that the
                                            class of support which closed substituted-criterion's
                                            weakest leg"
term=substituted-criterion._.choice._.md:76 "…the standard the party that does the work re-derived on
                                            its own, unprompted, and wrote a blocker against"
```

three clusters, three separate rounds, one concept, no word. that is precisely the state
`im_an.obsessive_learner.for.domain.terms.md` names as a lesson at risk — and it matches
`term=down._.choice.reason.md`'s own phrase for the same shape: *a word the fleet leans on, known
well enough to indict and not well enough to define.*

**this term was discovered, never invented** — and the demonstration is recursive, which is the part
worth the record: the evidence that `replication` belongs to the domain is that the domain kept to
reach for it, unnamed, in three separate files.

## .the rejected synonyms

| ⛔ synonym | why it distorts |
|-----------|-----------------|
| `reproduction` | **taken, and it is this term's complement.** `false report` uses it for a re-run of one's own method, and that file's sharpest line — *"a reproduction is not a diagnosis"* — depends on the two words apart. to merge them would erase the distinction that lesson rests on |
| `corroboration` | `false report` owns it for two instruments on one question, where a DERIVED pair corroborates while both are wrong. that word names agreement about a MEASUREMENT; this one names agreement about a DISTINCTION |
| `convergence` | overloaded twice over in the driver vocabulary — `converge-with-reviewers`, `converge-to-terminal` — and it carries the opposite prerequisite. two parties converge THROUGH contact; a replication demands they never had any |
| `confirmation` | over-claims. a replication says the distinction sits in the domain; it does not say the cut is correct. `confirmation` invites the reader to stop |
| `validation` | the glossary already uses it loosely as a verdict word (*"strongest validation to date"*). to promote a loose word to a term is to inherit its looseness |
| `second opinion` | implies the second party was ASKED. the whole property here is that nobody asked — an asked party has been handed the frame, which is transmission |

## .the disputes

### dispute: independent discovery — raised 2026-08-26 — status: RESOLVED (keep `replication`)

- raised.by  = beaver 🦫 (self-raised while the cluster was paved)
- claim      = `independent discovery` says plainly what happens and needs no citation to metascience
- counter    = it is two words where one serves, and neither slots against `reproduction`, which this
               repo ALREADY uses in its canonical sense. the value of `replication` is not that it
               describes the act — it is that it completes a pair the repo half-owns, so a reader who
               knows one gets the other free. `discovery` is also taken: `def.domain-discovery` is the
               architect's, and it names the ACT of a term's author, never a stranger's echo
- resolution = keep `replication`; `independent discovery` is NOT recorded as forbidden — it is a fair
               gloss in prose, and the forbid list is for words that would take its place in a contract

### dispute: is this a term at all, or a rule? — raised 2026-08-26 — status: RESOLVED (a term)

- raised.by  = beaver 🦫
- claim      = `rule.require.domain-term-itemization` says to itemize a word that composes a declared
               dobj or dop. `replication` composes neither — it is an epistemic property of a term, so
               it may belong in that rule's text rather than in a cluster of its own
- counter    = the glossary's actual scope is already wider than that rule's letter. `false report`,
               `partial audit`, `substituted criterion`, and `volunteered diagnosis` are every one of
               them an analytic word rather than a work primitive, and each earns its cluster because
               the supervisor thinks WITH it. `replication` is used the same way — as a verdict on
               evidence — and it now has four instances and three clusters that cite it
- resolution = pave the cluster. the rule's letter is narrower than the dir it governs, and that gap is
               itself worth a note to the learner role (below)

## .the evidence

| # | party | the artifact | rung | what makes it that rung |

| 1 | camp-grove | `rule.require.both-axes-of-grove-threat` | ⭐⭐ | a different repo, and it wrote a **blocker-severity rule** — a cost paid, not a remark |

| 3 | camp-grove r14 | its own `partial audit` self-catch | ⭐⭐ | caught on its own record, with no reviewer to force it |
| 4 | `fix-machine-wide-reach-unlock` | probe-residue sweep + `git show origin/main:` | ⭐ | same model family, and two single acts rather than an adopted habit |

the count is four; the weight is uneven, and to report only the count would be its own `partial
audit` — the selection criterion (a party agreed) is orthogonal to the property that matters (were
they positioned to agree by accident?).

### the caveat, stated where it costs me

instance 4 is **mine to discount, and it is the one that flatters me most** — a clone on a tree i
supervise, of the same model family, whose two acts fell within an hour of my own rounds on the same
subjects. i cannot rule out a shared prior, and i can only partly rule out the channel: it has never
read this glossary, but the rules it obeys (`rule.require.clamp-edge-cases`,
`rule.require.trust-but-verify`) were shaped by the same pressures that produced these terms.

so it is one star, and it is recorded at one star **in the round where it happened**, rather than
banked at full weight and quietly re-graded later.

that discipline is the term's own point aimed back at itself: a term whose evidence class is
*independent agreement* is exactly the term most tempted to over-count agreement.

## .the gap this surfaced in a peer rule

`rule.require.domain-term-itemization` (bhrain/learner) says to itemize a word that composes a
**declared domain object or operation**. by that letter, none of this glossary's analytic terms
qualifies — `false report`, `partial audit`, `substituted criterion`, `volunteered diagnosis`, and now
`replication` are all supervisor reason-words, not work primitives.

the dir's practice is therefore wider than the rule's text, and has been for many rounds. that is a
**seed for the learner role**, not a defect here: either the rule's scope should widen to name the
analytic class, or the dir owes an explicit second charter. recorded rather than repaired, because it
is that role's call.
