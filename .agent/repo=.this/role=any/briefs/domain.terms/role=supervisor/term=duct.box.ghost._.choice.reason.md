# domain.term.choice.reason: duct.box.ghost

## .etymology

**`ghost`** is borrowed from the interface convention every operator already holds: *ghost text*
is the industry word for a dim, non-committed suggestion inside an input — shells, editors, and
autocompletes all ship it under that name. the word carries **"visible, and not really there"**,
which is the whole of the concept.

the rejected alternatives, and why each fails:

| word | why it is forbidden |
|---|---|
| `placeholder` | a placeholder is what a box shows when EMPTY. a ghost is a concrete completion of what was typed |
| `hint` | says the box advises. it does not — accept it and it becomes your text verbatim |
| `shadow text` | two words for a one-word state, and the peers are all one word |
| `greyed` | names the RENDER, never the sense (`howto.domain-discovery` — name from why, never how). grey is how it happens to be drawn |
| `phantom text` | 🔴 the worst of them — see the collision below |

## .why it was itemized on 2026-09-05, and not sooner

it was **never new.** `term=duct.box.covered._.choice._.md` cites it by name, by glyph, and with
its verdict, in a comparison table:

> \| `👻ghost` \| dim text \| is refused — the check catches it \|

⇒ so it has been **spoken by a peer and declared nowhere** — a citation with no cluster behind
it. the arrear surfaced only when a supervisor met the state live for the first time and had no
file to read.

⚠️ **that is the shape worth the note, not the word.** a state can be in the classifier, in the
poll's render, and in a peer term's table, and still be undeclared — because every one of those
is a USE, and none is a DECLARATION. the peers (`covered` · `quiet` · `unread` · `inflight`) all
had clusters; the citation made this one look like it did too.

## 🔴 .the OPEN dispute — `ghost` against `suggested`

### dispute: suggested — raised 2026-09-05 — status: OPEN

- raised.by  = supervisor 🦫
- claim      = `suggested` is the better word, on three grounds:
    1. it is **already the vocabulary of a rule** —
       `rule.require.distinguish-prefilled-from-suggested` names this concept `suggested`, and
       that rule is the guard the babysit protocol invokes before every submit
    2. it makes a **symmetric pair** with `prefilled` — two past-participle adjectives on one
       axis (`rule.prefer.symmetric-term-pairs`). `prefilled`/`ghost` is asymmetric: one names
       how the text got there, the other what it looks like
    3. 🔴 `ghost` is a **forbidden synonym of `phantom`**, listed in
       `term=phantom._.choice._.md` — so the glossary forbids the word in one breath and ships
       it in another
- counter    = `ghost` is the **incumbent in the live instrument**. it is what `duct.poll`
    classifies, what `git.crew.poll` renders, and what `duct.box.covered`'s table teaches. a
    rename touches the classifier, the fleet render, a peer term file, and every snapshot of
    all three — and `rule.forbid.domain-term-synonyms` explicitly permits a synonym to stay
    **until disturbed**, with no forced mass-rewrite. `ghost` is also the wider industry word,
    so it costs a newcomer no gloss
- resolution = **UNSETTLED.** the contract keeps `ghost` while this stands open, per
    `howto.domain-term-disputes`. what would settle it: a verdict on whether ground 3 is a real
    collision or two unrelated concepts that happen to share a word — see below, because that
    question has evidence the raiser did not expect

## 🔴 .the `👻` GLYPH collision — and it is sharper than the word

the two concepts do not merely share a word. **they share a glyph, in one report, four lines
apart.** measured verbatim from `git.crew.poll` on 2026-09-05:

```
├─ 👻ghost × 1 — state is not `empty`, so no stone renders for these roles
...
└─ 👻 3 phantom    — felled trees whose registry rows remain
```

| | `👻ghost` | `👻 3 phantom` |
|---|---|---|
| the subject | one **input box** | a **registry row** |
| what is wrong | naught — it is inert | a record outlived its subject |
| the cure | **leave it alone** | clear the row |
| the layer | duct | crew |

⇒ **one glyph, two concepts, opposite cures, same screen.**
`im_an.obsessive_learner.for.domain.glyphs` names this failure outright — *"one glyph for two
concepts"* — and its caution applies exactly: *"a claimed glyph spreads at once, and each
surface makes the repair dearer."*

### ⚠️ what this does NOT license

**do not swap the glyph from a single read.** the same brief demands the check before the swap:
*"check the code before you swap — a type that names its own slots has already drawn the seam."*
a glyph reaches the classifier, the poll render, the fleet summary, the peer term tables, and
every snapshot of each. that is a repair to scope deliberately, never a find-and-replace at the
tail of a session.

⇒ so the glyph question is **recorded, with its measurement, and left open** — and it is now the
half of the dispute that carries the weight. the word `ghost` is defensible on incumbency; the
shared `👻` is harder to defend, because a reader cannot disambiguate it at all without the row's
text beside it.
