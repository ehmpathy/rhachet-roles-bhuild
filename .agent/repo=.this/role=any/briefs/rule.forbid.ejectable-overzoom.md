# rule.forbid.ejectable-overzoom

> **if a detail CAN be ejected into a zoom, it MUST be. never force a reader through a zoom level
> they did not ask for.**

an **overzoom** is detail pitched one zoom level below the passage it sits in. it is **ejectable**
when a named peer artifact could hold it and a citation could reach it.

## .the test — two questions, in order

> **1. can this be CUT with no claim lost?** → cut it.
> **2. can this be EJECTED to a zoom and cited?** → eject it.

only what survives both stays. *"but it is important"* answers neither — importance decides whether
it is **cut or ejected**, never whether it stays where it is.

## 🔴 .the burden of proof inverts — POSSIBILITY, never importance

`rule.require.reflexive-condensation` asks *"is this 95%+ important to mention?"*

- that is a judgment of worth, and the author who wrote the line already made it once
- ⇒ so the bar is graded by the one party guaranteed to pass it

this asks **can it be ejected?** — and the author holds no discretion over the answer.

⇒ a true, tight, important passage is still a violation when a zoom would hold it.

## .a zoom is PULLED, and that is what makes the eject a win rather than a relocation

- inline detail is pushed — every reader pays for it, and no reader chose it
- a cited zoom is pulled — it costs one line until a reader asks, then it costs its full depth
- ⇒ so the eject does not merely relocate the words; **it moves who decides whether to spend on them**

## .what a zoom is

a **zoom** is a named peer artifact, cited by path from the passage that owes it: a `.reason` file, a
`$stage.yield.appendix.*`, a `$route/.notes/` file, an `example=$slug.md`, a specialization brief.

## .the shapes — in every row the passage is TRUE and the repair is an EJECT

| the passage | 👎 the plea | 👍 |
|---|---|---|
| a derivation under a decision | *"the reader needs to see how I got here"* | the decision, plus a path to the derivation |
| a 7-row worked table where 2 carry the claim | *"the other 5 are real cases"* | the 2, plus a path to all 7 |
| a second example of a shape already demoed | *"it illustrates a different angle"* | one demo. the rest zoom out |
| a caveat about an edge nobody will hit | *"it is true"* | true, and it is a zoom |
| a preamble that earns the claim beneath it | *"it gives context"* | the claim. the context is a zoom |
| a per-item rationale in a config or an index | *"a future editor will want to know why"* | they will. from the zoom |

## 🟡 .the two bounds, and the rule is worse than useless without them

### bound 1 — a zoom must be REACHED, or the eject is a dodge

an ungraded destination lets an author satisfy the rule and help no reader: eject 6,000 words into an
appendix no lane reviews, and the passage is short and the reader is no better off.

⇒ so the zoom's own subject is graded too, and the citation must say what makes it worth an open. a
bare `⇒ see also: $path` is a pointer, never a transfer.

### bound 2 — it does NOT swallow the canon it sits in

its grain is volume, and its repair is **eject**. every neighbour rule repairs by a rewrite, a
promotion, a deletion, or an addition:

| the neighbour | its repair |
|---|---|
| `forbid.riddles` · `forbid.decode-friction` · `forbid.subversive-prose` | a rewrite |
| `forbid.diffusion` · `forbid.emphasis-noise` | a promotion, or a cut back to one |
| `forbid.purposeless-passages` · `require.reflexive-condensation` | a deletion |
| `forbid.obfuscation` | 🔴 an **addition** — the inverse |
| `require.archaeology-in-notes` | an eject — **the one genuine overlap**, and its species |

## .when it fires

| when… | then… |
|---|---|
| a section runs past a screen | the strongest cue. what in it is one zoom level down? |
| you would write *"for the curious"* or *"the full derivation is"* | it just named its own zoom |
| a table has more rows than the claim needs | keep the rows that carry it; the table is the zoom |
| you would add a second example of a shape already demoed | one demo. the rest eject |
| you defend a passage as *"important"* | the wrong test. importance decides cut-or-eject, never stay |
| the passage is a caveat the reader must ACT on | 🟡 it stays. a reader who must act cannot be asked to pull |
| there is no zoom for it to go to | then it stays — and the absent zoom may be the real defect |

## .enforcement

blocker: an ejectable overzoom left in place · a passage ejected with no citation from the claim it
supports (that is a deletion, and `archaeology-in-notes` already says so) · a passage defended as
*"important"* where the question was whether it could be **ejected** — the wrong test was answered.
nitpick: a zoom whose citation does not say what makes it worth an open.
false positive: a passage whose removal loses a claim and that has no zoom to go to · a caveat the
reader must act on · a passage the reader must hold at THIS zoom level to act on the claim beside it.
