# 🌙 dream: bhrain's peer tally should read the reviewer's verdict, not counts in its prose

kind: reseed · owner: ehmpathy/rhachet-roles-bhrain · caught 2026-10-05

## .the cue that fired

`rhachet-roles-bhuild` route `feat-brain-per-stage`, stone 5.3.verification, i003 r011: the review
stdout said `**0 blockers**` / `**0 nitpicks**`, yet the guard tallied `2 blockers, 1 nitpick` and
graded the lane `rejected`.

## .the gap, precisely

the reviewer's summary recounted a prior round of another lane: "i001 → r006: 2 blockers (…) + 1
nitpick (…)". the tally took those counts. an enrolled (claude) reviewer that summarizes history is
read as if the history were its verdict, so the driver must dispute a phantom concern.

## .the shape of the fix

when the stdout holds more than one count per dimension, prefer the last bare `N blockers` /
`N nitpicks` line (the contract's canonical form, `contract.reviewer-output`), or treat a conflict
as a malfunction rather than pick the first match. a clamp: a fixture stdout with a history line
"2 blockers" above a verdict "0 blockers" must tally 0.

## .why it is not done in this round

the parser lives in bhrain; this repo consumes it. not clean to fix here.
