⚠️ **a grain split (~26 files) is NOT the repair.** the grains here are not independent concerns;
they are the get/gen/del triads over three different mechanisms. `rule.require.group-by-noun-not-verb`
and `rule.prefer.most-common-denominator` both point at the subject seam.

### 🔴 the two edges that cross the seam — price these first

1. **`runCrew`'s driver names its peers.** its bash driver redefines the duct and term verbs as
   **recorders**, so the crew file imports or inlines duct/term vocabulary no matter where it lives.
   that is by design (`work.harness.ts` header: *"crewwork … is clamped with FAKES"*), so the crew
   file is not a clean island.
2. **`delStaleTestDirs` reaches into term.** it reads `PREFIX_TEST_TERM` and `tempDirs.prefix`. so
   the kept `shared` file depends on `term`, or the prefix moves to `shared`.

### the rerun cost

- **145 clamps** in `crewwork.integration.test.ts` bind to `runCrew`'s recorder behavior
- `ductwork.integration.test.ts` and `termwork.integration.test.ts` drive **real tmux** on a private
  socket, so a rerun is slow and host-bound
- ⇒ budget a full `*work` clamp rerun, and settle F9 (is the `*work` corpus a ci gate?) first — a
  split whose verification cannot run in ci is a split nobody can confirm
