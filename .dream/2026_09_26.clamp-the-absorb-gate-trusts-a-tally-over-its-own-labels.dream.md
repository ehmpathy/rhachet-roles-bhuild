| field | value |
|---|---|
| kind | **reseed** — the fix lives in `ehmpathy/rhachet-roles-bhrain`, not here |

| caught | 2026-09-26 |

## .the cue that fired

i ran the two-grain absorb gate on peer lane `enroll-impl-arch-defects` (i004 r011) and it demanded
**eleven** dispositions:

```
11 concerns of enroll-impl-arch-defects stand un-absorbed:
  - blocker.1 … blocker.5, nitpick.1 … nitpick.6
```

the given holds **ten** labeled concerns — `2 [blocker]` + `8 [nitpick]`, counted off the inline
`[blocker|nitpick][urgent|better]` tokens `contract.reviewer-output` requires. its own tally line
says otherwise:

```
**5 blockers, 6 nitpicks** (severity per `rule.forbid.overzealous-blockers`'s harm test)
```

⇒ the reviewer graded its items one way and summed them another. **the gate believed the sum.**

## .the gap, precisely

`contract.reviewer-output` asks a reviewer for two artifacts that can disagree:

| the artifact | what it is |
|---|---|
| the inline `[blocker][urgent]` token, per concern | the **grade** — what a driver disposes of |
| the `N blockers` / `N nitpicks` lines | the **tally** — what the parser reads |

the contract says the two numeric lines *"tally EVERY item by its severity"*, and no clamp enforces
it. so a reviewer that mis-sums its own labels produces a given whose grade set and count set are
inconsistent — and the gate keys its ordinals to the count.

### what that costs a driver

three of the five `blocker.*` slots had **no concern behind them**. a driver must still dispose of
each, and only two honest moves exist:

| the move | why it is wrong |
|---|---|
| grade the phantom slots `urgent` | 🔴 `rule.require.grade-a-concession-by-its-harm`: *"a concession graded `urgent` with no nameable shipped harm = blocker"*. a slot with no text cannot name a harm |
| grade them `better` and say so | the least-wrong option, and what i did — but it puts a **fabricated disposition** on the record |

⇒ and the reverse case is the expensive one. **the tally can UNDERCOUNT too.** this given's did, on
the nitpick axis: 8 labeled, 6 slots. §3.2 and §4.2 fell past the last slot, so **the gate never
asked about them and never would have.** a driver who works the ordinals and no more closes the lane
with two concerns un-dispositioned and a green gate.

🔴 **that is a `failhide`.** the gate's whole promise is *"a concern you have not answered holds the
road"* (`rule.always.absorb-every-concern`), and an undercounted tally quietly revokes it for every
concern past the count.

## .the shape of the fix

two halves, of different difficulty.

### half 1 — cross-check the tally against the labels, and refuse a mismatch

`contract.reviewer-output` already mandates both artifacts. so the parser can count the inline
tokens and compare:

| labels vs tally | verdict |
|---|---|
| equal | proceed, as today |
| unequal | 🔴 `malfunction` — the given is internally inconsistent, so its verdict is unreadable |

a `malfunction` is the right disposition, and the contract already says why: *"where it finds no
numeric count it can NOT assume zero … an unreadable review is a `💥 malfunction`."* a review whose
count contradicts its own labels is unreadable in exactly that sense — and a malfunction is
**dischargeable** (re-run the lane), where a silent undercount is not.

⚠️ the bound: a reviewer may legitimately write a token the counter miscounts — `[credit, no action]`
appears in this very given and is **not** a concern. so the token grammar must be pinned before the
cross-check can be trusted, which is why this half is not a one-liner.

### half 2 — key the ordinals to the LABELS, not the count

the deeper repair. an ordinal should name a concern, so it should be minted from what *is* a
concern. then `blocker.3` cannot exist over an empty slot, and `nitpick.7` cannot be absent over a
real one.

⇒ half 1 makes the defect **loud**; half 2 makes it **impossible**. half 1 first.

## .why it is not done in this round

the SAFE/CLEAN test, both halves:

**not SAFE** — it changes the gate every route in the ecosystem passes through. a cross-check that
mis-parses one token grammar turns every healthy given into a `malfunction` and halts every live
route at once. that needs the token grammar pinned by a clamp first, with fixtures.

**not CLEAN** — the code is in `rhachet-roles-bhrain` (`asPeerGivenVerdict`,
`getLatestPeerGivensPerSlug`, and the `--about` validator), so no part of it lands in this repo's
diff. a lift of the supervisor and prioritizer roles has no business inside the review engine.

⇒ so it defers, as a **reseed**: the find and its evidence belong upstream, where the fix is.
