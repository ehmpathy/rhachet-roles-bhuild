# 🌙 dream: classify every radio record field as fate or request, and pick the fault line once

kind: refactor · owner: rhachet-roles-bhuild (dispatcher) · caught 2026-11-02

## .the cue that fired

round 4 of the 5.1 peer review on `beav/fix-radio-refusal-parks-its-own-task` (r11,
enroll-impl-arch-defects, nitpicks 4 and 5). the new `reasonHeadline` field had to be listed by hand
in five places and added to ~30 test fixtures.

## .the gap, precisely

1. a fate field is listed in five places: the zod schema (`daoRadioTaskRecord/index.ts`),
   `RadioTaskRecord.updatable`, `FIELDS_OF_FATE` (`isRadioTaskRecordRepushedSince.ts`),
   `asRadioTaskRecordFromPush.ts`, and the dao omit-null rule. a field absent from `FIELDS_OF_FATE`
   makes every settle that changes it read as a re-push, so `setRadioTaskRecordSettled` returns
   `current` and the fate is never written — silent. (it happened once in this route: 7 red
   integration tests, caught only because the suite was run)
2. 17 hand-rolled `genRecord` fixtures; one new field touched ~30 test files
3. the drain prints its fault line from `asRadioErrorDetail(send.fault.message)`, the record keeps
   its words from `send.faultText` (command cut, stderr appended). two pipelines; with a multi-line
   `cmd` echo they could pick different lines. a risk, not an observed fault
4. the fault and auth fates of `setRadioTaskRecordUpstream` stay inline (round 6, r11 nitpick 2),
   while the gate and behind-halt fates are their own settlers. each inline path repeats settle →
   re-push check → fate, and the words policy can only be tested by a whole drain

## .the shape of the fix

- one const map `RADIO_TASK_RECORD_FIELD_KIND: Record<keyof RadioTaskRecord, 'fate' | 'request' |
  'identity'>`, typed so a new key fails compile; derive `FIELDS_OF_FATE` and the from-push fate
  copy from it
- one shared `genRadioTaskRecordFixture({ ...overrides })` under `src/.test/`; migrate the 17
- pick the fault line once from `faultText` in `setRadioTaskRecordUpstream`, carry it on the fate,
  and print that in the drain; or one parity clamp over an exec error whose `cmd` holds a newline
- extract `setRadioTaskRecordHeldOnFault({ record, dir, send })` and `setRadioTaskRecordHeldOnAuth`,
  peers of `setRadioTaskRecordHeldAtGate`; the orchestrator reads as a list of paths, and the words
  policy gets a unit test of its own. it pairs with item 3: the fault settler is where the one
  line pick would live

## .why it is not done in this round

not CLEAN: it touches ~30 test files and the fate type, far past the diff this fix opened. SAFE but
large — a rename-scale change belongs in its own pr.
