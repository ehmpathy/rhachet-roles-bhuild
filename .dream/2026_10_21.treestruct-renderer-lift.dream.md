# 🌙 dream: lift one recursive treestruct renderer into infra, and migrate its four peers onto it

kind: refactor · owner: ehmpathy/rhachet-roles-bhuild · caught 2026-10-21

## .the cue that fired

the `has-consistent-mechanisms` self review of route `v2026_10_21.feat-phone-text` (stone 5.1) found
that the phone added a fifth hand-rolled `├─` / `└─` renderer beside four extant ones.

## .the gap, precisely

five renderers draw the same branch glyphs, each its own way:

| file | depth | shape |
|---|---|---|
| `src/domain.operations/phone/render/asPhoneTreeLines.ts` | any (recursive) | `{ text, children? }[]` |
| `src/domain.operations/radio/record/asRadioTreeLines.ts` | 2, fixed | `{ line, children: string[] }[]`; a child may span lines |
| `src/domain.operations/radio/cli/asTaskListLine.ts` | 1 | one line per call |
| `src/domain.operations/behavior/init/computeOutputTree.ts` | its own | init output |
| `src/domain.operations/behavior/render/computeFeedbackTakeGetOutput.ts` | 3, inline | three copies of the corner pick |
| `src/infra/cli/getCliArgs.ts` (`genCliArgsErrorStdout`) | 1, inline | arg errors |

the phone could not reuse `asRadioTreeLines`: phone refusals nest three levels
(`you may reach` → its rows), and the radio renderer stops at two.

## .the shape of the fix

- move `asPhoneTreeLines` (it is the general one: recursive, any depth) to
  `src/infra/render/asTreestructLines.ts`, renamed, with its `{ text, children? }` node
- add the one feature radio needs that it lacks: a node `text` that spans lines continues on the
  node's own rail (radio's `.note`)
- migrate radio, behavior init, feedback take, and cli arg errors onto it one by one; each migration
  must leave its snapshots byte-identical — a diff in a snapshot is a regression, not a re-snap

## .why it is not done in this round

safe, but not clean: the migration touches four extant surfaces and their snapshots, none of which
this delivery opened. smuggled into the phone diff it would bury a refactor under a feature (the
CLEAN question of `rule.always.fix-forward-under-scouts-honor`). fulcrum F17 records the judgment.
