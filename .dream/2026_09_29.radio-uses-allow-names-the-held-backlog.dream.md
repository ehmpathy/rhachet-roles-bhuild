kind: fix · owner: bhuild/dispatcher · caught 2026-09-29

## .the cue that fired

peer review i005 r010 (5.1.execution of `v2026_09_28.fix-radio-recorder`) re-raised vision
research item A3: a human who lifts the gate is not told what waits behind it.

## .the gap, precisely

`rhx radio.uses allow` (any level) prints the new permission state and stops. held records in
`.behavior/**/.radio/` stay invisible until the next stop reminder or the next push. check: hold a
push behind a shut gate, run `rhx radio.uses allow`, read its output — no count, no "next push
delivers them".

## .the shape of the fix

- after a successful `allow`, read the held records with lift (`getAllRadioTaskRecordsHeldWithLift`)
- when any are now free, append one line: `🦫 N tasks held for the radio · next push delivers them`
- reuse `asRadioHeldOutput` stop-line mode; add one snapshot per level (local, org, global)

## .why it is not done in this round

safe, but not clean: it opens the `radio.uses` skill family (bash + ts) and its ~73 snapshots,
which the recorder tree does not otherwise touch. the wisher chose the stop reminder as the
channel (F9), so the gap is narrow. fulcrum: F21.
