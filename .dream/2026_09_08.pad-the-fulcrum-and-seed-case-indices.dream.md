# 🌙 dream: pad the fulcrum and seed `case=` indices to width 3

**kind:** reseed — the convention is bhrain's, so the repair is a dispatch, never a bhuild edit
**owner:** `ehmpathy/rhachet-roles-bhrain` — `role=driver` (fulcrums) · `role=learner` (seeds)
**caught:** 2026-09-08

## .the cue that fired

bhuild padded its own experience cases to `case=001..012` and wrote
`rule.require.zero-padded-case-index` to hold the convention. the rule's `.where` section then had
to carve out two peer inventories that carry the **identical shape and the identical defect** —
because bhrain declares them, not bhuild.

## .the gap, precisely

`asStoneGuardCounter.ts` already states the reason bhrain-side:

> *"a plain lexical sort of those filenames scrambles double-digit counters (i10 < i2, r10 < r2).
> a fixed pad width makes lexical order equal numeric order by construction"*

it is applied to the guard counters (`i001`, `r001`) and **not** to the two `case=` inventories the
driver and learner rules spell:

| rule | the shape it spells | at n=10 |
|---|---|---|
| `rule.always.itemize-the-fulcrums-you-best-guess` | `inventory.of=fulcrums.case=F$n-$slug.md` | 🔴 `F10` sorts between `F1` and `F2` |
| `rule.always.archive-the-wishers-words-verbatim` | `inventory.of=seeds.case=S$n-$slug.md` | 🔴 `S10` sorts between `S1` and `S2` |

⇒ measured on this route: **14 fulcrums and 39+ seeds**, so both are already past the threshold.
`case=F11-…` currently sorts directly after `case=F1-…`, ahead of `case=F2-…`.

🟡 **the guard counters are computed and the case indices are hand-named**, which is why one half
of the same repo's convention is safe and the other half is not. the pad is mechanized exactly
where a human never types it.

## .the shape of the fix

- amend both rules to spell `case=F$nnn-$slug` and `case=S$nnn-$slug`, width 3
- cite `asStoneGuardCounter`'s `COUNTER_WIDTH` as the source rather than restate the number
  - a second copy of the width drifts, and neither reader holds both
- 🔴 **do not sweep extant routes.** a rename repoints every `.taken`, every yield citation, and
  every review artifact — and the review artifacts are reviewer-authored, so an edit there
  falsifies the trail. new routes only, per `rule.forbid.domain-term-synonyms`'s own
  no-mass-rewrite clause

## .why it is not done in this round

**safe: no. clean: no.**

- the convention is **declared in another repo**, so a unilateral bhuild rename drifts from the
  rule that spells it — a tree adopts only what is scoped to itself
- the rework is **dirty**: this route holds 14 fulcrum files and 39+ seed files, each cited from
  the yield, the appendices, and the peer review trail

⇒ so it is a reseed rather than a fix, and bhuild's own rule carves the two out by name so no
reader mistakes the carve-out for an oversight.
