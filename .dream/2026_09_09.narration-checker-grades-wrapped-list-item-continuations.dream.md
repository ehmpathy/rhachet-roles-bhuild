# 🌙 dream: `review.for.narration.paragraphs.js` grades a wrapped list item's continuation as prose

- **kind** — defect
- **owner** — `ehmpathy/rhachet-roles-bhrain`, `role=telepath`
- **caught** — 2026-09-09

## .the cue that fired

a run over `1.vision.yield.md` reported 14 paragraphs at 3 claims. one hit was at line 560:

```
  3 claims /  46w  .../1.vision.yield.md:560
     ## what is awkward
```

line **559** opens `3. "does this earn its place" is a tidy frame…` — a numbered list item. lines
560-562 are its wrapped continuation, indented three spaces.

## .the gap, precisely

`STRUCTURED` (line 105) exempts a list item by its **opener**:

```js
const STRUCTURED = /^\s*(?:#{1,6}\s|>|\||[-*+]\s|\d+\.\s|```|:?-{3,}:?\s*\|)/;
```

⇒ so the opener line is exempt and **terminates the paragraph**. the continuation lines then start a
fresh paragraph with no opener, and the checker grades them as prose.

**the longer the list item, the more of it is graded** — and a list item is precisely the shape
`require.bulletize` asks for, so the tool penalizes its own remedy.

⇒ measured on this file: of 14 reported hits, at least 3 are list-item continuations
(`:560`, plus the pairs at `:474` / `:489` that sit under numbered items).

## .the shape of the fix

a continuation line inherits its opener's exemption. one state variable, in the same walk that
already tracks the `PRESERVED-ORIGINAL BLOCK`:

- when a line matches `STRUCTURED` **and** its opener is a list bullet (`[-*+]\s` or `\d+\.\s`),
  record that opener's indent
- while later lines are non-blank and indented **past** that indent, treat them as part of the same
  structured element rather than as a new paragraph
- a blank line, or a return to the opener's indent or shallower, closes the item

🟡 **do not simply exempt every indented line** — a fenced block's body and a blockquote already have
their own guards, and an over-wide exemption would blind the checker to a genuinely indented
paragraph inside a nested context.

## .why it is not done in this round

**SAFE — no. CLEAN — no.**

- the file is `ehmpathy/rhachet-roles-bhrain`, a peer repo. a tree adopts only what is scoped to
  itself (`rule.always.scope-onetime-lessons-to-the-behavior`)
- the checker's own header carries three measured false-positive classes with their reasons; a fourth
  guard must be argued against those, with a re-run over the 141-file corpus that header cites — a
  measurement pass rather than a ride-along

⇒ so it is re-seeded rather than repaired here. its consumer half rides the extant dispatch at
`$route/.dispatch/v2026_09_08.into=bhrain.unenroll-the-entooled-rules.md`.
