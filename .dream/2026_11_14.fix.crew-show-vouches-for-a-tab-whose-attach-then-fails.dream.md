# 🌙 dream: crew.show vouches for a remote tab whose attach then fails

- kind: fix
- owner: rhachet-roles-bhuild, supervisor (`work/crewwork.view.sh` `__crew_tab_defect`)
- caught 2026-11-14

## .the cue that fired
5.3 of `v2026_11_13.feat-supervisor-reach-over-tailnet`: `git.crew.show` on a hetzner grove crew
printed `dam fine! in view — every tab vouched for`. the grove's `tmux list-clients` was empty, and
`term.audit` a minute later read the same tab `🔴 … runs: sleep 5` — its attach had failed (no kitty
terminfo on the box; cured in termwork this round) and the tab sat in its retry loop.

## .the gap, precisely
the post-audit grades a tab by its kitty foreground. at launch that foreground IS the
`ssh -t … tmux attach` command, which names the duct, so the tab passes in the instant before the
attach fails. a verdict over a remote tab that never asked the grove whether a client attached is
a term=false-report.

## .the shape of the fix
- for a remote tab, the post-audit asks the grove: `tmux list-clients -t <session>` holds a client
- or it waits out one attach attempt (the loop's retry is 5s) before it grades
- a clamp: a stub attach that exits non-zero → crew.show refuses `vouched`

## .why it is not done in this round
the wish is reach over the tailnet; when the vouch reads predates it, and a grove round trip in the
view gate changes the gate's cost for every crew.
