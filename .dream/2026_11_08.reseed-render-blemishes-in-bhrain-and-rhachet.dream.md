## .the gap, precisely

- **bhrain, `route.stone.set` judge render**: a judge's stdout and stderr are drawn under
  `│  │  `, and a blank line of the judge's own output gets that prefix too, so it ends in two
  spaces, while the tree's own blank spacer lines (`│  │`) are bare
- **rhachet, the skill runner**: a skill that exits non-zero prints `🪨 run solid skill …` once at
  start, then the same banner again with `└─ ✋ blocked by constraints`, with naught between

## .the shape of the fix

- bhrain: trim end spaces from each indented output line (an empty content line gets the bare
  prefix)
- rhachet: on a non-zero exit, append `└─ ✋ blocked by constraints` under the first banner, or
  reprint it only when output came between

## .why it is not done in this round

both bytes are made in another repo; a mask here would make this repo's snapshots lie about what a
human sees (F23).
