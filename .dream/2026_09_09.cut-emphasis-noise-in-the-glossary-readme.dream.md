## .the shape of the fix

🔴 **`## .the open gaps` is over the bar because it is several sections that wear one header.** it
carries a definition, a three-sweep comparison table, a fourth question, and three blockquoted sweep
records — and each blockquote is already a sub-artifact with a needle of its own.

- split it at the blockquotes, one `###` per sweep
  - the per-section count then falls to 1 with **no mark cut at all**
  - ⇒ so the largest of the four is a structural repair, not an emphasis repair
- the three two-and-four-mark sections take a judgment cut
  - a header line and its first claim rarely both need a mark

## .why it is not done in this round

the split renames sections of a booted brief that other briefs cite by section name. that is
**dirty** under `rule.always.fix-forward-under-scouts-honor` — safe, and not clean. the three marks
this round introduced were cut on contact; the rest is deferred here.

🟡 **no fulcrum is raised, and the call is worth a line.** the dirt sits in another artifact's
structure rather than in a fork of this route's design, so a council row would put a question about
glyph density beside the scope-flag class and the size bar. the dream is the record.
