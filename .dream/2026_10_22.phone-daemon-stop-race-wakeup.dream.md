# 🌙 dream: a send that races `phone.daemon del` must not be told the daemon delivers it

kind: fix · owner: bhuild dispatcher · caught 2026-10-22

## .the cue that fired

r007 (arch-hazards-behavior) i005 nitpick.1, on route `v2026_10_21.feat-phone-text` stone 5.1.

## .the gap, precisely

- `genPhoneDaemon.ts:31` returns FOUND on any LIVE lock, even one whose pid just got SIGTERM
- `runPhoneDaemon.ts:77` returns `stopped` straight after it releases the lock; it does not re-read
  the mailboxes, unlike the idle path
- so a `phone.text set` whose record lands after the daemon's last tick, and whose
  `genPhoneDaemon` reads the lock before the release, prints `daemon: LIVE` while no daemon will say
  it. the text stays QUEUED on disk until the next `phone.text set` or `phone.daemon set`

## .the shape of the fix

mark the lock STOPPING when `delPhoneDaemon` signals (a lock state, written before SIGTERM).
`genPhoneDaemon` reads STOPPING as not-FOUND and spawns; the spawned daemon's `gen` loses to the
held lock, waits a bounded span for its release, then claims. clamp: an integration test that holds
a daemon mid-stop, sends, and asserts the text is said, red before the fix.

## .why it is not done in this round

dirty: it adds a lock state and a wait to the daemon lifecycle, with a new race test, and it lands
as the director's depth-0 l3 pass is due. the harm is a delay, bounded by the next send; no text is
lost. fulcrum F21 records the call.
