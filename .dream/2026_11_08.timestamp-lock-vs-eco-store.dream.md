2. here: the prioritizer store sheds wall-clock fields (order by sequence, `rule.forbid.timestamps…`
   `.how`), then this repo adopts the practice and its lock verbatim

either way, re-apply the husky practice (`declapract apply --practice husky`; see the dream
`2026_11_08.pave-declapract-apply.dream.md` for the skill that should front it) and keep
`check.timestamps.integration` and `ecowork.commit` green.

option 2 is a store redesign. the wish here is the vision root purpose.
