# 🌙 dream: a deliver held behind a nudged claim sends before the status-only re-run's claim

kind: fix · owner: dispatcher (radio) · caught 2026-11-18

## .the cue that fired

5.3 verification, peer lane enroll-verif-snapshot-blemishes (i006 nitpick.2) asked for [case16][t2] of

## .the gap, precisely

- a `CLAIMED` push with text into a public repo is nudged and DROPPED; a `DELIVERED` push waits behind it on the
  same issue. the nudge says "1 update waits behind it on #5; its deliver needs this claim first" and offers
  "to send status alone: re-run it without --description"
- the re-run is a new record. the push delivers the backlog first, oldest first, so the held deliver sends before
  the new claim, meets an unclaimed issue, and is refused: `deliver #5 — cannot deliver unclaimed task`, closed,
  never retried
- the refusal is shown (catalog #33 holds: no deliver strands unseen), but the human who followed the printed
  hint must push the deliver again

## .the shape of the fix

the status-only re-run's claim lands ahead of a deliver that waits on the same issue: an order rule, a claim for
issue N sorts before a held deliver for N, in the backlog the push delivers first.

the cheap half landed in the round that found it: the nudge now names the cost and the move, under the warn line —
"a re-run sends that deliver first, and it is refused; push the deliver again once the claim lands".

## .why it is not done in this round

the backlog order is the extant radio contract, shared with `queue del` of a claim; a change there ripples into
every drain (not CLEAN).
