- kind: fix
- owner: rhachet-roles-bhuild, supervisor role (`work/crewwork.view.sh`)

## .the cue that fired

a live walk of `git.crew.move` on the hetzner grove: `git.crew.list` printed
`whodis-client_beav_feat-declapract-upgrade`; the move, handed that string, answered about
`whodis-client.beav.feat-declapract-upgrade`.

## .the gap, precisely

one tree, two spellings on one screen. `git.crew.list` prints the tmux session form (`.` swapped
for `_`); every crew verb takes both forms and speaks the slug form back. naught breaks, but a
reader must map one name to the other by eye, and `--help` says "the tree slug, as git.crew.list
prints it" while the list prints no slug.

## .the shape of the fix

- `git.crew.list` prints the slug form (`<repo>.<who>.<branch>`), the one every verb echoes
- its snapshot and the `crewwork` view clamps resnap for the new form
- a clamp: the name `crew.list` prints, fed to `git.crew.move --tree`, echoes back unchanged

## .why it is not done in this round

the list is shared by every crew verb and its snapshots across the `crewwork` suites. the change
ripples past this route's diff (fails CLEAN under scouts-honor). no shipped harm: the move accepts
both forms.
