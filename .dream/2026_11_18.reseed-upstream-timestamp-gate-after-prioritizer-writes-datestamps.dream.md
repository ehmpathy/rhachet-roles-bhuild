# 🌙 dream: the prioritizer store writes datestamps, then the upstream timestamp gate reseeds

- kind: reseed + refactor
- owner: bhuild prioritizer (the store writes); then a declapract husky reapply
- caught 2026-11-18, on route `v2026_11_13.feat-supervisor-reach-over-tailnet`, in #663

## .the cue that fired
the rhachet bump reseeded `.husky/*` from declapract-typescript-ehmpathy 0.50.0. the upstream gate
forbids every clock time (`HH:MM` included) and ships a lock (`check.timestamps.lock.sh`) that
refuses any repo-local gate. 8 integration tests went red, and the director's call (a) pulled the
husky part out of #663.

## .the gap, precisely
- the prioritizer's `.eco/*` store keeps a clock-time `createdAt` / `updatedAt` on each row. this
  repo's gate exempts `.eco/*` for it; the upstream gate has no exemption, by design
- the clamps pin the repo-local gate: `.husky/check.timestamps.integration.test.ts` (the words a
  human reads, the `why:` line, the class on stderr), `src/.test/utils/getTimestampFound.integration.test.ts`
  (the test copy of each pattern equals the gate), and `ecowork.commit.integration.test.ts`
  [case46] (the committed store passes pre-commit)
- so the upstream gate cannot land until the store holds no clock time

## .the shape of the fix
1. the prioritizer writes `createdAt` / `updatedAt` as datestamps (`asIsoDateStamp`), and order
   comes from a sequence, never a clock (`rule.forbid.timestamps-in-tracked-files`)
2. a one-time migration cuts extant `.eco` rows to datestamps
3. then `npx declapract apply --practice husky`: the upstream gate, its lock, `check.secrets.sh`
4. the three suites move to the upstream gate's words and patterns; [case46] holds unchanged,
   since the store now passes the strict gate on its own
5. the repo brief `rule.forbid.timestamps-in-tracked-files` drops the `.eco/*` exemption row

## .why it is not done in this round
it is a change to how the prioritizer store writes data, plus a migration. #663 is the tailnet
reach move; the husky part left it by call (a), so the rest of the bump could ship green.
