# 🌙 dream: git.commit.push refuses a lease push that would drop a foreign commit

kind: reseed · owner: ehmpathy/rhachet-roles-ehmpathy (mechanic `git.commit.push`) · caught 2026-11-07

## .the cue that fired

r010 at i007 of `v2026_10_24.feat-init-behavior-preship-reflect`: 6.preship's "push only" arm
and its "never a force push" rule both sit on a supplier that pushes with
`git push origin HEAD --force-with-lease`.

## .the gap, precisely

- `--force-with-lease` with no explicit expected sha leases against the local
  `origin/<branch>` ref
- once that ref is current (after a fetch), a push over another party's commits passes the lease
  and drops them
- no non-fast-forward refusal ever reaches the caller, so a stone that tells its driver to hold
  on that refusal never sees one
- no hermetic test of the push contract exists (a temp repo with a bare remote)

## .the shape of the fix

- in `git.commit.push`, read the remote head first, then refuse (exit 2) when it is not an
  ancestor of HEAD
- or pass `--force-with-lease=<branch>:<remote sha>`, plus a `merge-base --is-ancestor` check
- clamp the fix with an integration test against a bare remote: a foreign commit on the remote
  must be refused, never overwritten

## .why it is not done in this round

the push belongs to the supplier repo. a guard copied into each caller's template would be the
workaround that `rule.forbid.workarounds` forbids.
