## .the cue that fired

`roleArtifacts.integration.test.ts` is ~1,300 lines that mix a role-generic budget harness with

## .the gap, precisely

- role-specific checks in a generic file: `CUE_CLAIMS_BEHAVER`, the behaver payload snapshot, and
  `[case7]` (supervisor catalog)
- regex yaml reads: the budget, the brief entries, the said list — each needs its own
  completeness and anti-vacuity guards
- the supervisor said set is stated three ways: `say:`, ten `not:` extglobs, and the catalog

## .the shape of the fix

- `src/domain.roles/bootBudget.integration.test.ts` keeps the shared harness
  (`getOneBootResult`, `genRoleSeatCopy`, `asBootVerdict`, `asBootBudget`) and the per-role budget
  cases; `behaver/` and `supervisor/` each hold their own role checks
- parse boot.yml with the `yaml` package (pin it as a devDependency); drop the regex readers and
  the guards that exist only to check them
- generate `briefs/catalog.of=supervisor.briefs.md` from disk (a build step or a skill); the test
  asserts regeneration yields the committed file, which retires `asCatalogNames` and `[case6][t6]`
- split lets `.test.yml`'s slow-test pattern shard the boot-heavy cases apart from the cheap ones
