# 🌙 dream: author the four briefs the lifted supervisor/prioritizer corpus cites and never had

- kind: **fill** — a gap the corpus declares about itself
- owner: **supervisor** (3) + **prioritizer** (1)
- caught: **2026-09-24**

## .the cue that fired

`briefCitations.integration.test.ts` — the clamp authored for `case=5` of this route's vision. it
walks every backticked `*.md` token inside a `.see also` section and resolves it against the
basenames the package ships. it went red with **7 unresolved citations**.

two were a cross-repo pointer with no repo named (repaired in place). **the other five named four
briefs that do not exist** — and the reflex was to read them as port casualties, since a lift is
exactly the act that drops a file.

they are not. a glob of the source clone returns **0** for each of the four:

```sh
rhx globsafe --pattern '.temp/source-lift-src/.agent/repo=.this/role=*/briefs/**/*pause-babysit*'
#   files: 0
rhx globsafe --pattern '.temp/source-lift-src/.agent/repo=.this/role=*/briefs/**/term={sponsored,prioritized,glyph}*'
#   files: 0
```

⚠️ and the control matters, because a glob that silently matches naught reads identical to a glob
that correctly matches naught. `term={steer,sponsored}*` returns **2** — both `steer`, no
`sponsored` — so the brace form works in the filename slot and the zeros are real.

⇒ **the source corpus carried these dead pointers too.** the lift inherited them; it did not
create them.

🔴 **the first one is the expensive one, and its citations are not decoration.** they describe its
contents from the outside, in a detail a reader can check:

- *"one of **six** actions that reset the streak"* — a named cardinality
- `howto.review-permission-requests.md:237` cites **§1** of it by number, for the claim
  *"repair or dispute, never quietly act on the better version"*
- `term=nudge._.choice.reason.md:22` puts it in a table of rules that govern a nudge, with the
  entry *"counts one as an action that resets the streak"*

⇒ five briefs agree on what a sixth says, and the sixth was never written. **that is not an absent
file; it is a consensus with no source.** a reader who follows any of the five meets a dead end and
has no way to learn which of the five descriptions is authoritative.

the other three are smaller and share one shape: **a brief that is the de-facto definition of a
term whose cluster is owed.** `define.prioritized-vs-sponsored.md` carries the whole sense of both
its words, so the glossary that should hold them is empty while a single non-glossary brief does
the work — the exact bind a term cluster exists to break
(`rule.require.persist-domain-term-evidence`).

## .the shape of the fix

1. **`rule.require.pause-babysit-cron-after-idle-streak`** — reconstruct from its five citers, which
   between them pin: the six actions that reset the streak, a §1 whose claim is quoted verbatim, and
   the relationship to `nudge` and `steer`. ⚠️ the five are **evidence, not a spec** — they are
   descriptions of a file nobody has read, so where two disagree the reconstruction owes a call, and
   the call owes a note. if the babysit cron no longer exists in the lifted skill set, the honest
   fix is to **retire the five citations** rather than to author the rule.
2. **`term=glyph._.choice._.md`** — the cheapest, and this route already did its discovery: the
   ⛺/🔦 cutover and the package-wide two-glyph ban (`glyphsRetired.integration.test.ts`) are a
   worked example of one-concept-one-symbol with a measured failure mode. write the cluster from
   that.
3. **`term=sponsored`** + **`term=prioritized`** — extract from `define.prioritized-vs-sponsored.md`,
   which holds both definitions, both enforcement lines, and the orthogonality argument against
   `define.cost-gain-matrix`. the brief then cites the clusters rather than substitutes for them.

## .why it is not done in this round

**SAFE: no.** the first one is a rule. to author a rule is to declare a blocker severity on conduct
this lift never studied — a babysit cron, an idle streak, and six actions whose boundaries live in
a skill set this drive ported and never ran. an invented sixth action is worse than an absent file,
because it reads as authority.

**CLEAN: no.** the three term clusters ripple into the glossary the `F2` call just re-homed, and
each one is a `domain.terms/` entry that owes discovery evidence per
`rule.require.persist-domain-term-evidence` — a `.choice._` plus a `.choice.reason`, six files for
three terms, none of them in this lift's diff.

⇒ so the five citation sites are marked **`🔴 owed, never authored`** in place, which converts a
dead end into a named gap a reader can act on, and the work is caught here.

🟡 **the in-place mark is the deliberate half, not a consolation.** `briefCitations` now passes over
these lines because they no longer carry a token that reads as resolvable — so **the clamp will not
re-raise them**, and this dream is the only record that survives. the alternative (leave them red)
mutes a clamp that finds real defects, which is the worse of the two.
