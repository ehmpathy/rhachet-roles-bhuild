(`rhachet/dist/contract/cli/invokeEnroll.js:274`). a route driven by a crew clone a director

or the guard runner stands up enroll lanes as peers of the driver's enroller rather than children
of the driver, or the halt for a depth refusal names the exact re-arrive command for the enroller.

review-scope change by the party under review. the template fix belongs to its own pr.
