- **kind** — `fix`
- **owner** — `ehmpathy/rhachet-roles-bhuild`, `role=prioritizer`
- **caught** — 2026-09-28, at `5.3.verification` of

---

## .the cue that fired

a frictionless-path walk of the two lifted roles' primary surfaces, side by side:

```sh
rhx eco.priority  --help | wc -l   # 545
rhx git.crew.list --help | wc -l   #  23
```

**545 lines**, and `usage:` does not appear until **line 41**. a human who types `--help` to
recall a flag name reads 40 lines of rationale before the first line that answers them, then 504
more below it.

---

## .the gap, precisely

the content is **correct and load-central** — it is not filler. what is wrong is that ONE
surface serves two readers with opposite needs:

| reader | wants | gets today |
|---|---|---|
| first-timer | the vocabulary: quant vs opine, the fibonacci ladder, why the csv is truth | ✅ served, and served well |
| repeat user (**the common case** after day one) | *"which flag is it — `--gain-cash` or `--cash-gain`?"* | 🔴 a 545-line scroll |

⇒ `rule.prefer.defaults-match-common-case` (ergonomist): the **bare** invocation should do the
common-case work. after day one the common case is recall, not a first lesson.

⇒ `def.ergonomic`, *output is scannable*: *"a human parses it at a glance."* 546 lines is not a
glance, and the cognitive-load laws behind it (miller 7±2, hick's law) say the cost grows with
the count of items a reader must skip.

⚠️ the peer surface proves the bar is reachable: `git.crew.list --help` carries `.what`, `.why`,
`usage`, and `guarantee` in **23 lines**, and reads in one pass.

---

## .the shape of the fix

the conventional split, two rungs:

```sh
rhx eco.priority --help          # ~40 lines: .what (1 line), usage, flags, one example
rhx eco.priority --help --full   # today's 546, whole and unchanged
```

the short head keeps a **pointer**, so the depth stays discoverable
(`rule.require.discoverability`):

```
  ⇒ the quant/opine split, the fibonacci ladder, and the csv-is-truth
    invariant: rhx eco.priority --help --full
```

what must NOT happen: a cut of the long body. every `.why` block in it is an argument a brief
cites, and several exist precisely to stop a clone from an invented opine
(`rule.forbid.fabricated-opines`). **this is a split, never a trim.**

---

## .why it is not done in this round

the SAFE/CLEAN test (`rule.always.fix-forward-under-scouts-honor`):

- **SAFE?** yes — help text, no behavior beyond it
- **CLEAN?** 🔴 **no.** the help output is **snapshotted** in
  `blackbox/role=prioritizer/__snapshots__/skill.eco.priority.acceptance.test.ts.snap`, a new
  flag is a **published contract change** on a package this PR is about to ship, and the long
  body is prose the wisher deliberately authored at that length

⇒ unclean, so it is deferred with a record — and the judgment to defer is itemized as a fulcrum
(`F25`), because a ripple estimate is a best guess and a best guess is what a fulcrum list
exists to surface.
