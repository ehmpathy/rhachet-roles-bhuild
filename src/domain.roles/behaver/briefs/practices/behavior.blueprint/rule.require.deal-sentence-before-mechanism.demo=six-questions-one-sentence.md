# rule.require.deal-sentence-before-mechanism.demo=six-questions-one-sentence

a demo of `rule.require.deal-sentence-before-mechanism`.

## .what happened

a prepaid-credit design was modeled from its mechanisms outward: the customer's prepaid balance was
debited by the provider's raw cost. six design questions went to the human who owned the deal.

each answer followed from one sentence never written: *the customer prepays to buy units at a
quoted price; the seller buys the inputs and bears the gap between a unit's cost and its quote.*

four of the six were already answered by the extant invoice, the card-statement precedent, or
rules the house already had. the other two fell out of the sentence once it was written.

## .which clause each miss broke

| miss | clause |
|---|---|
| a money flow designed with no deal sentence | a design of a money flow with no deal sentence |
| a balance debited by raw cost, a mechanism no clause served | a mechanism that serves no clause of the sentence |
| six questions to the human, each answered by the sentence | a question to a human that the sentence answers |
