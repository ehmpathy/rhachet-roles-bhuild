# rule.always.fix-the-verb-that-left-you-the-gap

## .what

> **when a crew verb leaves the job half-done and you finish the rest by hand, the leftover is
> a defect in the VERB. fix the verb, in the same tick — before you report the sweep done.**

the trigger is the FIRST hand-finish, never the second. a gap seen once is a gap.

```
crew verb runs  →  you still type a keystroke to complete it
                →  🔴 that keystroke IS the defect report. close it in the verb, now
```

⇒ the supervisor-domain specialization of `rule.always.entool-the-skills-you-touch`
(**foreign** — `bhrain/learner`): "you call a skill, then run a command to finish what it
started — the follow-up belongs inside the skill." a supervisor mid-sweep often reads that as
a rule about commands and misses that it is a rule about their fleet.

## .why — the measured miss

a fleet-wide boot across many trees left every seat live and INVISIBLE (`crew.boot` opens no
tab by design — work and view are separate axes), and the supervisor opened tabs by hand one
at a time rather than repair the verb, twice, after the gap was named both times. the report
("N seated") was true about the duct and false about the capability it implied
(`term=false-report`) — a seat a human cannot open is not a seat.

## .the cue table

| when… | then… |
|---|---|
| a crew verb runs and you still type to finish the job | 🔴 the whole rule, in one row — fix the verb |
| about to ask permission for the obvious completion step | that ask is a hand-finish with a question mark on it |
| a sweep verb takes a hardcoded role/tree list | a list the fleet can outgrow ages into a silent omission |
| a report true of the substrate, false of the capability | `term=false-report` — name the half not delivered |
| a health verb covers one defect on one axis | its name claims the crew; its scope claims one symptom — narrow the name or widen the scope |
| the human names the gap before you do | 🔴 the fix was owed a tick earlier, trust cost already paid |

## .the test

after the verb returns, ask:

> is there a keystroke left between this and the outcome the human asked for?

no → the verb is whole. yes → 🔴 that keystroke is the spec for the fix. do not type it
twice — type it once if you must unblock the human, then close it in the verb the same tick.

## 🔴 .a verb that reports success on half a job is the worst shape

a verb that fails is fixed the day it is written — the failure is the spec. a verb that
succeeds on the axis it measures and leaves the other axis dark reports success forever, and
is repaired only when a human notices what they cannot see. grade a crew verb on the outcome,
never on its own axis.

## .what "fix the verb" means here

this repo originates the crew/duct/grove skills (`rule.always.pave-on-second-repeat` — the
originator test), so the fix is direct and in place, verified against the live fleet:

| the gap | the fix |
|---|---|
| a verb completes one axis and leaves the other dark | widen the verb, or make its peer verb detectable by the sweep |
| a sweep verb hardcodes its subject set | derive it — from the ledger, from a declared constant |
| a health verb sees one defect | add the defect you just found by hand, with its clamp |
| the substitution table names no verb for what you did | write the verb, add its row the same round (`rule.always.entool-the-layer-you-drop-below`) |

a dream is not the fix here. a caught dream is right for genuinely large work; it is the
wrong artifact for a gap you just walked around by hand — the walk-around proves you held the
whole context and chose to spend it on one instance.

## .enforcement

- 🔴 a crew verb hand-finished once with no repair attempted the same tick = **blocker**
- a hand-finish repeated after the gap was named = **blocker**
- a sweep reported done where a whole axis went undelivered = **blocker**
- a sweep verb with a hardcoded subject set, left hardcoded once the omission is seen = **blocker**
- a gap too large to close in the tick, caught as a dream AND named in the report = ✅ correct
