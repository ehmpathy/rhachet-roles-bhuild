# rule.forbid.sev-from-cash-alone

## .what

a `sev` graded on the **cash row alone** is forbidden.

> **"it makes no money"** and **"it yields naught"** are two different claims, and only
> the second bears on `sev`.

`define.cost-gain-matrix` holds three rows — **time**, **cash**, **rank**. a gain in any
of them is a gain. to require cash is to discard two thirds of the space.

## 🔴 .why — the store counts ONE of the six cells

```
        cost    gain
time     ·       ·
cash     ✅      ✅     ← the only row `quant.gain`/`quant.cost` can hold
rank     ·       ·
```

so a `sev` bound to cash is a `sev` bound to **whatever this tool happens to be able to
count** — which is the exact failure `term=eco.quant` names: *"a rank driven by quants
alone would rank by whatever happens to be countable."*

three consequences, and the third is the one that kills it:

| | what a cash-bound `sev` does |
|---|---|
| 1 | discards `gain.rank` — trust, quality, reputation — whose whole purpose in the matrix is to **prevent the undervalue of work that builds preference** |
| 2 | discards `gain.time` — hours saved forever — which is a real yield that simply has no invoice |
| 3 | 🔴 makes `sev` a **function of `cashPerHour`**, so ⚖️ can never disagree with it |

⇒ consequence 3 is fatal by construction. ⚖️ exists to fire when the cash rank and your
judgment part ways. if `sev` is derived from cash, the two can never part, the flag falls
permanently silent, and **the opine is no longer an opine**
(`define.invariant.eco.quant-informs-opine`).

⚠️ that is not a defect you would notice. a ⚖️ that never fires looks exactly like a rank
whose evidence agrees with it.

## ⚠️ .and the flag mechanism already leans this way

only a row with a cash figure can enter the cash sort, so **only cash rows can ever earn a
⚖️**. the flag mechanism can promote cash work and can promote no other kind. ❔ exists
precisely to keep that absence visible.

⇒ so a cash-bound `sev` does not merely repeat the tool's bias — it **compounds** a bias
the tool already carries and already flags against.

## .the test

> **"which cell of the 3×2 does this gain land in?"**

name the cell. `cash`, `time`, or `rank` — any one of them is a legitimate answer.

- you can name one → the `sev` has a basis. how high it goes is still your call
- you can name **none** → that is a different defect, and `rule.require.a-gain-cell-behind-every-sev` owns it
- you reached for `cash`, found none, and stopped there → 🔴 **this rule.** ask the other two rows

| when… | then… |
|---|---|
| you would write *"no direct revenue, so not p1"* | 🔴 the cue. you tested one row of three |
| a defect saves hours and earns naught | `gain.time`. a real cell, and it has no invoice |
| a defect costs trust — a tool that lies, a guarantee broken | `gain.rank`. the row the matrix exists to defend |
| a row carries ❔ | it means **unmeasured**, never **worthless** (`howto.read-eco-priority-flags`) |
| a ⚖️ fires and the inputs are right | ask what the cash figure cannot hold, then re-judge or hold on purpose |
| **no** ⚖️ has fired across a rank of many rows | 🔴 suspect this rule was broken. a `sev` derived from cash can never disagree with cash |

## .the bound — this does not make cash weak

cash is a **real** row and often the decisive one. the claim is narrower: cash is not the
**only** row, and an absent cash figure is not evidence of an absent gain.

and this grants no licence to inflate. a row with no gain in **any** of the three rows is
not p1 — that is the peer rule, and it is the half of the instinct that holds.

## .enforcement

- a `sev` justified solely by the presence or absence of a cash figure = **blocker**
- a row demoted because its gain is `time` or `rank` rather than `cash` = **blocker**
- a ❔ read as *"no value"* rather than *"not measured"* = **blocker** (that is the flag's
  whole purpose, inverted)
- a rank of many rows where ⚖️ has never once fired, unexamined = **nitpick** (the likeliest
  cause is this rule broken)
