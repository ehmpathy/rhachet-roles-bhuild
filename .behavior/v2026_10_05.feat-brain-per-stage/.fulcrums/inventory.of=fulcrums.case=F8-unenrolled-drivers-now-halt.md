always-halt on purpose, aware that an unenrolled drive is the common case: "a gate that fires on the
DEFAULT path matters more, not less" (S8). the halt teaches its own fix in one command, and one
re-enroll clears the whole route. (c) still halts, just later and mid-work. (b) defers the wish.
