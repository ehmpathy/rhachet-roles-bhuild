# F1 · every guard template declares a brain; unguarded stones inherit

## .the fork

- **a.** declare `brain:` on every guard template, and let unguarded stones inherit
- **b.** declare it only at the boundaries (the first design stone, the first build stone)
- **c.** add a guard to every stone, so every stone declares its own brain

## .taken — a, and why

the wish says "every stone … is driven on" its brain. bhrain's prescription is sticky, so (b)
already lands every stone right on a clean drive. but (b) breaks when a route resumes mid-phase on
a fresh session, or after a hand swap: no guard before the next boundary would put the brain back.
(a) re-asserts at every guarded stone. bhrain re-dispatches on every entry regardless (it
converges, never compares), and a switch keeps the conversation (seed S5), so the repeat costs naught.
for an unenrolled driver, (a) and (b) halt alike: both declare a brain on `1.vision`, and one
re-enroll clears every later stone (F8). (c) adds
~25 guard files to research stones that need no gate — churn for little gain over (a).

## .rework

clean. it is one line per guard template. a move to (b) or (c) touches no caller.

## .confidence — 85%, and why not higher

(c) is the only form where *every* stone lands right regardless of session history. the wisher may
read "every stone" that strictly.

## .where

the `brain:` line in each `src/domain.operations/behavior/init/templates/*.guard*`.
