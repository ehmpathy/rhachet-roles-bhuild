# case=2 · the blueprint boundary switches to sonnet

feel = happy · care = critipath · the densest cell (c3, with c14 as its setup)

## .narrative

mara runs `rhx init.behavior --name feat-invoice-tax --size mini`. the tree it prints now lists one
more file than before: `4.1.roadmap.guard`. she does no step after init.

her clone drives the design stones on opus (`case=1`). the blueprint passes. the drive moves to
`4.1.roadmap`. until now that stone had no guard, so it would have kept opus. it now has a guard of
one key — `brain: claude-sonnet-5-5[1m]` — and no reviews or judges. bhrain switches the clone to
sonnet on entry.

the clone writes the roadmap on sonnet and runs `--as passed`. the guard holds no reviews and no
judges, so bhrain passes it at once; the pass render carries the note `artifacts only`.

the clone moves to `5.1.execution.phase0_to_phaseN`. that guard also declares sonnet, so bhrain
re-dispatches it — a benign repeat. execution, verification, and playtest all run on sonnet —
cheaper and faster than opus.

the aha: the brain moved at the one place the work changed kind — from shape to build — and nobody
had to remember to move it.
