# case=4 · a sonnet reviewer runs on sonnet-5-5

feel = happy · care = critipath

## .narrative

i am the driver on `3.3.1.blueprint.product`. i run `--as passed` and the guard pours its peer
lanes. two of them are claude lanes — the architecture lane and the intent lane. last month their
`.given` files came back from `claude-sonnet-5[1m]`. today they come back from
`claude-sonnet-5-5[1m]`: the guard's `enroll claude --model` line names the newer sonnet. i read
the same shape of review, written by the current model.

the other lanes on the same guard — the `rhx review` ones — come back as they always have: their
render names `openrouter/deepseek/flash`, bhrain's default, as before. only my claude lanes read
differently, and only in which sonnet wrote them.

later, on `5.1.execution` and `5.3.verification`, i see the same: their claude lanes come back from
`claude-sonnet-5-5[1m]`, and their `rhx review` lanes come back as before.

## .timeline

```
given('[case4] an enrolled driver on a fresh mini route, at 3.3.1.blueprint.product')
  when('[t0] the driver runs --as passed and the guard pours its peer lanes')
    then('each enroll claude lane runs with --model claude-sonnet-5-5[1m]')
    then('each rhx review lane runs on the brain it ran on before (unchanged)')
  when('[t1] the driver passes to 5.1.execution and runs --as passed again')
    then('the claude lanes on that guard also run with --model claude-sonnet-5-5[1m]')
```

the proof that no other lane moved is a test, not part of this walk — see `case=10`.
