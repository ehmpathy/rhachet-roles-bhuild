# case=3 · nano crosses from vision to build

feel = happy · care = critipath

## .narrative

a nano route has no criteria, no blueprint, no roadmap. the vision is the whole design phase.

jun inits a nano behavior to fix a typo in a cli hint. the clone writes the vision on opus. the
next stone is `5.1.execution.from_vision`. its guard declares `brain: claude-sonnet-5-5[1m]`, so
the clone switches to sonnet right there and writes the fix on sonnet. `5.3.verification` declares
sonnet too; bhrain re-dispatches it, a benign repeat.

the boundary moved with the shape of the route. no blueprint stone was needed to mark it.
