# experience dimensions: the public-repo promise gate

the gate asks one question per record, at the moment it would be sent: *is this text bound for a
public repo, and has someone vouched that it is anonymized?*

## .axes — each orthogonal to the rest

| axis | values | why it varies the experience |
|---|---|---|
| **content** | `new` (title + description) · `exid.text` (`--exid` with title or description) · `exid.status` (`--exid` with `--status` only) | decides whether new text leaves the machine |
| **visibility** | `public` · `private` · `internal` · `unreadable` (gh fails: network, auth, absent repo, a token that cannot read it) | decides whether the text is world-readable once sent |
| **promise** | `absent` · `present` | decides whether anyone vouched for the text |
| **pusher presence** | `present` (the record is this push's own) · `absent` (the record waits in the backlog, carried out by a later push or by `queue drain`) | decides the fate of an unvouched public record: dropped (the pusher is here to re-run) or held (no one is here to re-run it) |
| **channel** | `gh.issues` · `os.fileops` | decides whether a remote send happens at all |

## .why `presence` is its own axis

an earlier draft folded presence into the promise axis (`on-push` · `on-record` · `on-drain`), so
two promise values could only occur on the drain path — a whole empty region of the box, the tell
of two correlated axes. the latent axis underneath was presence. it now stands alone, and the
promise collapses to `absent · present`.

where the promise comes from is a mechanism, not an axis:

| presence | a promise is `present` when… |
|---|---|
| present | this push carries `--promise task-to-public-repo-is-anonymized` |
| absent | the held record carries it (recorded at its push) **or** the `drain` passes `--promise` |

## .surfaces beside the gate

two surfaces show the gate but send naught, so they sit outside the product and are itemized apart
in `case=_`: `--help` (both skills), and `queue get` / the stop-hook reminder.

## .the product walked

3 content × 4 visibility × 2 promise × 2 presence × 2 channel = 96 cells. `case=_` verdicts every
one; where a rule is the same across a value set, a row says `any` and names the set it covers.
