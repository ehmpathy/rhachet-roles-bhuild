# 1.vision.experience.case=_ — the inventory

every cell of the walked product, verdicted. the axes and the collapse rules (R1–R6) that fold
the raw product to these grids live in `1.vision.experience.dimensions.md`. each grid below is
walked whole: every value of its axes has a row.

care = freq × cost. verdicts: **demoed** (a case file) · itemized (on record, no demo) ·
**forbidden** (the actor may try; the behavior refuses) · **impossible** (no attempt can occur).
