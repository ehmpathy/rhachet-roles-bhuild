# 1.vision.experience.case=_

the coverage contract. one row per cell of `1.vision.experience.dimensions.md`.

care = freq × cost. a **critipath** is a path the wish leans on, or one whose break costs a lot.
an **alterpath** is cheap to absorb because a workable fallback still reaches the goal.

cells marked **deferred** sit outside this behavior's scope: the vision and blueprint checkpoints,
and the pii hold they need (F1), moved to the follow-up behavior
(`dreams/2026_10_24.feat-init-behavior-checkpoint-commits-with-pii-hold.dream.md`). each keeps its
verdict, its care, and its demo, as the record the follow-up starts from.

## .boundary density

c3 is the densest cell. it crosses five boundaries at once: local → remote, uncommitted →
committed, no pull → open pull, red → green watch, and the line the driver must not cross (merge).
it is the center demo. c23 is the densest sharp cell: the push, the pull's head, and the watch's
verdict must all agree before the stone may pass.
