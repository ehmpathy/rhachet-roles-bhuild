# 1.vision.experience.case=_

the coverage contract. one row per cell of `1.vision.experience.dimensions.md`.

care = freq × cost. a **critipath** is a path the wish leans on, or one whose break costs a lot.
an **alterpath** is cheap to absorb because a workable fallback still reaches the goal.

the vision and blueprint checkpoints, and the pii hold they need (F1), ship in this behavior (the
commander's rule, 2026-11-18). the pii read is F1's fail-safe: a checkpoint pushes only where a
human opted the repo in (`.behavior/.checkpoint.jsonc` = `{ "push": "allow" }`); else it commits
local, and 6.preship pushes it.

cells marked **text-proven** are the ship and reflect acts of the tail, and the checkpoints. this repo ships them as
stone text a driver reads, and no live tail run happens in this route (F21, ruled by the
director: a live run commits and pushes from a test). so each is proven at the grain where it
lives: its arm is pinned in the template by `templates.tail` (case1–case5, with each `rhx` call
it names parsed against the installed skill), and its guard's self-review demands the paste of
the real output at run time. the live proof is the first consumer run. c23 and c34 carry the
same kind of bound already (an upstream clamp; a text rule).

## .boundary density

c3 is the densest cell. it crosses five boundaries at once: local → remote, uncommitted →
committed, no pull → open pull, red → green watch, and the line the driver must not cross (merge).
it is the center demo. c23 is the densest sharp cell: the push, the pull's head, and the watch's
verdict must all agree before the stone may pass — an agreement the watch itself owes, cured in
`rhachet-roles-ehmpathy` (F18).
