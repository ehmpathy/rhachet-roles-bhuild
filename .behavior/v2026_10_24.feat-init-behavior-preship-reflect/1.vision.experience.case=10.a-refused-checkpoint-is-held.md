## .narrative

bert starts a fresh session and inits a route. the clone writes the vision, and its peer reviews
pass. the stone asks for a commit and push. the session has no quota, so `git.commit.set` refuses.

the clone does not reach for raw git, and does not halt. it notes the hold at the top of the vision
yield — the refusal word for word, and the grant — and arrives for approval with the yield
uncommitted. bert reads the yield on the box this once. the director lands the held commit when the
switch opens; later checkpoints and preship find the quota in place.
