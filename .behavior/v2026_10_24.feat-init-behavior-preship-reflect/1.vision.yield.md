# 1.vision · init.behavior ends with 6.preship and 7.reflect

closes ehmpathy/rhachet-roles-bhuild#590 · supersedes #589

## .scope

this behavior ships the **tail**: `6.preship` and `7.reflect` on every size. the wish's second
outcome — the vision and blueprint **commit checkpoints**, with a pii hold — is deferred to its own
follow-up behavior, by the director's split (2026-10-24). it hinges on F1 (how a repo is known as
pii), which is dirty, risky, and the commander's call. the follow-up:
`dreams/2026_10_24.feat-init-behavior-checkpoint-commits-with-pii-hold.dream.md`.

the cut is clean because F1 governs only the push **before** the finish. preship pushes **at** the
finish, which the wish already prescribes for pii repos ("push at preship"). so preship needs no pii
read, and with no checkpoint, no stone in this behavior pushes before the finish.

## the outcome world

### before

a route's last stone is `5.3.verification` (nano) or `5.5.playtest` (mini+). the driver passes it
and stops. no stone asks it to commit, and `rule.forbid.commits-the-route-did-not-ask-for` (bhrain
driver) forbids a commit no stone asked for. so the work sits on a local tree, suite green, until a
human chases it. measured 2026-10-23: 12 hours moved a 22-row ship bar from 0 to 3; about 8 rows sat
route complete, uncommitted.

### after

every route that `init.behavior` writes ends in two stones:

```
… → 5.3.verification → [5.5.playtest] → 6.preship → 7.reflect
```

- **`6.preship`** — the full suite green locally; then commit at the done gate, push, open the
  pull, and `rhx git.release --watch` until green for the pushed head. never `--apply`, never merge.
  a refused commit is **held**, never a halt: the refusal is noted verbatim, the route goes on, and
  the director lands the commit when the switch opens (F3, F4). a refused push is held the same way
  (F10, ⚑ a flagged best guess).
- **`7.reflect`** — what could we have done better, and what to generalize? three whys deep. at each
  layer, enbrief the lesson and entool the cure in the repo that owns it.

### the aha

the commander's night changes from "chase eight worktrees" to "open eight green pulls". the route
carries its own work across the line; the human only holds the gate.

### the contract, in one view

| where | what the driver does | the warrant |
|---|---|---|
| `6.preship` | full suite green → commit → push → pull → `git.release --watch` green for the head → stop | the stone asks |
| `7.reflect` | three whys; enbrief + entool per layer, in the owner repo | the stone asks |
| any other stone | no commit | `rule.forbid.commits-the-route-did-not-ask-for`, unchanged |

the driver rule is not loosened. these stones are what it already calls sanctioned commit #1: *the
route asks*. the push policy every repo has today is unchanged before the finish: no stone pushes
earlier than preship.

## mental model

- to a friend: "the route doesn't stop at 'tests pass' any more. its last steps are 'ship it' and
  'what did we learn'."
- analogy: a beaver does not stop once the dam holds; it checks the flow downstream and patches the
  cause. preship is the dam tested in the real stream; reflect is the patch at the cause.
- their words → ours: "land it" → `6.preship`; "the retro" → `7.reflect`; "it's on the box" →
  uncommitted, the defect.

## evaluation

### how well it solves the goals

| goal (from the wish) | met by |
|---|---|
| a completed route ships | `6.preship` on every size |
| the driver may commit | the stone is the warrant the driver rule already honors |
| a completed route learns | `7.reflect` on every size |
| pii stays local until done | no stone pushes before preship |
| the tail cannot drift | the clamp, red then green |
| the wisher reviews on github | **deferred** — the follow-up behavior (F1) |

### pros

- one change in one place (`nano.adds`) reaches every size, since sizes are cumulative.
- no driver rule changes; the warrant moves into the route, where the rule already looks.
- the hold rule keeps the human in charge of every grant without a stall: the route finishes, and
  the held commit waits for the director.
- the tail ships without the pii question: no repo is named, no new push lands before the finish.

### cons

- every route now spends two more stones. a typo fix pays for a reflect.
- reflect after preship means an in-repo cure lands after the pull went green (q6).
- the stones lean on skills from another role (`git.commit.set`, `git.release`, ehmpathy mechanic).
- the full suite runs again at preship, though `5.3.verification` already ran it. the re-run earns
  its cost: `5.5.playtest` fixes land between the two, and the commit must carry what was tested.
- the wisher still reads vision and blueprint yields on the box until the follow-up lands.

### edgecases and the pit of success

- **no quota** → the commit is held: the refusal noted verbatim, the grant command named, the route
  goes on (case=5). the driver never reaches for raw git.
- **red local suite** → no commit; the full suite gates it, not the touched scope (case=4).
- **red remote** → fix, re-commit, re-watch; a flake gets the deflake workflow (case=6).
- **a watch that reports done too soon** → `git.release --watch` can read an empty or stale rollup;
  the stone asks the driver to match `headRefOid` to `git rev-parse HEAD` (c23; a dream is caught
  for the owner repo).
- **the urge to merge** → the stone forbids `--apply` and merge outright (c9, forbidden).
- **extant routes** → findsert keeps their stones; untouched, they never gain the tail (c16). a
  re-run of `init.behavior` with the same name finds the extant route and adds only the absent
  stones (case=14).

assumptions, stated:

- stone order is name order, so `6.` and `7.` sort after every `5.x` (verified below,
  `computeNextStones.js:24-25`).
- "commit at the done gate" = after the full local suite is green, not after a human approval.
- "drive it green" = until the pull's head is the pushed sha and every check on it is green; then
  the stone passes.
- "the full suite" = every test kind the repo declares, run via `rhx git.repo.test --what all
  --mode apply` (ehmpathy mechanic). a consumer repo's suite is its own; the stone names the skill,
  not a test list.
- the route runs on a feature branch. `git.commit.push` never pushes to main, so a route on main
  meets a refused push — held (c6).
- this route is nano and was inited before the change, so it carries no preship of its own. its
  pull is warranted by the wish's done bar and by "the route is finished".

  pins "keeps the extant … guards". so a re-init adds the tail and keeps every edit (case=14).

  rule threw `stone has a prefix outside 1.–5.` (q2).

- no template mentioned `commit`, `push`, or `git.release` before this change (grepsafe over
  `templates/`).
- driver rule `rule.forbid.commits-the-route-did-not-ask-for` — sanctioned commit #1 is "a stone or
  guard names a commit as a deliverable". the new stones are that.
- bhrain `dist/domain.operations/route/stones/computeNextStones.js:24-25` — the next stone is the
  first incomplete one in `compareStrings` order, and stones that share its prefix run together. so
  `6.preship` follows every `5.x`, and `7.reflect` follows `6.preship` alone.
- ehmpathy mechanic `git.commit/git.commit.set.sh:766-767` — absent quota prints the constraint
  `no commit quota set` and the grant `git.commit.uses set --quant N --push allow|block`: the
  verbatim text a held commit quotes (case=5).
- ehmpathy mechanic `git.release` — `--watch` watches cicd with no automerge; `--apply` enables
  automerge. the stone names the first and forbids the second. `_watch_pr_transport` reports done on
  an empty rollup and never reads `headRefOid` (c23): a defect owned there, a dream caught.

## what is awkward?

- **reflect after preship.** the order ships first, then learns, so an in-repo cure arrives after
  the green pull (q6). the reverse order would learn before the ship, but the wish orders it 6 then 7.
- **half the wish.** the checkpoints wait on the follow-up; until then the wisher reads design yields
  on the box.
- **a cross-role lean.** the stones speak in mechanic skills; a behaver route now assumes a mechanic
  is enrolled (q7).
- **two more stones on every nano route.** the cost is small, but a one-line fix now ends in a
  retro.
