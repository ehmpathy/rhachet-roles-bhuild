# case=9 · reflect goes three whys deep

feel = happy · care = critipath

## .narrative

the pull is green. the clone moves to `7.reflect` and asks: what could we have done better?

it finds one: the playtest cited a test that did not exist, and a reviewer caught it two rounds
late.

- why? the playtest was written before the acceptance test.
- why of why? the playtest stone does not ask for the test first.
- why of why of why? the template treats a citation as text, not as a path to check.

at each layer it writes a brief for the lesson, and puts the cure where it is owned. the third
layer's cure lives in the playtest template of this repo, so it lands here. one cure lives in
another repo's driver skill, so the clone dispatches it there and catches a dream with a route
symlink.
