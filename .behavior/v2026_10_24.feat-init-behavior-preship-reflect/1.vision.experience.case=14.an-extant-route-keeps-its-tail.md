# case=14 · an extant route keeps its tail

feel = sharp · care = alterpath

## .narrative

bert has a route he inited last week; it ends at `5.5.playtest`. he upgrades bhuild and expects
the new tail. re-run of `init.behavior` on that route keeps every extant file and adds the stones
it lacks — findsert writes absent files, so `6.preship` and `7.reflect` appear, and the stones he
edited stay as he left them.

the edge: a route he never re-inits never gains them, and naught tells him so. the fix is one
command he already knows: `init.behavior` with the same name **and the route's own `--size`** finds
the route (`init.behavior.ts:138`) rather than a new dated dir.

the second edge, surfaced at the blueprint review (i006): `--size` defaults to `medi`
(`init.behavior.ts:89`) and a route does not record its size. a bare re-run of a nano or mini route
findserts every medi stone into it. so the re-run must name `--size`; the readme says so, and the
dream `init-behavior-warn-on-bare-reinit-of-extant-route` carries the root fix.

## .timeline

```
given('[case14] a route inited before this change; it ends at 5.5.playtest')
  when('[t0] the driver passes 5.5.playtest')
    then('the route is complete; no preship stone exists')
  when('[t1] the human re-runs init.behavior on the same route, with its own --size')
    then('6.preship and 7.reflect are created; every extant stone is kept unchanged')
    then('no stone of another size is added')
```
