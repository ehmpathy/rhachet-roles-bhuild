## departures from the archived blueprint

- case5 checks `git.commit.set` by flag parse only, not by a plan run in a fixture repo with a
  quota state file. a hermetic plan run must also fake the global and org blocker files under
  `$HOME`, the remote the org check reads, and the sponsor state — and `git.commit.set` reaches
  for the keyrack after its sponsor guard. that is a fixture of the supplier's whole gate stack,
  owned better by the supplier's own suite. the stone keys its sort on the class of refusal
  (input vs grant), never on an exact refusal string, so no assertion here depends on that text
- case=16 ("no ship tools halts at the first commit") is proven in two halves, not one live
  drive: `journey.tail` [t7] proves the ship tool fails loud where no role is enrolled, and
  `templates.tail` holds the stone's `.an absent skill` arm (a halt that names the role to
  enroll). a live drive to 6.preship's commit step is the live tail run F21 rules out
- case=15 ("a feigned reflect is sent back") is not built here. its sense is a judgment: the
  7.reflect self-reviews ask whether the reflect read the record and went three whys deep (F5,
  self-reviews only). the deterministic floor under it (a promise with no fresh articulation is
  refused) is bhrain's own, held by bhrain's suite, not by a template this repo ships
