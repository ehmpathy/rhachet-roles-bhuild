# case=2 · a broken tail goes red

feel = sharp · care = critipath

## .narrative

a maintainer adds a `6.2.deploy.stone` to the mega size, or a `dels` entry that drops
`7.reflect` from giga. the suite goes red before the change ships. the failure names the size
and the two stones it found at the tail, so the fix is plain.

## .timeline

```
given('[case2] the size config, with one size whose tail is broken')
  when('[t0] the clamp runs')
    then('it fails, and names the size, e.g. size=mega tail=[6.2.deploy, 7.reflect]')
  when('[t1] the maintainer restores 6.preship, 7.reflect as the last two stones')
    then('the clamp passes')
```
