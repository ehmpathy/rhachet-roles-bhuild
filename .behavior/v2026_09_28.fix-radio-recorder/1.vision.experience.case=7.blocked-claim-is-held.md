# case=7 — a blocked claim is held, and a stale replay is refused loud

cells: **U2** (critipath), **U3** (critipath) · feel: sharp `[t0]`, happy `[t1]`, sharp `[t2]`

## .narrative

the clone picks up issue #412 and claims it. the radio is blocked. today that claim dies, and
nobody upstream knows the clone took it. with the recorder, the push records the claim, holds it,
and says so. the clone drives on and does the work.

later the gate opens and the clone pushes a new task. the drain carries the held claim out first,
oldest first: #412 turns CLAIMED upstream. if the clone also held a deliver for #412, that deliver
replays right after the claim — the line order is the lifecycle order.

on another day, the held claim waits while a human on another branch claims #412 themself. when the
clone's drain replays its claim, `setPartRadioTask` refuses: *task already claimed by different
branch*. the recorder does not retry and does not hide it. it closes the record as **refused**,
prints who holds the task, and delivers the rest of the backlog.

        🦫 back in the river! the backlog is delivered

## .why refused is its own outcome

a refusal is a **constraint** — the world moved, and a retry will never pass. an upstream fault
(case=5) is a **malfunction** — a retry may pass. so a refused record closes; a faulted record stays
QUEUED. to retry a refusal would re-print the same error on every push forever; to drop it silently
would let two actors each believe they hold #412.
