## invariants

- **no fallback past a chosen auth** — rows 15–18, 24, 25 never read ambient. a clamp asserts it
  for 16 (push `--auth`), 17 (stored auth), 24 (`drain --auth`), and the replay guard for 18
- **the line names a kind, never a value** — `GH_TOKEN env` · `GITHUB_TOKEN env` · `gh login`;
  no token text, ever
- **one delivery per record** — a resend under ambient reuses the upstream findsert, so a refusal
  under the bot then a pass under ambient yields one issue, never two
