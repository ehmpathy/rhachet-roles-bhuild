# case=8 — drain, bot token accepted (happy · critipath)

## narrative

a clone on a grove finishes its stone and remembers two follow-ups it pushed last week, held
behind a gate that a human has since opened. it runs a drain with no flags. both land as the
ehmpath bot, the identity the commander expects on every robot task. the clone reads its output
and sees two deliveries and no fallback line — so it knows, without a second look, that no
human or grove identity was borrowed. the `GITHUB_TOKEN` its grove carries never left the box.
