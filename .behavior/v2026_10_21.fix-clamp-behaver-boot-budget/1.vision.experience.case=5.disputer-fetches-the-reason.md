## .narrative

a reviewer flags a route: an experience found at execution was never itemized. the driver asks
*"why must the catalog evolve mid-route? the vision already passed."* the resident cue says what,
not why. its `.see also` names `rule.require.experience-catalog-evolution.reason.md`, and the boot
lists that file under `<briefs.ref>`. the driver reads it: a stale inventory lies; downstream
stages inherit the gap; the walked space stops its claim to completeness. the dispute settles in
one fetch.

alterpath: a dispute is rare, and its fallback (read the old `.md` in git history) is cheap.
