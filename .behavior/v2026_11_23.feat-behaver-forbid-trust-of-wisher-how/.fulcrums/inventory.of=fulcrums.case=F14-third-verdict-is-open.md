# F14 — the third verdict on a how is `open`

## .the fork

round i004 found the vision's verdicts were keep or drop alone, while a how can also turn on a call only the wisher can make. the word for that third verdict:

- `open` — the how stays unsettled, and it rides the vision's open questions as `[wisher]`
- `challenge` — the word the issue uses for the act on every how
- `ask` — names the remedy, not the state

## .taken — `open`, and why

- **`challenge` is the act, not a verdict.** every how is challenged; keep and drop are its outcomes too. a verdict named `challenge` would overload the one word for two senses (`rule.forbid.domain-term-ambiguity`).
- **`open` matches the vision's own vocabulary.** the vision stone already holds an open-questions section, so an open how lands in a place the reader knows.
- **`ask` names the next act, not where the how stands**; keep and drop each name a state.
