# 1.vision.experience.dimensions

the experience space of "how many crews can we afford, and what raises it". each axis is a small
closed set, and each carries its own reason it is orthogonal to the rest. the product is walked
below and verdicted in `1.vision.experience.case=_.md`.

vocab: a **limit** is one axis's crew count (tokens.5h, tokens.7d, grove.ram, grove.cpu). the
**affordable count** is the lowest limit. the limit that sets it **binds**. a **slot** is the
gap between the affordable count and the crews the ledger holds.

## .the axes

| # | axis | values | why it matters | why it is orthogonal to the rest |
|---|------|--------|----------------|----------------------------------|
| A1 | **actor** | `commander` (human) · `supervisor` (clone) · `tick` (system: a cron, no human) | each reads the answer through a different surface | who asks implies no grove, account, or read state |
| A2 | **goal** | `read` (how many fit?) · `gate` (may I sprout one more?) · `rank` (which lever?) · `verify` (did it pay?) · `calibrate` (cap % → token limit) | each wants a different shape of answer | the goal implies no grove or account state; it only selects which other axes the actor sees |
| B | **grove** | `local` (the box the verb runs on) · `cloud` (0901) | limits differ per box | identity alone; its liveness is axis L |
| L | **grove liveness** | `awake` · `asleep` (hibernated) | an asleep grove cannot be read and must not stall the sweep | a cloud grove can be either, whatever the account or read state. `local × asleep` is impossible (below) |
| C | **cap calibration, per window** | `both` · `7d only` · `5h only` · `neither` | a token limit exists per window, only once that window's tokens-per-% is known; the two windows calibrate apart (case=3 case3) | a property of the account's calibration, independent of grove, share, or read state |
| S | **account share** | `sole` (one grove's seats) · `shared` (seats on two groves) | a shared account splits its token limit | independent of calibration: a shared account is calibrated or not |
| D | **crew kind** | `behavior` · `directive` · `other` | the wish asks per kind; burn differs ~10× (one window) | kind is fixed at sprout; it implies no grove or account state |
| E | **crew state** | `gate` · `work` · `ladder` | burn swings by state | a crew of any kind, on any grove, passes through all three |
| F | **limit that binds** | `tokens.5h` · `tokens.7d` · `grove.ram` · `grove.cpu` | decides which levers pay | once the affordable count is defined, exactly one limit binds. a token window that C leaves un-derived cannot bind (rows 5–6 below) |
| K | **slot state** | `free` · `full` | the gate's answer | free vs full is a count against F's limit, not a choice of which limit binds |
| G | **read completeness** | `whole` · `partial` (a config dir or seat unread) · `piped` (stdout closed early) | the extant meter is blind to actor dirs and died on EPIPE | a per-file or per-stream fault, not a grove fault; grove reach is axis L. the one correlation: on an asleep grove no read happens, so G has no value there (below) |
| H | **lever evidence** | `2+ reads, varied conditions` (of the lever's before/after, or of the cost it targets) · `1 read` · `0 reads` (config / issue thread) | `rule.always.re-measure-a-lever-before-you-rank-it`: only the first may rank; the wish allows an estimated gain if it rests on a measured cost | evidence is about the lever, not the grove or account |
| T | **lever target axis** | `tokens` · `grove.ram` · `grove.cpu` | a lever pays only when its target is the limit that binds | what a lever cuts is fixed by the lever; which limit binds is fixed by the grove. K2 is the cell `T ≠ F` |
| P | **calibration read shape** | `one read, no reset time` · `one read + reset time` · `pair in one window` · `pair straddles a reset` · `pair with no burn between` | decides whether tokens-per-% can derive, and from which window | it depends only on when reads land against each window's reset; grove, kind, and limit do not move it |
| N | **concurrency** | `sole ask` · `concurrent asks` (two askers, one tick) · `ask amid a sprout` (a crew boots between read and act) | the gate is check-then-act | when an ask lands is independent of which grove, kind, or limit is in play |

## .the walk — how the product collapses

the raw product, one factor per axis in table order (A1 · A2 · B · L · C · S · D · E · F · K · G
· H · T · P · N), is 3 × 5 × 2 × 2 × 4 × 2 × 3 × 3 × 4 × 2 × 3 × 3 × 3 × 5 × 3. most axes reach
only some goals, so the walk folds by which axes each goal varies on.

**feel is not an axis.** happy vs sharp is an attribute of each cell, derived from the others: a
cell is sharp when it meets a refusal, a partial read, or an absent limit, and happy otherwise.
to walk feel as an axis would double-count those cells.

| goal | varies on | does not vary on | distinct |
|---|---|---|---|
| `read` | B, L, C, S, D, F, G | E (in the mix), H, K, N, T, P | 9 |
| `gate` | B, L, C, F, K, N, and E where a crew is parked | D (fixed by the sprout), H, G | 7 |
| `rank` | F, T, H | the rest (a rank is per limit that binds) | 4 |
| `verify` | H, E | the rest | 2 (+ the `0 reads` cells, impossible) |
| `calibrate` | C, P | the rest | 3 (`one read + reset time` and `pair in one window` share one render → C1; `one read, no reset time` and `pair with no burn between` share one → C3) |

the fold rule: two cells collapse when the actor sees the same render and acts the same way.

## .impossible and forbidden cells the walk yields

one row per cell, so the count is checkable.

| # | cell | verdict | why |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | `local × asleep` | impossible (nature) | `local` is the box the verb runs on; it is awake while the verb runs |
| 2 | `asleep × whole` | impossible (nature) | on an asleep grove no read happens, so G takes no value — an L fact, not a G one |
| 3 | `asleep × partial` | impossible (nature) | same: `partial` means a file unread on a grove that *was* read |
| 4 | `asleep × piped` | impossible (nature) | same: no stream opens for an unread grove |
| 5 | `C ∈ {7d only, neither} × F = tokens.5h` | impossible (nature) | the 5h window has no tokens-per-%, so no 5h limit exists to bind |
| 6 | `C ∈ {5h only, neither} × F = tokens.7d` | impossible (nature) | same, for the 7d window. `7d only × tokens.7d` stays possible, and is R9 |
| 7 | `verify × 0 reads × gate` | impossible (nature) | a verify is a before/after read; with no read there is no after |
| 8 | `verify × 0 reads × work` | impossible (nature) | same |
| 9 | `verify × 0 reads × ladder` | impossible (nature) | same |
| 10 | a grove limit (ram, cpu) on an account | impossible (nature) | an account holds no ram or cpu |
| 11 | a crew state (E) on a lever rank | impossible (nature) | a rank is per limit, never per crew state; the mix is folded in |
| 12 | `tick × gate` | forbidden (nurture) | a tick could ask, but a sprout is decided by a human or a supervisor; a tick never dispatches |
| 13 | `gate × asleep` (G4) | forbidden (nurture) | a supervisor can ask for a slot on an unread grove; the gate refuses, since a count it cannot read is a guess and a guess is the over-sprout the gate prevents |
| 14 | `rank × H = 0 reads` (K4) | forbidden (nurture) | a lever priced off a config or an issue thread can be listed; `rule.always.re-measure-a-lever-before-you-rank-it` bars its rank |

## .the axis that does the most work

**F, the limit that binds.** it changes the rank of every lever: a lever on an axis that does
not bind buys **+0 crews** until the one that binds moves. every render that ranks a lever names
F first.
