# F11 · a crew's kind is derived from its tree

## .the fork

the wish counts **behavior** crews (driver, reflector, forks) apart from **directive** crews
(director, observer, forks). no process or transcript names its kind. so the kind is either:

- **(a) derived from the tree** — a route bind flag for the branch ⇒ behavior; a repo root
  outside `_worktrees` ⇒ directive; all else ⇒ other
- **(b) declared** — each seat records its kind at boot, and the readers read that

## .taken, and why

(a). it needs no change to how crews boot, so it reads every crew live today, and the two verbs
ship without a fleet rollout. behavior crews are the route-bound worktrees by construction.

## .rework

clean. the derivation is one function in each reader (`git.crew.tokens`, `git.grove.capacity`);
a switch to (b) replaces it and touches no caller.

## .confidence, and why it is low

65%. two reads on 0901 found no directive crew that burned in the window. so the directive rule
is untested on live seats, and a director that runs inside a worktree would read as `other`.
the directive row borrows its costs from the grove median, and says so.
