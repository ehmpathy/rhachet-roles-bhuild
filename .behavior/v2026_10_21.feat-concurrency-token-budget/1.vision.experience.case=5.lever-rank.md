# case=5 · the commander ranks the levers

covers: K1 (happy), K2 (sharp), K3 (sharp), and the forbidden K4 · care: critipath

## .narrative

the commander has budget for two fixes this week and wants the two that buy the most crews.

none of the candidate levers is built yet, so none has a before/after. the table still ranks
them: each **estimated** row rests on a measured cost — the share of ram, cpu, or tokens the
lever would remove, read twice in different conditions — times the share it would remove,
with that share's basis stated. gain is in **crews, per grove, against the limit that binds
there**.

the top row on 0901 is not the largest token cut — it is the fork cap, because forks hold ram
and 0901 is ram-bound. the start-forks-small lever targets ~half of cost-weighted token spend,
yet shows `+0 crews` on 0901 and on cpu-bound local: a real cut on axes that do not bind.

one lever's target cost has only one read; it prints its gain and `1 read · unranked`. one
more comes from an issue thread, with no read of the cost it targets; it prints `unmeasured`,
and the table refuses to rank it.

the commander funds the fork cap and the hook server — the top rows on the axis that binds on
each grove — and, once they land, a before/after read moves each into the **measured** tier.

## .bdd sketch

```
given('[case1] levers whose target cost has 2+ reads in varied conditions')
  when('[t0] the commander runs `rhx git.grove.capacity --levers`')
    then('levers rank per grove by gain in crews against the limit that binds there')
    then('each row names its axis, the measured cost it targets, the share it removes, and its read count')
    then('a measured row (before/after) ranks above every estimated row')

given('[case2] a lever whose axis does not bind on this grove')
  when('[t0] the commander runs `--levers`')
    then('its gain reads +0 crews, beside its real cut, with the axis that does bind')

given('[case3] a lever whose target cost has one read')
  when('[t0] the commander runs `--levers`')
    then('it prints under `unranked`, with its gain, never with a rank number')

given('[case4] a lever with no read of the cost it targets')
  when('[t0] the commander runs `--levers`')
    then('it prints under `unmeasured` with `?` gain; it is never ranked')
```
