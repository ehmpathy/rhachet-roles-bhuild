## .the coverage contract

6 demos (case=1..6) cover 14 critipaths (R1, R2, R3, R5, R9, G1, G2, G6, G10, K1, K2, K3, C1,
C2). 36 alterpaths (R4, R6, R7, R8, R10–R14, G3, G5, G7, G8, G9, G11–G16, K5, V1, V2, C3–C8,
L1–L6), of which V1 is demoed in case=6 and C3 in case=3; the other 32 itemized, each tested row
named by its clamp. 3 forbidden cells (tick × gate, G4, K4) and 11 impossible cells, one row each
in the dimensions file.

the rows R10, R11, R14, G8–G16, K5, C4–C8 and L1–L6 were added at 5.3, when the verification walk
found tests of experiences the catalog did not hold (`rule.require.experience-catalog-evolution`).

break costs a read or a seed, never a wrong number.

six role=any skills this route touched (`grove.census`, `git.branch.merge.main`,
`route.concerns.absorb`, `chmodsafe`, `task.output.await`, `claude.statusline.probe`) are clamped
in `roleAnySkills.integration` but belong to other wishes. they are not held here; the 5.3 yield
flags them to the wisher ("flagged to the wisher — tested here, held by no catalog").
