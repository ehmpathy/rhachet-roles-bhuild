# case=4 · part of the fleet cannot be read

covers: R2, R5, and the forbidden G4 · feel: sharp · care: critipath

## .narrative

the 0901 grove has hibernated overnight, and one worktree's actor dir is unreadable (a crew
was felled mid-write). the commander runs the read anyway.

local prints in full and promptly. the 0901 block prints `💤 asleep — unread (timeout 20s)`
rather than a hang. local's burn row carries a mark — `≥ 6.6M/h (1 config dir unread)` — and
the unread dir is named, so the commander knows the burn is a floor, not a total.

the supervisor then tries to gate a sprout onto 0901. the gate refuses: an unread grove has no
affordable count, and a guess here is the over-sprout the gate exists to prevent. it names the
wake command.

this is the shape of the extant meter's defect, as the wish reports it: crews under
`_worktrees` went uncounted, and the total it printed looked whole. (the likely cause — it read
`~/.claude` alone, while each crew's transcripts live in its worktree's actor dir — is a
hypothesis until its source is found; yield Q1.)
