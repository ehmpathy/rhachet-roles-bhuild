# case=4 — the prioritizer seat refuses supervisor material

> **actor** = `clone` (a 🔦 prioritizer seat) · **goal** = `learn` — get the concepts
> **cell** = 07 (`clone × learn × origin`), sharp leg
> **feel** = sharp · **care** = critipath · a **rejection invariant**

## .the narrative

this one already happened, and the record of it is why the lift must not carry `role=any` across.

a prioritizer session opened in nheuron. asked to work a prioritization task list, it ran a
**fleet sweep** and reported on crews. the wisher stopped it in four messages:

> *"hey why are you sweeping anything"*
> *"do you not understand your purpose?"*
> *"your purpose is to help prioritise"*
> *"not supervise"*

⚠️ the four lines above are the wisher's words verbatim (`rule.always.archive-the-wishers-words-verbatim`).

the obvious diagnosis — the clone was careless — is wrong, and so the obvious repair fails. the
cause is the **boot ratio**. `repo=.this/role=any/` boots into *every* role in that repo, and it
is almost entirely supervisor material:

| what booted | how much |
|---|---|
| `im_a.bhuild_supervisor` — a persona, with a vibe and a mandate | the whole of it |
| the babysit cron, the crew verbs, the grove saturation ladder, the modal-key table | ~150 briefs |
| 🔴 the prioritizer's own briefs | **zero** |

🔴 **zero, and that is measured, not rhetorical.** `role=prioritizer/boot.yml` states it at source:

> *"this role's briefs had NEVER been in a clone's context. `role=prioritizer` appeared in no
> SessionStart hook, while `rhx eco.priority` resolved by name and ran fine — so a clone could
> write to the priorities store all day under NONE of its rules."*

⇒ the ratio at the moment of the drift was **~150 : 0**. the seat had a supervisor's whole
procedure set in context and not one line of its own. ⚠️ the boot hook that fixed this landed
**2026-09-14**, after the incident — so a lift that carries `role=any` across re-creates the exact
pre-hook state in every consumer repo at once.

a session that opens with **one detailed, imperative, ready-to-run procedure** and **no domain
rules of its own** will run the procedure. not out of disobedience — because *"babysit the fleet
each tick"* is the one item in context shaped like an instruction.

🔴 **and it re-arms.** the supervisor material carries a **cron** and a **tick contract**; the
prioritizer material carries neither. the drift arrives with its own schedule, and each tick
re-proposes it.

⇒ **the aha**: the role boundary is not documentation. it is **what boots**. a seat that can see
another seat's procedures will eventually run them, and no amount of instruction outruns the
ratio. the lift is the cheapest moment to draw the line, and the line is a directory.

## 🔴 .how it fails safe

the narrative above proves the edge exists. it does **not**, by itself, prove the edge is guarded —
and this drift is silent by construction: a drifted prioritizer looks **productive**. it polls, it
reports, it acts. the wisher paid four messages to notice.

⇒ so the fail-safe cannot be "the clone notices". it must be **structural**, and it must be a test:

```gherkin
[t7] given a test that boots role=prioritizer and captures the brief set
     when it asserts the set
     then NO brief whose path is role=supervisor/** is present
     and the test names the offender when it fails
     ⇒ fast (a unit test) · loud (red) · teaches the fix (the path names the wrong role dir)

[t8] given the symmetric test for role=supervisor
     then NO eco.* / goal.* brief is present
     ⇒ the pair is what makes it an invariant rather than an aspiration
```

⚠️ **the directory split is the guard, and the test is what keeps it one.** without `[t7]`/`[t8]`
the split holds only until the next person adds a brief to the convenient dir — and that regression
is exactly as silent as the original defect.

✅ **`[t8]` asserts a 13-artifact floor.** `F2` places the whole `eco.*`/`goal.*` vocabulary under
`role=prioritizer`, so `[t8]` is literal: **no `eco.*` / `goal.*` brief under `role=supervisor`** —
the 8 `term=eco.*`, the 2 `term=goal.*`, the 2 `define.invariant.eco.gain.*`, and the one stray
fixture, all of them moved. ⇒ a partial move goes red here, the same **floor** shape `case=6`
`[t7]` uses on the fixtures.

⚠️ **and it makes `case=5`'s citation audit carry weight.** supervisor briefs genuinely cite
`goal.surgoal` and `goal.effect` (`surgoal.squeeze-the-grove`, `define.cost-gain-matrix`). those
become cross-role paths the moment the move lands, and they fail silently — so `[t8]` and
`case=5` `[t6]`/`[t7]` are now a pair: one asserts the move happened, the other asserts it did not
mint phantom paths.

## ⚠️ .the bound

this is not a ban on shared verbs. a prioritizer still commits, reads a repo, runs a test, greps.
what is forbidden is the **supervision procedure** — the poll-then-steer loop over another party's
live work. the line is drawn by the rank-vs-run test, never by the tool's org.

## .the peer rule — port them as a pair

`rule.always.prioritize-never-supervise` (prioritizer) names one layer;
`rule.require.speak-at-the-supervisor-layer` (today `role=any`) names the other. **the pair names
the seam**, and the brief says to read them together. a lift that moves one and not the other
leaves half a seam — and mints a phantom citation besides (`case=5`).
