### ⚠️ .the four gates are COMMAND-level, so where they fire is wider than where they are recorded

the gates live in `git.tree.behavior` and `git.tree.duct`, never in a locale. so they fire in
**every** cell that reaches the command — 01, 02, 03, 13, 14, 15. the table above records the
invariant at **two** of those, and the rule for which two is written down here rather than left for
a reader to infer:

> **the invariant is recorded where the reach is NEW.** cells 02 and 03 are the two the lift
> creates — a consumer and a grove each answer `no skill found` today. an invariant that has never
> once been exercised is the one a lift can drop and no one notices.

| cell | why it is not in the table |
|---|---|
| 01, 13 (`supervise × origin`) | the gates **already fire today** at origin. their sharp leg is a *regression* risk, not a new invariant — `itemized · alter`, and the row says so |
| 14, 15 (`human × supervise × {consumer, grove}`) | a human types the same command a clone does. these inherit 02 and 03 rather than add a rejection, and their notes name the row they mirror |

🔴 **cell 03 carried `itemized · alter` for five drafts** — the same four gates as 02, on the lift's
own headline cell, verdicted as though a grove were exempt. `case=1` `[t6]`–`[t9]` demos them **from
that cell**, so the demo and the matrix disagreed and the demo was right. ⇒ found by
`dimensional-decomposition` at i002, and the repair is the verdict, the table, and this rule.
