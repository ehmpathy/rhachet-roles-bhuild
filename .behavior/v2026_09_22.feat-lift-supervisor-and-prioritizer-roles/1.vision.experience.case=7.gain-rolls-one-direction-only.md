# case=7 — gain rolls one direction only

> **actor** = `clone` (🔦 prioritizer) and `human` (reads the rank) · **goal** = `prioritize`
> **cells** = 04 (`clone × prioritize × origin`), 17 (`human × prioritize × consumer`)

## .the narrative

a human asks the store one question: *"what is this whole root worth?"*

two open issues answer it in opposite directions, and both are live in the backlog today:

| issue | opened | the rule |
|---|---|---|
| `#386` | 2026-09-13 22:45 | gain propagates **DOWN**, surgoal → subgoal, and sums across parents |
| `#389` | 2026-09-14 06:37 | 🔴 gain flows **UP**, parts → whole, and a whole's **own** figure **overrides** its parts' sum |

`#389` names itself the reversal of `#386` in its own title and says, of `#386`: *"close it against
this one, or amend it; do not leave both open as live rules."*

**the arithmetic settles it.** a parent's own figure and its children's sum are two *estimates of
one quantity*, never two quantities — so `own + Σ children` is `whole + whole`. under `#386` the
parent's figure appeared on the parent **and** on every child, which forced that brief to forbid a
portfolio total outright. **that forbid was the tell**: the one number a human most wants could not
be computed from the store at all. up-summation is additive by construction, so a root's figure IS
the portfolio figure.

⚠️ **and the reversal gives up a real signal.** `#386` existed for the N-birds-one-stone case: a
goal that advances three roots **rose without an advocate** — the wisher's word was *"naturally"*.
up-summation removes that. a multi-parent goal now reads identical to a single-parent one, which
is the exact failure the graph was built to cure. `#389` records this as an **open fulcrum** with
an unbuilt candidate repair: surface the **serve count** (`×3`) as a visible rank input — a count
is not a dollar, so it never enters a total, and it carries the signal the figure used to.

⇒ **the aha**: both briefs ship. `#386`'s conclusion is superseded and its **argument outlives
it**, so it is kept with a banner rather than deleted. a store that quietly implements one
direction and discards the other's record loses the reason the direction was chosen.

## ⚠️ .the counter-argument, stated fairly

own-wins can be the **worse** estimate and it discards the better one. a root priced `USD 1000.00`
from a gut call, whose four children each carry a measured `USD 600.00`, reports the guess and
buries the `USD 2400.00` measured from below.

the answer that holds is thin: the override is **visible** — the children keep their figures and
the parent carries no `derived` mark — so a human can see the disagreement and strike the parent's
figure. `#389` grades this clause **nurture**: an implementation may reasonably surface **both**.
it may not **add** them.
