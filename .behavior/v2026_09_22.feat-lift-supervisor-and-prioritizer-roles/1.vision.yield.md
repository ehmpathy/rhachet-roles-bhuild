# 1.vision.yield — graduate the supervisor and prioritizer into published roles

## .the outcome world

`ehmpathy/rhachet-roles-bhuild` declares **six** roles. two of them — ⛺ `supervisor` and 🔦
`prioritizer` — carry two years of tools that run today in exactly one repo on exactly one laptop.

### a day in the life

a beaver opens a grove box to sprout a tree for `ahbode/svc-jobs`. one command brings the tree up:
worktree, ducts, a clone per seat, a bound route. an hour later it polls the crew and gets one
verdict per crew. it asks the store what is worth the work next, and the store — *this* repo's
store, ranked by *this* repo's goals — answers in a treestruct.

none of that is new capability. **all of it is new reach.**

### before / after

| | before | after |
|---|---|---|
| a grove sprout | `no skill found`; **eight commands by hand** | one command |
| a third-party repo that wants a rank | no path at all | `rhx eco.priority get` |
| the dependency | `git.tree.behavior` shells out to `rhx init.behavior` — bhuild, one repo over | one package |
| the prioritizer's seat | boots ~150 supervisor briefs and runs a babysit tick | boots its own 30 |
| 31 radio dispatches | open, up to two months | judged, each with a cited artifact |

### the aha

> **the tools were never absent. the *reach* was.**

every `no skill found` on a grove, every repo that cannot rank its own backlog, every hand-typed
eight-command sprout — one cause: `repo=.this` links to one repo. the lift builds no feature. it
changes where the code lives, and a whole class of failure disappears at once.

⇒ the second aha, and the one that costs money if missed: **the expensive half of a role is not its
code.** it is the 426 lines of boot curation that decide what a session must already know versus
what it can look up. each half carries its own measured cost, in its own header:

| the uncurated boot | measured |
|---|---|
| supervisor (`role=any/boot.yml`) | **159,698 tokens — 63% of a ~253k window** |
| prioritizer (`role=prioritizer/boot.yml`) | **65,049 tokens ($0.20)**, 2026-09-14 |

⚠️ **they are separate measurements and a consumer that enrolls both pays both** — a floor of
**224,747 tokens per session, ≈89% of the window**. ⇒ the headline `63%` is the **supervisor half
alone**; a both-roles consumer opens near the whole of it. that verdict took two years and it ports
unchanged or not at all.

## .mental model

### how a user describes it to a friend

> *"the beaver tools used to only work in vlad's brain repo. now they're an npm package, so any
> repo can boot a crew or rank its own backlog."*

### the analogy that fits

**a trade moves from one workshop to a toolbox you can carry.** the saw did not change. what
changed is that it is no longer bolted to one bench — and the fixtures in the bottom of the box
(56 files, 51 of them captured panes) are why the saw still cuts true after the move.

the rejected analogy: *"a library extraction."* it misleads, because the expensive artifact here is
**briefs and boot curation**, not code. a library extraction that carried 77,742 lines of shell and
dropped 426 lines of yaml would be called a success and would be a failure.

### their words vs ours

| they say | we say | why ours |
|---|---|---|
| "spin up a worktree" | **sprout** | `#325` — sprout is the only word that boots a tree; every other dispatch **seeds** |
| "the boxes" / "the fleet" | **crew** · **tree** · **grove** · **camp** | `#320` — the four work-layer words. ⚠️ `duct`, `term`, and pane are **substrate**: diagnosis vocabulary, never a verdict's subject |
| "a stuck session" | **husk** | a husk has a precise discriminator, and 56 fixtures pin it |
| "how important is it" | **sev** · **urg** · **gain** · **work** | an opine is authored; a gain rolls. conflate them and you fabricate opines |
| "the box's user" | **camper** | 🔴 `clone-user` is a **forbidden** synonym for it. below |

⚠️ **one term is ours and contested**: `role=any`. it reads as "shared", it behaves as "every role
in this repo", and that gap is the whole of `case=4`. the lift retires it.

### 🔴 .the camp mythology — four terms, three repos, one role that must speak all of them

the supervisor's vocabulary does not originate here. it is assembled across a boundary, and the
⛺ artifact names the top of it:

```
camp              1 aws account — the elastic container of groves + their shared services
 └─ grove         0..N hibernation-ready ec2 work-boxes, elastic, self-hibernated when idle
     ├─ camper    1 OS login — the door every crew member enters by
     └─ tree      0..N git worktrees on the box
         └─ crew  the seats on one tree
             └─ clone   1 actor instance per seat
```

| term | it IS | it is NOT | declared in |
|---|---|---|---|
| **camp** | an aws **account** that holds an elastic set of groves + the vpc/nat/iam/reach they share | an env, a fleet, a workspace | `ahbode/infrastructure` |
| **grove** | a **work-box** in a camp — many git trees, durable keys, hibernates when idle | an ec2, a vm, a devbox, a sandbox | `ahbode/infrastructure` |
| **camper** | the **OS login user** the crew ssh in **as** | 🔴 a clone. `clone-user` and `crew-user` are forbidden | `ahbode/infrastructure` |
| **clone** | a live instance of an **actor** — differs from its peers only by **session** | a session, a process, a thread | `ehmpathy/rhachet` |

🔴 **the seam a supervisor must hold: the VERB is ours, the NOUN is not.** a clone **camps out** on
a grove — and `camper` names the **door**, never the worker. the grove brief says it in one line:

> *"where a **clone** (robot or human peer) **camps out** to work, reached over an ssm tunnel **as
> the `camper` user**."*

⇒ today there is **one** camper per grove (`CAMP_GROVE_CAMPER_USER = 'camper'`, a singular
constant). per-clone isolation is named as a future tightened state, never the current one — so
*"each clone has its camper"* is false as well as forbidden.

🔴 **and the second half is a safety fact, not a naming call.** `camper` and the grove role's
**tier** are ORTHOGONAL boundaries: IMDS (`169.254.169.254`) answers **any** uid with no privilege,
so a camper with zero sudo already holds the grove's full badge with one unprivileged `curl`.
**camper bounds the BOX; tier bounds AWS. a control on one buys none of the other**
(`rule.forbid.camper-sudo`). ⇒ a supervisor that reads `camper` as a privilege boundary will
tighten the wrong dial and believe it shrank the blast radius.

## .evaluation

### against the wish's three gains

| gain | verdict |
|---|---|
| `gain.time` — every skill answers `no skill found` on a grove | ✅ **repaired by construction**, for every repo at once. `case=1` |
| `gain.rank` — the dependency is real and inverted | ✅ rung 3's `rhx init.behavior` call becomes in-package. `case=1` `[t4]` |
| `gain.cash` — 20+ radio tasks settled | 🟡 **partly.** 31 issues judged below — 12 fully, 13 partly, 4 untouched, 2 superseded |

### pros

- one PR, one seam — the roles, their briefs, their boot curation, and their test corpus move together
- the `role=any` → `role=supervisor` split repairs a **measured** defect at the cheapest moment
- the lift is mostly a **move**, so the blast radius is legible: a file either landed or did not

### cons

- 🔴 **a big-bang PR.** 77,742 lines, 305 brief files (275 supervisor + 30 prioritizer, measured at
  `role=*/briefs/*`), 63 code files. review is the bottleneck, and `case=6`'s fixtures and
  `case=3`'s boot curation are exactly the parts a large diff scrolls past
- 🔴 **the test corpus (36,948 lines) has never run in ANY ci** — nheuron has zero github workflows.
  it moves into a repo with two test gates, never once required to be hermetic, while it drives
  tmux, ssh ducts, and a live ec2 box. ⇒ expect *"some of this cannot run here"*, not *"some of this
  is red"*, and budget a **run-scope decision** per suite
- 🟡 one cross-package edge — `F3`, the `achiever` seam — is a best guess at 80%

### the pit of success

every sharp critipath fails **silently** — no error, no red, no human present. so each names the
guard that makes it loud, and each guard is **fast, loud, and teaches the fix**:

### ⚠️ .the checks this route's own figures owe

a claim restated in a second artifact is a **cache**, and seven shapes of cache have already gone
stale in this route's documents. the verification stone owes each check:

| the check | the shape it catches |
|---|---|
| the matrix's verdicts vs the `itemized · criti` tally | a tally that drifts from what it tallies |
| each `case=N` header's `cells =` vs the matrix's citations, **both directions** | a one-way citation |
| 🔴 each `case=N` header's **axis values** vs the closed axis set and vs its own cited cells | a header that names a value the walked product never carries |

| 🔴 each stated count vs its own **enumeration** | a true sum over false parts |
| 🔴 each **derived** figure (a %, a ratio) vs the composite it derives from | prose about a number, frozen while the number moved |

🟡 the third catches two live instances at once and they sit on **different axes**: a `goal` of
`learn / port`, where `port` is no member of `{supervise, prioritize, learn, migrate}`; and an
`actor` of `clone or human` on a case the matrix cites at one `clone` cell. ⇒ **run it per axis,
never per header** — one sweep of the goal line leaves the actor line unchecked.

🔴 **the last three share one mechanism: a claim that supplies its own verification.** a sweep for
*"56 captured panes"* passes over *"56 verbatim panes"*. `11 + 2 + 13 + 5 = 31` is a true sum whose
parts are wrong. *"near two-thirds spent"* was true of the supervisor-only 63% and stayed put when
the figure beside it became the both-roles 89%.

⇒ **none survives a check the author does not author** — grep the wrong form, re-derive the
enumeration, recompute the ratio. and **an audit is exhaustive only over the shapes its author
enumerated**: a sweep of every composite against its parts leaves every *percentage* unchecked.

### 🔴 `#377` names a gain beyond the wish — the lift dissolves a HAZARD, not only a gap

`#377` weighs three ways to get a verb onto a reviewer seat's PATH, and rules **candidate 2** —
*"`rhachet roles link` the supervisor role into the mirror"* — hazardous:

> 🔴 *"a mirror is reset to the crew's snapshot on every refresh, and `tree.sync` refuses when the
> mirror was edited since its last sync. whatever is written INTO the mirror to provision it becomes
> the very state that blocks the next pull."*

⇒ **that hazard is a consequence of a repo-local registry.** to link `repo=.this` is to *write into
the mirror*. a **package** role resolves from `node_modules` and writes not one byte into the tree,
so the mirror stays byte-identical and the next pull is not refused.

🟡 conditional: it holds only where the **crew's own repo** already depends on
`rhachet-roles-bhuild`. elsewhere the hazard stands and candidate 1 is still the move.

⇒ `case=1`'s aha, one step further: reach does not merely repair `no skill found` — **it retires a
class of workaround that exists because the one way to gain reach is to write into someone else's
tree.**

## .what is awkward

1. 🔴 **the PR is too big to review well, and both ways to split it are closed** (see *what is
   settled*). the awkwardness does not go away — it moves from *"should we split?"* to *"how is this
   reviewed?"*. ⇒ the mitigation is a **review order**: read `boot.yml` and `work/.test/.assets/`
   **first**, before 77,742 lines scroll past them. those are the two artifacts a large diff hides
   and the two whose loss is silent in every test we run.

   (`role=any` → `supervisor`), re-homes a vocabulary (`F2`), settles a reversal (`F1`), decides a

   miss the five decisions inside it.

   box". the axis that predicts behavior is *"is a repo-local registry linked?"* — invisible, and
   unconsidered until `no skill found`. the decomposition is right and it is **not** how anyone
   talks. that gap is why the defect went unrepaired for two years.

   hazards"* — a closed-set title over an open set.

   already in this yield. ⇒ a confidence column is a weaker instrument than it looks. the workaround
