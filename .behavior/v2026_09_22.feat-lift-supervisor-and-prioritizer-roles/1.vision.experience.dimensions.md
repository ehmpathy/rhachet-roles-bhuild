# 1.vision.experience.dimensions

## .what

the axes this lift's experience space varies over, and the product they generate. the walked
product lives in `1.vision.experience.case=_.md`; the demos in `1.vision.experience.case=N.*.md`.

## .how the axes were picked

a lift moves no feature. it moves **reach** — who can invoke a capability, and whose context
carries its concepts. so the space is not factored by feature; it is factored by **who stands
where, after the move**.

⇒ that is why `locale` is an axis and `skill family` is not. a crew verb and a grove verb fail
alike on a box with no repo-local registry, and succeed alike after. the family is a detail; the
stance is the experience.

## .the four axes

### A — actor (3)

| value | who |
|---|---|
| `clone` | an ai seat in a repo — the party that types `rhx …` |
| `human` | the wisher/operator — enrolls, boots, migrates, reads a rank |
| `system` | ci, the build, the boot hook, the babysit cron — **no human present** |

`system` is a legitimate value, not a filler. it surfaces the unattended paths (a cron tick, a
build that must copy `.sh` and `.log` assets) that a human-only list drops.

### B — goal (4)

| value | what the actor came for |
|---|---|
| `supervise` | sprout a tree, poll a crew, reach a grove — the 🦫 seat |
| `prioritize` | rank the work, seed a row — the 🔦 seat |
| `learn` | get the role's **concepts** into context (the `boot.yml` half) |
| `migrate` | move off the repo-local registry onto the package |

🔴 `learn` is split out from `supervise`/`prioritize` deliberately. the handoff's §4.3 records the
exact failure that forces it: `rhx eco.priority` **resolved by name and ran fine** while the
role's briefs had never been in a clone's context. **the capacity booted and its concepts did
not.** a space that folds `learn` into the act cannot express that cell, and that cell is a
critipath.

### C — locale (3)

| value | where the actor stands | repo-local `.agent/repo=.this/`? |
|---|---|---|
| `origin` | `uladkasach/nheuron` — the repo that holds it repo-local today | yes |
| `consumer` | any other repo that enrolls bhuild | no |
| `grove` | a remote box (ec2) that drives a worktree | 🔴 **no — and this is the defect site** |

`grove` is not a kind of `consumer`. a grove has a checkout **and** a `rhachet` on PATH with no
`repo=.this` role linked — a third state, and the one `git.tree.behavior`'s own header names:
*"`rhx git.tree.behavior` there answers `no skill found`."*

### D — feel (2)

`happy` (glides) · `sharp` (hits an edge that can cut).

## .orthogonality

| pair | independent? | why |
|---|---|---|
| actor × goal | ✅ | each of the 3 actors can hold each of the 4 goals. ⚠️ the walk's six `impossible` cells are **uniform across actor**, so they come from the pair below rather than this one |
| actor × locale | ✅ | a clone, a human, and a cron each occur at all three locales |
| 🔴 goal × locale | ⚠️ **correlated today** | `migrate` is attemptable at `origin` **only** — rows 11, 12, 23, 24, 35, 36 are `impossible`. so a goal value implies a locale value, which is exactly the correlate the method's test asks about |
| feel × all | ✅ | every real cell has both a happy and a sharp leg |

### 🔴 .the `goal × locale` correlation — why the axes still stand apart

the method's reviewer test is *"does any value on one axis imply a value on another?"*, and here
the answer is **yes**: `migrate` implies `origin`. the latent axis underneath is
**`had-repo-local?`**, and it is **degenerate with `locale = origin` today** — `origin` is a set of
size one, so the two cannot be told apart by any cell in this walk.

⇒ we keep the axes separate rather than fold `migrate` into `had-repo-local?`, for one reason:
**the correlation is a fact about the world today, not about the concepts.** the moment a second
repo vendors these roles locally, `origin` gains a member, `had-repo-local?` is no longer
degenerate, and the whole `migrate × ¬origin` column revives. a fold would have to be un-folded to
see that.

⚠️ **and the correlation is evidence that carries weight, never a blemish to hide.** a column that
cannot occur is what the method predicts when a latent axis hides underneath — so the honest record
is the `⚠️` above, never a `✅`. the walked product is what caught this; the table's earlier `✅` was
a claim made **before** the walk and never re-checked against it.

⚠️ **the one axis pair we rejected.** an earlier cut used `actor = {consumer-clone, lift-driver,
incumbent, system}` against `goal = {sprout, rank, port}`. it fails orthogonality outright —
`lift-driver` implies `port` and naught else, so the two were one axis under two names. the
repair was to lift the *stance* (`locale`) out of the actor, which is what makes `clone ×
migrate × origin` and `clone × migrate × consumer` distinct and both real.

## .the product

```
actor (3) × goal (4) × locale (3) = 36 blocks
                     × feel  (2)  = 72 cells
```

the inventory tables **36 rows**, one per `actor × goal × locale`, with a `happy` and a `sharp`
column — 72 verdicted cells, the full product.

## .the latent axis the walk surfaced

the `system` row forced a dimension that no free-list would have produced:

> **`has-fixtures?` — does the classifier under test carry the verbatim panes it was judged
> against?**

it appears only at `system × supervise × *`, because only an unattended classifier run can be
wrong in a way no human sees. it is the axis behind `case=6`, and it is why the handoff's
instruction to carry `work/.test/.assets/` verbatim is a **critipath**, not a chore.

⇒ a second latent axis, `has-citation-target?`, fell out of the `learn` row the same way — see
`case=5`.

## 🔴 .the axis we considered and declared OUT of scope — `concurrency / time`

the method names concurrency as one of the **usual omissions**, beside `actor = system` and
resource-absent states. the other two are handled — `system` is a full actor row, and absent
resources drive `case=2`, `case=8`, `case=10` — so concurrency owes an explicit answer rather than
a silence.

**the candidate values**: `first` · `concurrent` · `retried`.

⚠️ **it is declared out of scope, and the reason is a property of THIS behavior rather than a
shrug**:

> **the lift relocates code; it changes no concurrency semantics.** every race in this domain — two
> clones that sprout into one repo, a cron tick that fires mid-session, a re-run sprout — behaves
> **identically before and after**. the lift changes *where the verb resolves from*, never *what
> happens when two callers reach it at once*. ⇒ a `concurrency` axis would multiply 36 rows into
> 108 and every new cell would carry the verdict *"same as today"*.

🟡 **three time-shaped behaviors exist here, and each is already placed:**

| the time-shaped behavior | where it lives |
|---|---|
| the babysit cron fires **periodically**, unattended | `actor = system`, the whole row. `case=6` |
| the migration is a **one-time** act | `case=9` — and its `freq = 1` is argued explicitly there |
| a **retried** migration after a half-edit | `case=9` `[t6]`–`[t8]`, the negative leg |

🔴 **and one genuine concurrent state IS real and IS demoed** — the migration's transient window,
where a repo-local role and a package role are both linked at once:

> `case=9` `[t2]`: *"no repo-local copy shadows them ⇒ two sources for one slug is its own defect —
> the shadow wins silently."*

⇒ so concurrency yields exactly **one** experience the lift creates, and it already has a `[tn]`.
that is why the axis is declared rather than walked: a walk would find one live cell and 71 that
read *"unchanged by this lift"*.

⚠️ **the honest bound on this claim.** *"the lift changes no concurrency semantics"* is an argument,
not a measurement — nobody ran two concurrent sprouts before and after. it is the same class of
claim as `H4`'s run-scope question, and it is falsifiable: if the package's resolution path holds a
lock or a cache that `repo=.this` did not, a race could differ. ⇒ **a check for the verification
stone**, never a settled fact.

🟡 and the open concurrency question in this domain is on record as a radio issue the lift does
**not** touch: `#313` — *a supervisor collects factory upgrades while it supervises*. it is listed
`untouched` in the yield's backlog table, which is the same verdict this axis gets, reached
independently.
