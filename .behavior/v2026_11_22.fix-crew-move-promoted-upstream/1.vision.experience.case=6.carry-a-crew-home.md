# case=6 — carry a crew home from the grove

happy × critipath. the reverse of case=1: the laptop's weekly cap resets, or hetzner's account caps, and the operator wants the crew back where they sit. the contract is the same; three boundaries are not.

## .narrative

the week turns and the laptop's claude account is free again, while hetzner's is near its cap. the operator plans to carry the crew home. they name only `--to local`: the plan reads the ledger row, finds the tree on hetzner, and says so in its `from:` line. the bytes now flow the other way, pulled from hetzner straight onto this box, so the plan names the local paths it will write. the operator applies with a why. the seats come up in this box's own tmux server, and each local bar names its tree the moment its duct opens. a repo the operator's config marks local-only may take this move freely; only a move onto a cloud grove is refused for it.
