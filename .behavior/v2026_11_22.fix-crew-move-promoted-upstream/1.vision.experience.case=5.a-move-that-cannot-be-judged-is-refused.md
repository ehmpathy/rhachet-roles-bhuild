sharp × critipath. a move writes a tree, transcripts, and a ledger row across two boxes. if either end cannot be read, or a policy forbids the destination, the only safe outcome is naught moved, with the reason and the fix said aloud.

## .narrative

after one move lands, the grove drops off the tailnet. verify cannot read the seats, so it marks each unread and exits 1 rather than claim the move holds.
