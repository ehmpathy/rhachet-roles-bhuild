# case=2 — a busy or boxed seat is refused

sharp × critipath. a move mid-turn would carry a transcript that is still written, and resume a conversation at a cut point.

## .narrative

the operator plans a move while the mechanic is mid-turn. the plan refuses at once, before any byte moves: it names the seat, says it is busy, and gives the read that shows when its turn ends. a seat with unsent text in its box is refused the same way, with the cure to let its owner submit or clear it. apply refuses on the same facts. once the turn ends, the same command moves the crew.

## .timeline

```
given a crew whose mechanic seat is mid-turn at the source
when [t0] rhx git.crew.move --tree <tree> --to cloud://<grove> --mode apply
  then exit 2; ✋ ConstraintError: seat mechanic is busy mid-turn at the source (...)
  then the fix line reads: wait for its turn to end: rhx git.crew.read --tree <tree> --who mechanic
  then naught changed at either grove
given the same seat with text typed in its box, no turn live
when [t1] the same command
  then exit 2; seat mechanic has text in its box at the source; fix: let its owner submit or clear the box
given the turn ended and the box is empty
when [t2] the same command
  then the move proceeds as case=1 [t1]
```
