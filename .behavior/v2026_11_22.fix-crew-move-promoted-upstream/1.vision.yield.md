# vision — git.crew.move in bhuild, and a bar that every raised seat owns

## .what this builds

1. **`git.crew.move` in bhuild** — the pioneer verb lands at `src/domain.roles/supervisor/skills/`
   beside `git.crew.boot` · `heal` · `fell`, with its two suites. source: the four files under
   `~/.rhx/promote/git.crew.move/`, byte for byte, then adapted only where bhuild's lib split
   demands (`work/crewwork.sh` loads the `ductwork.*.sh` parts here, not one `ductwork.sh`).
   two operator policies the pioneer hard-codes become config, since the verb now ships in a published
   package (`rule.forbid.private-business-in-published-roles`; the clamp
   `src/domain.roles/orgNamesAbsent.integration.test.ts` forbids the names, 13 hits in the pioneer):
   - the org allowlist (`CREW_MOVE_ORGS="…four orgs…"`) reads `GIT_CREW_MOVE_ORGS`; unset = no org bound
   - the `nheuron.*`-stays-local guard becomes `GIT_CREW_MOVE_LOCAL_ONLY_REPOS` (repo names whose crews
     move only `--to local`); the operator sets it, the package names no repo
   - comments and fixtures that cite real groves and trees speak of sandpine
2. **the bar stamp, in the duct lifecycle** — bhuild's ductwork gains the pin nheuron already holds
   (`__duct_stamp_status_box`): a duct writes `@duct_repo` / `@duct_branch` and pins its session's
   `status-left` / `status-right` to them. the call sits inside the three duct verbs every seat raise
   passes through (`ductwork.life.sh`), so no crew verb has to remember it:

   | duct verb | the crew paths it serves |
   |---|---|
   | `duct.open` (`:13`), on both `created` and `found` | boot and `--resume` (`crewwork.boot.sh:155`), and so a move |
   | `duct.reboot` (`:256`) | `crew.reboot` (`crewwork.pane.sh:301`) |
   | `duct.refresh` (`:365`) | heal (`crewwork.heal.sh:1337`, `:1392`), the view repaint, a duct opened before the pin |

   the stamp reads repo · branch from the duct's **home anchor** (`@home_cwd`), never the live cwd — a
   stamp from the live cwd on a `found` duct whose shell has `cd`'d away would pin the drift it cures
   (nheuron `ductwork.sh:4521-4522`). so the anchor comes over too:
   - `duct.open --cwd` writes `@home_cwd` (nheuron `:1243-1245`)
   - `duct.refresh` gains `--home`, which corrects a wrong anchor out loud (nheuron `:4786-4827`)
   - `crew.refresh` (`crewwork.view.sh:475`) passes `--home` from `__crew_role_cwd` (`crewwork.sh:1885`),
     the worktree convention, so a seat born in the wrong dir is re-anchored to its own tree

## .why it works — the bar gap, named

- the bar draws ` #{@repo} ` / ` #{@branch} ` (tmux.conf). the user's zsh hook rewrites those pane
  options on every prompt, from whatever cwd that shell sits in
- nheuron cured that by a duct-owned pin (`ductwork.sh:4556-4619`), called at crew boot
  (`crewwork.sh:9803`, `:9892`) and every `duct.refresh` (`ductwork.sh:4843`)
- **bhuild never got the pin.** grep of `src/domain.roles/supervisor/skills/work/` for
  `@duct_repo|status-left|__duct_status_box` = 0 hits; bhuild's `duct.refresh`
  (`ductwork.life.sh:365`) stamps naught
- and even in nheuron, a seat raised by `git.crew.boot --resume` skips the stamp. read: both stamp
  calls sit in `__crew_await_boot` (`crewwork.sh:9803`, `:9892`), whose one caller is `crew.seat`
  (`:7209`, hire / respawn); `crew.boot` (`:11820`) never awaits. the pioneer measured the effect
  (`git.crew.move.sh:202-206`): a seat on the hetzner grove drew a blank bar after a move, 2026-11-20
- ⇒ **cause:** the stamp is absent from bhuild, and in the pioneer it rides a crew verb (the boot
  await), so any raise that skips that verb skips the bar. **cure:** put the stamp one layer down,
  in the duct verbs every raise already calls — boot, resume, heal, reboot, and move all get it, and
  no post-move sweep is owed

## .the outcome world

before: the operator moves a stalled crew from the laptop to hetzner by a hand chain of git, tar,
scp, ssh. the seats come up, the bar reads blank or names whatever repo a seat shell last `cd`'d
into. they `rhx git.crew.read` each seat to learn which tree it is.

after:

```
rhx git.crew.move --tree svc-lessons.beav.feat-book-a-lesson --to cloud://grove-sandpine-hetzner-v20261119
#   plan: branch, head, pushed?, dirty paths, each seat + transcript, every refusal
rhx git.crew.move --tree svc-lessons.beav.feat-book-a-lesson --to cloud://grove-sandpine-hetzner-v20261119 --mode apply --why 'weekly cap on local'
rhx git.crew.move --tree svc-lessons.beav.feat-book-a-lesson --to cloud://grove-sandpine-hetzner-v20261119 --mode verify
```

each seat on hetzner resumes its conversation, and its bar reads ` svc-lessons ` · ` beav/feat-book-a-lesson `
— the same as a local seat.

aha: the bar is no longer a truce with the shell hook. it is the duct's, on every grove, from the
moment the seat stands.

## .mental model

- the operator says: *"move that crew to hetzner"* — a house move: the tree, the unsaved work, and
  the conversations ride together; the credential stays home
- the bar is the name plate on each seat's door. it is screwed on when the seat stands, not
  chalked by whoever walked past last

## .evaluation

- goals: both land. one verb replaces the hand chain; one stamp call cures the bar on every path
- pros: plan before apply, proof after; refusals name their fix; the source stays untouched (a
  fell is a later act); the stamp is cosmetic and never fails a raise
- cons: a large lift (~2.5k lines of operations); it drags dependencies bhuild lacks (below)

## .what is awkward

- the operations file is large and pioneer-shaped (one 2.5k-line lib). bhuild splits libs by axis
  (`crewwork.pane.sh`, `crewwork.heal.sh`). a byte-for-byte lift keeps the proof; a split is a later
  act
- two operator policies (an org allowlist, a stays-local repo) turn into env reads; a bare install
  carries neither bound until the operator sets it (F4, F5)
