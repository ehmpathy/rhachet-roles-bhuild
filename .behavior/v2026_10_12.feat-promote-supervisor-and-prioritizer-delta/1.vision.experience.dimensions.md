# 1.vision.experience.dimensions

## .the dimensions

who acts × on what kind of artifact × changed how × in which repo × in what state × how often.
six axes, each a small closed set, each value one concept:

| dim | values | one concept per value |
|---|---|---|
| **A. actor** — who acts | `supervisor-clone` · `prioritizer-clone` · `operator` (the human who owns the store and grants) · `maintainer` (a clone or human that edits the package — this tree, and the next) | an actor **kind**, never a time. the next delta is the `maintainer` at `T=repeat`, not a fifth actor |
| **K. kind** — what artifact | `skill` · `brief` · `store` · `fixture` | the artifact alone; who owns it is a reachability fact (below), not a value |
| **C. change** — how the delta touched it | `new` · `cured` | split out of the old `skill.new`/`skill.cured`, so it applies to every kind |
| **R. repo** — where the actor stands | `consumer` (no `.agent/repo=.this/`) · `source-like` (has one, with its own same-named skills) | see `.the R axis` — the two values are walked, not merged |
| **S. state** — the artifact's state at touch | `absent` · `populated-pre-change` · `partially-consumed` · `refused-input` | the artifact's state, never the clone's freshness. `partially-consumed` = a grant made but unspent, a migration interrupted |
| **T. occurrence** — how often | `first` · `repeat` · `concurrent` | the axis the first walk lacked: retries, the next delta, and races |

## .step 1 — reachability: A × K × C = 4 × 4 × 2 = 32 cells

which (actor, kind, change) triples the delta can produce at all. every one of 32 is accounted for:

| actor | skill·new | skill·cured | brief·new | brief·cured | store·new | store·cured | fixture·new | fixture·cured |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| supervisor-clone | ✅ 4 new skills | ✅ cured skills + `work/*` libs | ✅ 6 rules + 1 define | ✅ 8 | ⛔ a | ⛔ a | ✅ 40, via the classifier | ⛔ b |
| prioritizer-clone | ⛔ c | ✅ `eco.priority`, `ecowork` | ✅ lowfruit rule | ✅ 11 | ⛔ d | ✅ fruit columns | ⛔ a | ⛔ a |
| operator | ✅ `git.tree.size.uses allow`, `git.crew.commit.sponsor set` | ≡ e | ⛔ f | ⛔ f | ⛔ d | ✅ their store migrates | ⛔ g | ⛔ b |
| maintainer | ✅ port | ✅ port | ✅ port | ✅ port | ⛔ d | ✅ port + prove | ✅ port + test | ⛔ b |

| key | reason | cells | coordinates (actor × kind·change) |
|---|---|---|---|
| ⛔ a | the store is the prioritizer's; fixtures are the supervisor classifier's. **impossible** (nature: the other role has no verb that reaches it) | 4 | supervisor × store·new · supervisor × store·cured · prioritizer × fixture·new · prioritizer × fixture·cured |
| ⛔ b | measured: all 40 fixture entries in the delta are additions (`--diff-filter=A` = 40). no fixture is cured. **impossible** | 3 | supervisor × fixture·cured · operator × fixture·cured · maintainer × fixture·cured |
| ⛔ c | the delta adds no new prioritizer skill. **impossible** | 1 | prioritizer × skill·new |
| ⛔ d | the store is not new — fruit adds columns to an extant table. **impossible** | 3 | prioritizer × store·new · operator × store·new · maintainer × store·new |
| ≡ e | an operator who runs a cured verb meets the clone's experience with other hands. **collapsed into** the clone cell (same behavior, same output) | 1 | operator × skill·cured → S2, P1 |
| ⛔ f | an operator does not boot briefs; a clone does. **impossible** | 2 | operator × brief·new · operator × brief·cured |
| ⛔ g | only the classifier reads a fixture. **impossible** | 1 | operator × fixture·new |

the supervisor's new permissions init (F6) is not a fifth kind: it has no behavior of its own, and a
clone meets it only as a refusal of the skill it guards. so it rides in the `skill` cells it guards
(S1, O1, M1).

tally: 17 reachable (✅: supervisor 5, prioritizer 4, operator 2, maintainer 6) + 14 impossible
(⛔: a 4 + b 3 + c 1 + d 3 + f 2 + g 1) + 1 collapsed (≡) = **32**.

## .step 2 — state × occurrence on the 17 reachable cells

S × T = 4 × 3 = 12 per cell; most do not apply, and the reason is stated per **kind**, once:

| kind | S values that apply | T values that apply | why the rest drop |
|---|---|---|---|
| brief | `absent` (first boot) | `first` · `repeat` (next delta) | a brief is read, never invoked: no `refused-input`, no `partially-consumed`, no race |
| skill | `absent` · `refused-input` · `partially-consumed` (the size grant) | `first` · `repeat` · `concurrent` | `populated-pre-change` belongs to stores |
| store | `populated-pre-change` · `partially-consumed` (interrupted) · `refused-input` (cashless `--fruit`) | `first` · `repeat` · `concurrent` | `absent` (a fresh store) is reachable and kept: itemized as inventory row 23 — it now gains the fruit columns at create |
| fixture | `absent` (a pane never seen before the fixture) | `first` · `repeat` (the next delta's fixtures) | a fixture is a frozen capture; it has no runtime state and cannot race |

each surviving (cell × state × occurrence) is verdicted in `1.vision.experience.case=_.md`, with the
cell id it comes from.

## .the R axis — walked, not merged

the first walk merged `source-like` into `consumer` on an "iff" — it assumed the outcome. walked
properly, the two values carry different experiences:

| R | what an actor meets | verdict |
|---|---|---|
| `consumer` | a published skill that still reads `.agent/repo=.this/` **fails** — the path is absent. so this value **exposes** the defect | the proof cell (case 2, case 7) |
| `source-like` | the same defect **passes**, because the source repo has the path. so this value **masks** the defect, and a test here proves naught about portability | itemized as the masked cell: never used as proof |
| `source-like` | the repo holds its own copy of a same-named skill (`git.tree.list` in `.agent/repo=.this/` and in the package) | itemized: which copy rhachet resolves is rhachet's rule, unchanged by this delta; flagged so the operator is not surprised |

## .the composite walk

case 7 is a **sequence of cells**, not a fifth actor: `prioritizer-clone × skill·cured × first`
(the board) → `supervisor-clone × skill·cured × first` (nano sprout) → `supervisor-clone ×
skill·new × first` (sponsor) → `supervisor-clone × fixture·new × first` (the cured poll), all at
`R=consumer`. the inventory lists it as a walk over those cell ids.
