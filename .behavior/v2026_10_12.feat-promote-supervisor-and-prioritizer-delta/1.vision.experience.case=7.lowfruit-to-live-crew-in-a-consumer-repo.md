## .how it is proven — composed, not one live crew

a live end-to-end run would need a real tmux crew and a real brain. that costs minutes and proves
naught each part does not. so verification proves the walk as a composition, in one temp consumer
repo: `[t0]` runs live (board read); `[t2]` and `[t3]` run through the same pane harness
(`work.harness.ts`) the classifier suites already use. the one thing that must hold across the whole
walk — no read under `.agent/repo=.this/` — is asserted once, at the end.

`[t1]` is the one step with no cheap live form: `git.tree.init behavior` has no plan mode (F19), and
a real sprout needs a duct host and a worktree. at execution the consumer suite runs the sprout to
its first gate (`--into required`), which proves the verb ships and reads its gates from the
package. the full nano sprout is owed to `5.3.verification` as a catalog critipath.
