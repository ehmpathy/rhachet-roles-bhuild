# case=4 — the board shows fruit and flags a topfruit that jumps a lowfruit

**feel:** happy · **care:** critipath (the rank is what a supervisor dispatches from; a hidden trivial win is lost cash)

## .narrative

the prioritizer clone runs `eco.priority get --output rootstruct`. under `sandpine.ensell`, two p1
rows: `book-a-lesson/reminder-sms` (two days of thoughttime, cash gain) and
`coachbook/instructor-calendar` (34 days, cash gain). the board shows 🫐 on the first, 🍊 on the
second, and sorts 🫐 above 🍊 within the rung. when a p0 🍊 outranks an unstarted p1 🫐, the board
prints the warn and names the leaf. bert reads it and either starts the lowfruit or writes the
`--fruit-why`.

