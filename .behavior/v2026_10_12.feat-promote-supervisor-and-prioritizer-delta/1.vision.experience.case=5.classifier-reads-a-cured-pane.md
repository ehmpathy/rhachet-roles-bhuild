# case=5 — a pane the classifier once misread now reads right

**feel:** happy · **care:** critipath (a misread pane makes a supervisor nudge, kill, or ignore a live clone)

## .narrative

a supervisor polls its crews. one foreman's pane shows the weekly-limit menu. the published
classifier, before this delta, read that menu as an ask and would nudge into it. after the delta it
reads it as a cap and reports the seat limited. each of the 40 new fixtures is one such pane,
captured live when the source misread it.

⚠️ **measured: only 29 of the 40 are read by a source test.** 11 are cited by a brief as evidence
and named by no test (searched by full name and by distinctive fragments across every source
`*.test.ts`; no suite loads fixtures by glob). so in the package each of those 11 owes a new test
case, written here, that reads it and pins its cure — or the fixture does not move (F5).

## .timeline (sketch)

```

    then the pane classifies as a cap, not an ask
```

## .the scrub constraint

12 of the 40 fixtures carry a private name. each is scrubbed with a **same-length** substitute,
so column widths, wraps, and smears the classifier reads stay identical (fulcrum F2). the cure's own
test, green on the scrubbed bytes, is the proof the scrub kept the shape.
