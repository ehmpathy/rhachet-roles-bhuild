## narrative

a clone in the surdirector tree holds four records: A, an edit of #599 the bot cannot sign; B, a
create into sandpine/svc-lessons; C and D, creates into sandpine/spot-forecast. it pushes. github
refuses A; the radio sets A aside, delivers B, then hits a rate limit at C and halts with D behind
it.

an hour later a human, who was not at that drain, runs `get`. A's row shows github's own words —
`refusal: GraphQL: Resource not accessible by integration (updateIssue)` — and the fix: amend it
to as-human. C's row shows the halt. the human amends A and drains. the limit has cleared: A, C,
and D deliver, oldest first. A's reason and its headline are gone with its fault.
