# case=6: purpose stated, then ignored

sharp × alterpath · inventory row 8b · a chosen alterpath

## .narrative

a clone drives `sandpine-book-a-lesson`. it writes a sound root purpose, three motives deep:

```md
- why: a surfer should book a lesson in under a minute
  - why: a surfer who meets a long form abandons it and calls the front desk
    - why: every call the desk takes is an instructor hour not spent in the water
```

then it writes the shape: a twelve-field form, an account signup first, a waiver to scroll, a payment page. the evaluation praises its validation. not one line asks whether the flow is under a minute, or whether it spares the desk a call.

the depth lane counts three and approves. the motive lane finds every layer a motive — no blocker — then reads the shape against the root, and raises it loud as a nitpick:

```
[nitpick][better] the vision states its root purpose, then never answers to it
  root: "every call the desk takes is an instructor hour not spent in the water"
  the shape: a 12-field form behind a signup; the evaluation grades validation, not time-to-book
  fix: grade the shape against the root in `## evaluation` — does each step cut desk calls? cut or justify what does not
```

a nitpick, not a blocker: the wish's refusal list is absent · under 3 · mechanism-rooted, and "the shape ignores its root" is a judgment of degree. the concern still lands — the driver owes it a `.taken` (`rule.always.converge-with-reviewers.via-a-taken-per-point`), and the human approver reads it beside the vision.

the clone takes it: rewrites the evaluation around time-to-book, finds the signup step fails it, and moves signup after payment. the purpose now steers the shape — which is the point of a purpose at all.
