# wish: every vision digs to its root purpose (ehmpathy/rhachet-roles-bhuild#651)

## .outcome

every vision that init.behavior scaffolds owes a mandatory `## .the root purpose` section: why the vision is wanted at all, at least 3 layers deep (a why under a why under a why), each layer a motive, never a mechanism. a peer reviewer on the 1.vision stone refuses a vision whose root purpose is absent, under 3 layers, or names a mechanism as its root.

## .why

a reviewer reads the motive for a behavior before its shape; the builder knows why it did the work; and the vision is checked to address its purpose rather than ignore it.

## .done

the vision template carries the section with a 3-layer scaffold; the vision guard runs the peer review; a clamp test is red on a vision with no section or a 2-layer one, and green on a 3-layer one. a pr against main, ci green, ready for review. it is a peer of #590 (6.preship + 7.reflect), in its own tree.
