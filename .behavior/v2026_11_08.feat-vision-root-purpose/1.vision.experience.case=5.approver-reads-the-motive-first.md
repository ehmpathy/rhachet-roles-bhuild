# case=5: the approver reads the motive first

happy × critipath · inventory row 13

## .narrative

bert, sandpine's lead instructor, owns the `sandpine-lesson-reminders` route. the vision stone clears its lanes and halts for his approval. he opens `1.vision.yield.md`.

the first section is the root purpose — *full lessons, because an empty slot is revenue the school never recovers*. he reads it in a breath. then he reads the shape: an sms the day before, an email fallback. he grades the shape against the motive — would an sms fill more slots than an email? yes, surfers read texts. he approves.

before this change, bert would have read the sms design first and approved it on whether it looked tidy. the question *does this fill lessons?* would never have been asked.

## .timeline

```
given a vision that cleared the root-purpose lanes
  when [t0] the approver opens 1.vision.yield.md
    then the first section after the title is `## .the root purpose`
    then its deepest layer names the motive the work serves
  when [t1] the approver reads the shape that follows
    then each design choice can be graded against that root
  when [t2] the approver runs `rhx route.stone.set --stone 1.vision --as approved`
    then the approval was made against a stated motive, not an inferred one
```
