# case=9: a mechanism slips past the lane

sharp × critipath · inventory rows 10b (`[t0]`) and 13c (`[t1]`) · the densest experience

## .narrative

a clone drives `sandpine-lesson-reminders`. it writes three nested layers; the third reads like a motive but names a mechanism in motive words:

```md
- why: surfers should get a reminder before their lesson
  - why: a surfer who forgets misses the slot
    - why: so the reminder service stays the single source of truth for lesson times
```

the depth lane counts three. the motive lane — a brain, a judgment — reads "stays the single source of truth" as an aim and approves. it is wrong: that layer names an architecture, not why the school or the surfer needs it. the defect the whole change exists to catch has passed the lane.

the stone halts at the human gate. bert opens the yield. the root purpose sits first, three lines from the top, and the third line answers *how should the system be shaped?*, not *why does sandpine want this?*. it takes him one read to see it, because the section is short and sits before all the shape. he does not approve; he leaves the feedback: *"the root names a service, not a reason — why do we care that a surfer misses a slot?"*. the clone digs: *an empty slot is revenue the school never recovers*.

the lane is the first gate, not the only one. its false pass is caught because the change put the motive where the approver reads it first.

## .timeline

```
given a vision whose deepest layer names a mechanism in motive words
  when [t0] the author runs `--as arrived`
    then the depth lane prints `layers: 3 of 3` and `0 blockers`
    then the motive lane mis-grades it and prints `0 blockers`
    then the stone halts for human approval
  when [t1] the approver opens 1.vision.yield.md
    then the root purpose is the first section, and its deepest layer reads as a *how*
    then the approver withholds `--as approved` and names the gap in feedback
  when [t2] the author rewrites the deepest layer as a motive
    then the vision returns to case=1
```

## .the calibration that keeps this rare

the rule `rule.forbid.vision-root-purpose-mechanism`, the one the motive lane binds, carries paired examples — a mechanism in motive words beside its true motive — so the brain lane meets the hard case on every read; the vision stone shows the author one such pair before the first word. the false pass is not driven to zero; it is driven down, with the human gate behind it.
