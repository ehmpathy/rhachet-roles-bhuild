# case=4 — held records across many dirs drain in push order, with no clock

feel: sharp · care: critipath — the lead experience: the cross-dir drain order ([case1], [case3], [case4])

also hosts: `[case2]` byte-identity per dir — happy · critipath (the case=1 claim, across many dirs)

## .narrative

a clone holds three pushes in one worktree, all gate-shut:

1. `book-a-lesson` route: `fix lesson reminder copy` → `$route-a/.radio/`
2. no route bound: `rotate wetsuit sizes` → `.behavior/.radio/`
3. `spot-forecast-widget` route: `swell chart axis` → `$route-b/.radio/`

today the drain sorts them across dirs by `heldAt` first; `heldSeq` is minted per dir, so all three
could read `heldSeq: 1`. strip `heldAt` and the per-dir seq can no longer tell push 1 from push 3 —
the tiebreak falls to the key hash, and the drain order becomes arbitrary. that is the sharp edge.

so the seq spans the worktree: the next seq is 1 + the highest across every record dir. the three
records read `heldSeq` 1, 2, 3. the gate opens; they deliver 1, 2, 3 — the order they were pushed.

## .the fail-safe

the drain order can tie in two ways; both fail safe the same way:

- **legacy seqs** — each dir minted from 1 before this fix, so legacy records in two dirs can share
  a seq ([case3]).
- **a concurrent mint** — two pushes in two dirs at one instant both read the same max and mint the
  same seq. the mint runs in `radioTaskPush.ts:198`, outside `withRadioDrainLock` ([case4]).

in both, the order stays total — the key breaks the tie — and the drain still visits every held
record once. no record is lost or doubled. the only cost is that two independent creates may land
in swapped order; a held update carries an exid, so the create it updates landed before it.

## .bdd sketch

```
given('[case1] a worktree with two routes and the unbound home; all gates shut')
  when('[t0] three pushes land, one into each dir, in order a, unbound, b')
    then('they hold heldSeq 1, 2, 3 — one seq across the worktree')
  when('[t1] the gate opens and a fourth push drains the backlog')
    then('the three deliver in order 1, 2, 3, then the fourth')
given('[case2] the same three pushes replayed in a fresh worktree on a later day')
  when('[t0] they land')
    then('each record file is byte-identical to its twin from case1')
given('[case3] two legacy records in two dirs, both heldSeq=1 (minted per dir before this fix)')
  when('[t0] a new push lands in a third dir')
    then('it mints heldSeq=2 — 1 + the max across the worktree')
  when('[t1] the gate opens and the drain runs')
    then('all three deliver; none is lost or doubled')
    then('the two legacy records drain before the new one; between them, the key breaks the tie')
given('[case4] two pushes into two dirs at one instant (both read max=0)')
  when('[t0] both mint')
    then('both may hold heldSeq=1')
  when('[t1] the gate opens and the drain runs')
    then('both deliver once; the key orders them; none is lost or doubled')
```
