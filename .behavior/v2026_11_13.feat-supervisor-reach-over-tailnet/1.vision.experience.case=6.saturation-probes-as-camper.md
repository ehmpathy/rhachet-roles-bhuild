# case=6 — the operator's saturation sweep reads every ec2 grove, after the tunnel is gone

cell `c06` (+ its alterpath `c24`) · sharp→happy · critipath

## .narrative

bert, the foreman, runs `rhx git.grove.saturation` before he dispatches three more crews. he wants one answer: which grove has room?

the day after the upgrade, without this case, he would see every ec2 grove read as no-answer. each is registered twice, once as `grove-x` (camper) and once as `grove-x.ground` (an admin seat), and the sweep reads each grove through whichever seat sorts first, which is `.ground`. the tailnet refuses `ground` at the key. bert would conclude the fleet is dark and stop his dispatch, while every box sits idle and healthy.

with this case, the sweep reads each grove through its camper seat. bert sees one row per grove, each with its real load, and sends his crews to the one with room. if a grove was ever registered only as `.ground` (`c24`), the sweep says so: the refusal reads as a refusal, not as a quiet empty box.

## .sketch

```
given('[case6] two rows for one grove: <name> (camper), <name>.ground (ground)')
  when('[t0] rhx git.grove.saturation')
    then('one grove is reported, not two')
    then('the probe rides the camper entry')
    then('the grove reads its real load, never a no-answer verdict')
given('[case6.ground-only] one row, <name>.ground, no camper twin')
  when('[t0] rhx git.grove.saturation')
    then('the probe rides the ground entry and reports its key refusal, loud')
```
