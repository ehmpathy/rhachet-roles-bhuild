# case=001 — the prose gate blocks a bloated yield

> **who** — a driver clone, four peer rounds into a `1.vision` stone
> **came for** — to pass the vision gate and move to criteria
>
> cell — `actor=driver · lens=prose · feel=sharp · care=critipath`

## the narrative

i have converged. four peer rounds, zero blockers left. i signal `--as arrived` and expect the
stone to release.

it does not. a lane i have never met returns:

```
🔴 3 blockers
   ├─ 1.vision.yield.md:88  — chronological accretion
   │     "## what i003 found — the axes moved from 6 to 9"
   │     a time-ordered log where current truth would serve.
   │     fix: fold the result into the claim it repairs, then delete the log.
   ├─ 1.vision.yield.md:210 — restatement
   │     the access-vs-stage divergence is declared in 4 sections.
   │     fix: declare it once; cite it from the other three.
   └─ 1.vision.yield.md:340 — self-defense
         "an earlier draft said four over five cases, and review.peer i008 caught it"
         fix: the corrected claim stays; move the narration to the .taken.
```

my first reaction is that this is unfair. i wrote every one of those lines to *prove* i engaged
with a reviewer. that is exactly the incentive the gate exists to break: a repair narrated in the
artifact looks like diligence, and a repair made silently looks like neglect. so the artifact grew
every round while the design stood still.

the fix is mechanical and it takes one pass — `[t1]` walks it. no claim is lost: every citation,
every table, all case demos survive. what left was the retold.

i re-arrive. the lane returns `0 blockers`.

## the demo

```gherkin
given a 1.vision route whose peer lanes have converged to 0 blockers
  and 1.vision.yield.md carries 3 iNNN-titled sections, 4 restatements of one claim,
      and 2 paragraphs that narrate how a nitpick was addressed

[t0] the driver signals the stone as arrived
  when the prose lane runs its rubric over $route/*.yield.md
  then it returns 3 blockers
   and each names the file, the line, the defect class, and the concrete fix
   and the stone does not pass

[t1] the driver folds each result into the claim it repairs
  when the driver deletes the iNNN sections, collapses the 4 restatements to 1 declaration,
       and moves the two narration paragraphs into review/self/...taken.by_self.md
  then 1.vision.yield.md states the same content in 199 lines, down from 615
   and every citation, table, and case demo from the 615-line version survives

[t2] the driver re-arrives
  when the prose lane re-runs
  then it returns 0 blockers
   and the stone passes
   and the .taken retains the full narration, ungraded by this lane
```

## what this proves

- the gate fires on the **artifact**, never on the `.taken` — so the driver keeps a legal home for
  the narration. a reviewer that graded both would push the prose back into the artifact.
- the blocker **names the fix**, not just the symptom (`rule.require.errors-name-the-fix`).
- the repair is a single pass, so the gate costs one round rather than a spiral.
