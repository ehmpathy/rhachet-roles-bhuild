## .settled

**a support artifact carries the artifact it supports in its own filename.** the reader learns what
produced it from the name, with no lookup and no prose citation to drift.

⇒ the shape is an **infix**, not a dir: `$stage.yield.appendix.$what.md`. a dir would file every
stage's appendices together and break the `N.stage.` prefix sort that orders the route.

🟡 **and the infix is a lever, not only a label.** it partitions one glob into three, so a rubric
can name the decisions, the evidence, or both:

| glob | reaches |
|---|---|
| `*.yield.md` | the decisions |
| `*.yield.appendix.*` | the evidence |
| `*.yield*` | both |
