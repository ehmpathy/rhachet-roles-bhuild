## .settled

two claims, and the second is the one no extant rule carries.

### 1. the burden of proof inverts completely

> **if a word CAN be cut or moved to a zoom, it MUST be.**

the extant bar is a judgment — `require.reflexive-condensation` asks *"is this 95%+ important?"*, so
a line the author judges important stays. this removes the judgment: **possibility, not importance,
is the test.** a line that could have been moved and was not is a defect regardless of its merit.

### 2. the subject is the READER'S TIME, and bloat is EVIDENCE of a failed grasp

every extant condensation rule frames density as a property of the **artifact**. this frames it as
an **externality** — a cost the author transfers to every peer who reads it.

⇒ and the sharper half: *"mountains of prose that indicates we did not understand the problem simply
enough ourselves."* volume is not merely expensive; it is **a signal that the author has not
finished the thought.** that is `require.brevity`'s feynman argument — *"brevity proves grasp"* —
turned from a rationale into a **diagnosis a reviewer may cite**.
