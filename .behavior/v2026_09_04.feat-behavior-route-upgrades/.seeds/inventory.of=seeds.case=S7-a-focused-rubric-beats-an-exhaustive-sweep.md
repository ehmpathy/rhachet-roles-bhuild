## .settled

**a reviewer's rubric dilutes, and that dilution is distinct from the two the route had already
named.** three dilutions exist, and each takes a different repair:

| the dilution | the repair |
|---|---|
| **which lines** of the corpus get read | `--goal exhaustive` |
| **which files** compete for attention | a subject split — one lane per subject |
| **which rules** carry weight | 🔴 **a narrowed rubric** — the one this seed adds |

⇒ a lane that enrolls 25 rules gives each roughly 4% of its attention. where the concern is 3 of
those 25, a sweep over every line moves the verdict not at all: the sweep fixes the corpus, never
the rubric.

**and the narrow set must reach the THOUGHT, not merely the word count.** the two obvious rules
(`reflexive-condensation`, `dense-prose`) grade the result; the three the wisher named
(`domain-discovery`, `distillation`, `elucidation`) grade whether the claim was drilled to its root
before it was written. a lane with only the first two cuts words and leaves every surface-pitched
claim in place.

**a closed rule set may be braced; an open one may not.** the test is never *"is it a rule name?"*
but *"is the set the lane enumerates open or closed?"* — a lane that wants the whole canon globs,
and a lane whose design IS five named rules braces.
