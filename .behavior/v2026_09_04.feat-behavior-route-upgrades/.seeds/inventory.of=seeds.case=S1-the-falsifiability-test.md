## .settled

### 1. the test is an OBSERVATION question, never a taste question

> **what observation would settle this sentence?**

⇒ never *"could a reader disagree with it?"* — that grades **taste**, and a metaphor passes it. you
may disagree with *"it took the cheaper of two doors"*, and no observation on earth settles the
disagreement.

### 2. one concept, two shapes — and the split is a SORT, not a flag

| shape | reads as | what is absent | the repair |
|---|---|---|---|
| `shallow` | insubstantial | the sentence asserts none | **cut it** |
| `squishy` | unjustifiable | it conforms to whatever the reader wants | **replace it with the mechanism** |

⇒ the two repairs are **opposite**, so a bare *"this is subversive"* flag is one notch too coarse —
a reader told only that cannot know whether to delete the line or to write a different one.

### 3. two words per shape, and each does a different job

the plain word is the definition a reviewer checks for; the short word is the handle an author
recalls mid-sentence. `unjustifiable` is barred as the contract word because in plain english it
also means *morally indefensible*; `squishy` carries one sense.

### 4. squishy is the dangerous shape

shallow prose reads as filler, so a reviewer cuts it on sight. squishy prose reads as **insight**,
so nobody cuts it — and it takes the slot a settleable claim was owed.

## .the correction the wisher made, and why it is the seed's core

my two-sense claim was **mine, not theirs**. i traced one sense to this steer and a second to
bhuild#358/#360, concluded they had authored both branches, and opened a synonym dispute.

⇒ they had authored one concept; **i had mis-tested it.** the defect was one word of my own gloss —
i read *"disputable"* as *"could a reader disagree?"*, which is taste. their actual test is
falsifiability, under which both shapes fail identically.

🟡 the settled lesson, which outlives the term: **a test that answers YES on both the good and the
bad case is not a test — it is a filter one notch too coarse.** the check, before any rule ships:
write the defect three ways — absent, plausible-but-wrong, and correct — and confirm the test
separates all three.

## .the day the term fired on its own author

measured 2026-09-06, one day after the cluster was written. i verdicted a queue pull-in with
*"it falsifies my fail-safe"* — **squishy.** it reads as insight, invites disagreement, and names no
mechanism. the observation test cut it, and the read that replaced it **reversed the verdict**.

⇒ the sentence was not merely empty; it stood where a check belonged, and it deterred me from the
check. that is the shape `shallow` cannot describe and `squishy` can.
