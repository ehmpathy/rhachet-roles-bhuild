## part 1 — the rule

### the wisher's words, verbatim

> *"if you need something to unblock the review process, you should raise a blocker and ask me, not
> just keep cycling through and burning budget."*

### the test it adds

`rule.always.converge-with-reviewers` says settle it yourself and escalate only at a real wall. it is
silent on **how to recognize the wall while the budget still reads healthy** — which is exactly when
a driver re-arrives round after round.

> **"is there a change I am PERMITTED to make that would close this point?"**
>
> - yes → `--as contemplated`, then re-arrive
> - no → 🔴 `--as blocked`, **with the ask stated as a command the human can run**

### 🔴 the mechanism — why more rounds could not converge it

**a `.taken` converges a lane whose objection is an ARGUMENT. it cannot converge a lane whose
objection is ABSENT COVERAGE.**

that distinction is the whole rule, and it is stated nowhere in the extant ladder.

`rule.always.converge-with-reviewers.via-a-taken-per-point` gives two shapes — `[REPAIR]` (*"i fixed
it"*) and `[REFUTE]` (*"it does not hold"*). a lane that grades **coverage** accepts neither: the
driver did not fix it (they cannot), and it does hold. so the point survives every round, correctly.

### the measured case

repo `ehmpathy/rhachet`, behavior `v2026_08_25.fix-node-pty-install`, stone
`5.1.execution.from_vision`, rounds i069 → i073. two l3 lanes read the same artifact each round:

| round | r010 `enroll-impl-behavior-intent` | r011 `enroll-impl-arch-defects` |
|---|---|---|
| i070 | 1 blocker | approved |
| i071 | 1 | approved |
| i072 | 2 | approved |
| i073 | **4** | **approved** |

**r010's count ROSE while the defect surface SHRANK.** at i073 its four blockers resolved to: one
refuted (it demanded a clamp green since i071), two real and closeable only by a human, and one
disclosed + dreamed since i070.

**five rounds spent, and budget was never the wall** — r010 sat at 45/107, r011 at 42/107. neither
exhausted.

⇒ r010 grades implementation against wished behavior; r011 grades architecture. so a disclosed,
correctly-reasoned deferral reads as *sound* to one and *uncovered* to the other. r010 wrote both in
one sentence:

> *"the deferral itself is reasonable"* — and blocked on it.

**it was not wrong.** a reasonable deferral is still an uncovered behavior, and coverage is the only
quantity its rubric measures. only a test closes it, and that test needed a credential the driver
could not unlock.

### 🟡 the counter-bound — so this is not an early-exit license

it fires **only** after `rule.always.spend-own-levers-before-escalation` returns *no* — every driver
lever spent, each on the record. **a blocker raised with unspent levers is the defect this rule would
otherwise create.**

🟡 **and budget left is not evidence of a remedy.** a lane rejected for absent coverage re-raises
identically at 45/107 and at 105/107. **exhaustion is terminal; rejection is not** — so a lane can
block forever with budget to spare, and that shape reads like progress from the inside.

## part 2 — the structural find, worth its own row

the same drive measured a loop the extant rules do not name:

```
no commit quota → zero commits → `since-main` unions the whole staged+untracked set
                → ~293 files → 9 of 11 review lanes overflow their context window → dark
                → and every artifact written to ANSWER a reviewer enlarges the diff again
```

🔴 **the work that clears a reviewer is the work that blinds the next one.** a `.taken`, a dream, a
term cluster — each is the correct response to a review, and each makes the next round harder to
review.

`rule.always.diagnose-reviewer-malfunctions` names the **cure** (re-run the rubric scoped) and is
silent on the **cause that regenerates it**. the scoped re-run is a bail with a bucket; this is why
the water returns.

⇒ **the collapse lever is a commit**, which resets the diff to the fork point — and it is gated by a
quota the driver cannot grant itself. so it belongs in the **human-fixable** row of that rule's cause
table, beside the credential and the permission gate, with the exact command.
