## .the test

> **strip the diff of every mark, glyph, and header. re-read both versions. did any CLAIM change?**

no → superficial. the counter passes and the reader gained naught, so the point is still open.
yes → structural, however small the edit.

🟡 it grades the **repair**, never the point. a countable rule is a real rule and its results are
real; what this forbids is the cheapest response to one.

## .the shapes

| the point | 👎 the superficial repair | 👍 the structural one |
|---|---|---|
| a section stacks N emphasized lines | strip N−1 marks | ask **why N claims compete for one rank** — subordinate them, or cut the ones that lost |
| an absolute over an unwalked set | hedge it — `never` → `rarely` | **measure it**, or name the mechanism that closes it |
| one claim stated three ways | delete two copies | ask **which is authoritative** — declare it once, make the others cite it |
| decode friction | expand the reference inline | state the referent **once, where the reader stands** |
| a passage that is true, tight, and archaeology | cut it (lossy) or keep it (bloat) | **move it, and ref it** — the third verdict |

⇒ in every row the superficial repair passes the same counter the structural one passes, and only
one of them makes the artifact shorter, or truer, or reachable.

## 🟡 .the two carve-outs, because a coarse rule here is worse than none

| not superficial | why |
|---|---|
| **one mark, one line, stripped** | the mark WAS the whole defect. a single stray bold has no deeper repair to find |
| **a split into sections with genuinely different subjects** | `forbid.emphasis-noise` names the split as its own remedy — *"a second means the section is two sections"* |

🔴 **the split is the carve-out most likely to be abused, so it carries its own test:** name the two
subjects. if you cannot name them without a reference to the emphasis count, the split was a
count-dodge and the section is still one section.

## .the discriminator, in one line

> **repair the CLASS, never the COUNT.**

a repair that brings a number under a bar and leaves the class live will be re-raised at the next
round on the next instance — which is what a re-raise IS, and why a reviewer escalates one.

## .enforcement

- a repair whose whole content is a mark strip, a hedge, or a rename, where the point named a
  class = **blocker**. the point is not discharged
- a section split whose two subjects cannot be named without the emphasis count = **blocker**
- a `.taken` that reports a count moved under a bar, with no claim changed = **blocker**
- a single stray mark stripped = **false positive** — that is the whole repair
