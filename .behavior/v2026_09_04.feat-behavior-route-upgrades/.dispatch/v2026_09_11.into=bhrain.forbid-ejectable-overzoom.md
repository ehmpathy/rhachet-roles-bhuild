# dispatch → `ehmpathy/rhachet-roles-bhrain` — `rule.forbid.ejectable-overzoom`

> **if a detail CAN be ejected into a zoom, it MUST be. never force a reader through a zoom
> level they did not ask for.**

an **overzoom** is detail pitched one zoom level below the passage it sits in. it is **ejectable**
when a named peer artifact could hold it and a citation could reach it.

authored on route `bhuild/.behavior/v2026_09_04.feat-behavior-route-upgrades`, from two wisher steers
archived verbatim at `$route/.seeds/inventory.of=seeds.case=S8-prose-bloat-is-a-charge-on-a-peer.md`.
it belongs in `role=telepath`, beside the condensation acts it sharpens.

## .the three claims, and no extant rule carries any

### 1. the burden of proof inverts — POSSIBILITY, not importance

`require.reflexive-condensation` asks *"is this 95%+ important to mention?"*. that is a **judgment
of worth**, and an author who wrote the line has already made it once — so the bar is graded by the
one party guaranteed to pass it.

> **this asks: CAN it be cut, or moved? then it MUST be.**

⇒ the author's discretion is removed. a line that could have been moved and was not is a defect
**regardless of its merit**, so a true, tight, important line is still a violation when a zoom would
hold it.

### 2. the subject is the READER'S TIME — and bloat is EVIDENCE, not merely cost

- every extant condensation rule frames density as a property of the artifact
- this frames it as an **externality** — a cost the author declines to pay and transfers to each peer
- ⇒ and the sharper half, the wisher's own
  - *"mountains of prose that indicates we did not understand the problem simply enough ourselves"*
  - volume is not merely expensive; it is a signal the author has not finished the thought

⇒ that is `require.brevity`'s feynman argument — *brevity proves grasp* — turned from a rationale
into a diagnosis a reviewer may cite. a reviewer today can say *"this is long"*; it gives them
*"this length is evidence you have not simplified it yet."*

### 3. a zoom is PULLED, and that is what makes the move a win rather than a relocation

- inline detail is pushed — every reader pays for it, and no reader chose it
- a cited zoom is pulled — it costs one line until a reader asks, and then it costs its full depth
- ⇒ so the move does not merely relocate the words; **it moves who decides whether to spend on them**

🟡 without this claim a move reads as a filing preference, and bound 1 reads as bureaucracy rather
than as what keeps the pull honest.

## .the test — two questions, in order

> **1. can this be CUT with no claim lost?** → cut it.
> **2. can this be MOVED to a zoom and cited?** → move it.

only what survives both stays. *"but it is important"* answers neither — importance decides whether
it is **cut or moved**, never whether it stays where it is.

## .what a zoom is

a **zoom** is a named peer artifact, cited by path from the passage that owes it: a `.reason` file, a
`$stage.yield.appendix.*`, a `.notes/` file, an `example=$slug.md`, a specialization brief.

⇒ the MOVE verdict and its contract — a move with no citation is a deletion — are
`rule.require.archaeology-in-notes`'. this does not restate them; it generalizes their reach from
the read-trail to any deferrable passage.

## .the shapes

| the passage | 👎 the plea | 👍 |
|---|---|---|
| a derivation under a decision | *"the reader needs to see how I got here"* | the decision, plus a path to the derivation |
| a worked table of 7 rows, where 2 carry the claim | *"the other 5 are real cases"* | the 2, plus a path to all 7 |
| a second example of a shape already demoed | *"it illustrates a different angle"* | one demo. the rest zoom out |
| a caveat about an edge nobody will hit | *"it is true"* | true, and it is a zoom |
| a preamble that earns the claim beneath it | *"it gives context"* | the claim. the context is a zoom |
| a per-item rationale in a config or an index | *"a future editor will want to know why"* | they will. from the zoom |

⇒ in every row the passage is **true** and the repair is a **move**, not a cut. that is the whole
difference from a density rule.

## 🟡 .the two bounds, and the rule is worse than useless without them

### bound 1 — a zoom must be REACHED, or the move is a dodge

an ungraded destination lets an author satisfy the rule and help no reader: move 6,000 words into an
appendix no lane reviews, and the passage is short and the reader is no better off.

⇒ so the zoom's own subject is graded too, and the citation must say **what makes it worth an open**.
a bare `⇒ see also: $path` is a pointer, never a transfer.

### bound 2 — it does NOT swallow the canon it sits in

its grain is volume, and its repair is **eject**. every neighbour repairs by a rewrite, a promotion,
a deletion, or an addition — walked neighbour by neighbour under `.the name`, below.

⇒ a two-word sentence can hold no ejectable overzoom at all and still be a riddle. one grain each.

## .enforcement

blocker: an ejectable overzoom left in place · a passage ejected with no citation from the claim it
supports (that is a deletion, and `archaeology-in-notes` already says so) · a passage defended as
*"important"* where the question was whether it could be **ejected** — the wrong test was answered.
nitpick: a zoom whose citation does not say what makes it worth an open.
false positive: a passage whose removal loses a claim and that has no zoom to go to · a passage the
reader must hold at THIS zoom level to act on the claim beside it.

## .the name — the wisher's second coinage, and it passes the test the first one failed

this dispatch first carried `rule.forbid.prose.inconsiderate`. the wisher re-coined it
`rule.forbid.ejectable-overzoom` on 2026-09-11, and the swap is not a preference — it closes a
failure `rule.require.enumerate-before-you-name` had already recorded:

| the check | `inconsiderate` | `ejectable-overzoom` |
|---|---|---|
| covers every instance? | ✅ | ✅ the same six rows below |
| **excludes its neighbours?** | 🔴 **no** — a riddle, a diffusion, an obfuscation is each inconsiderate too | ✅ **yes** — see the walk below |
| matches telepath's adjective-noun shape? | 🔴 no, it was noun.adjective | ✅ `ejectable` + `overzoom` |

⇒ **the differentia is the EJECT verdict**, and that is what the new word names outright where the
old one named the genus of the whole role.

### the exclusion walk — each neighbour, and why the word does not reach it

| the neighbour | its repair | is that an EJECT? |
|---|---|---|
| `forbid.riddles` | state the subject | no — a rewrite |
| `forbid.decode-friction` | name the referent | no — a rewrite |
| `forbid.diffusion` | elevate the needle | no — a promotion |
| `forbid.emphasis-noise` | cut back to one mark | no — a deletion |
| `forbid.obfuscation` | add what was omitted | 🔴 **the inverse** |
| `forbid.purposeless-passages` | drop the section | no — a deletion |
| `forbid.subversive-prose` | name the mechanism | no — a rewrite |
| `require.archaeology-in-notes` | **move the read-trail, and cite it** | ✅ **yes — the one genuine overlap** |

⇒ the single overlap is the species this rule is the genus of, and the dispatch already declares that
relation. **every other neighbour repairs by a rewrite, a promotion, a deletion, or an addition.**

## 🟡 .what the new name deliberately gives up

- `inconsiderate` covered both verdicts — *cut it* and *move it*
- `ejectable-overzoom` names only the second
- ⇒ that is a **narrowing, and it is correct**
  - the CUT verdict is already `require.reflexive-condensation`'s and `forbid.purposeless-passages`'
  - this rule's whole claim was that a THIRD verdict was absent
  - the test keeps both questions — an author still asks *cut?* before *eject?* — and the rule no
    longer claims the first

⇒ on the two words themselves:

- `overzoom` is a coin — a collision sweep on 2026-09-11 returned 0 files org-wide for `term=*zoom*`
  and `term=*eject*`
- `eject` is a cite — it is already this repo's verb for the act, in the self slug
  `has-ejected-the-archaeology`
- ⇒ the fulcrum that carried the open name is now closed:
  `$route/.fulcrums/inventory.of=fulcrums.case=F16-the-name-is-the-genus.md`
