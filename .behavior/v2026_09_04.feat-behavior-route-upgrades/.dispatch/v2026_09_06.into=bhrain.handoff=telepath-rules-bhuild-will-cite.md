## 1. `rule.forbid.subversive-prose` — the one that must be exactly right

🔴 **its test is *"what observation would settle this sentence?"*** — never *"could a reader
disagree with it?"*

the second is a test of **taste**, and a metaphor passes it. you may disagree with *"it took the
cheaper of two doors"*, and no observation on earth settles the disagreement.

### the two shapes, one rule

| shape | reads as | what is absent | example |
|---|---|---|---|
| **shallow** | *insubstantial* | no substance beneath it — the sentence asserts none | *"we carefully considered the tradeoffs"* |
| **squishy** | *unjustifiable* | no justification reaches it — it conforms to whatever the reader wants | *"robust and scalable"* · *"the cheaper of two doors"* · *"✅ F7 free"* |

🟡 `unjustifiable` also means *morally indefensible* in plain english, so **`squishy` is the
canonical term** and `unjustifiable` is its gloss — never a contract word.

### the enumeration that proves one question is not enough

one claim, three ways — *the guard checks reviews at the wrong moment*:

| # | prose | is there a claim? | what observation settles it? |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | *"the review flow has some issues worth a look"* | ❌ | — |
| 2 | *"it took the cheaper of two doors that both looked legitimate"* | ✅ | 🔴 **none** |
| 3 | *"`runStoneGuardReviews` never calls `getStone…Unforgiven`, so the check runs at passage"* | ✅ | ✅ open the file |

⇒ **a test that asks *"is there a claim?"* answers YES on rows 2 AND 3.** it catches the cheapest
instance and passes the expensive one.

### what the reviewer must do

1. **sort, do not merely flag** — report which shape fired. the repairs differ: shallow is **cut**;
   squishy is **replaced** by the mechanism it stood in front of
2. **run the substitution sub-test on squishy prose** (bhuild#358) — substitute the sentence for the
   mechanism it describes; if the reader can no longer act on it, it displaced rather than explained
3. **hold the false-positive bound** (bhuild#360) — the defect is **ambiguity**, never a rhetorical
   register. a metaphor that *accompanies* a stated mechanism is fine; one that *replaces* it is not
4. **terse is not thin** — a settleable claim stays, however long it runs

⇒ **this is bhrain#444 in full.** if that issue is already in the inflight tree, this rule is done.
