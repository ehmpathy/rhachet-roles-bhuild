`contract.reviewer-output` is satisfied by `N blockers` + `M nitpicks` plus locations.

- that shape is correct for every rubric that grades a **state** on the page
- it is the wrong shape for the one rubric that grades what the page could have been

## 🔴 .why — the ask is to AUTHOR, and the contract does not admit it

| the property | what the reviewer does | reachable? |
|---|---|---|
| an emphasis stack, a wide glyph, an absolute, a 3-claim paragraph | look at the page | ✅ and each is already free, via a `review.for.*` checker |
| *"could this have been said in fewer words?"* | **construct the shorter version, then compare** | ❌ that is a rewrite, and no review contract asks for one |

⇒ so a lane handed the density rubric declines the second and delivers the first, and the decline is
**silent** — a lane that found 4 marks to strip and no cuts is indistinguishable from a lane that
looked for cuts and found none.

## .the ask — one rule, and it binds the OUTPUT rather than the context

a `--refs` cannot close this.

- refs are read and never graded — `compileReviewPrompt.ts:136,140`
  - so a hint about purpose competes against the output contract rather than binds it
- the precedent is `--goal exhaustive`: the same property was asked for in prose, and it took a
  **flag** to land

```md
# rule.require.propose-the-shorter-version

> **a density point is not a point unless it carries a REWRITE.**

per point, emit three things:

1. the passage, quoted verbatim, with its line range
2. the shorter version, written out in full
3. the delta — words before → words after

a point with no rewrite is not a point. drop it.

🟡 the rewrite must be LOSSLESS. if your shorter version drops a distinction the original carried,
that is a **lossy cut** and `rule.require.reflexive-condensation` forbids it — write a different one.
a lossy cut is a defect of the cut, never a property of density.

close with the total: N passages, X words → Y words.

blocker: a density point with no rewrite · a rewrite that drops a distinction the original carried.
false positive: a passage whose purpose genuinely demands its length, named as such with no rewrite
offered — that is a considered KEEP, and it is the honest verdict.
```

## .why it belongs to bhrain rather than to bhuild

the rule governs a **reviewer's output shape**, which is `contract.reviewer-output`'s subject and
`role=reviewer`'s domain. bhuild's behaver owns stage practices and holds no reviewer contract.

⇒ two homes are defensible and the choice is bhrain's:

- `role=reviewer` — it is a contract amendment, and that is where the contract lives
- `role=telepath` — it binds only the density rubric, and that rubric is telepath's
