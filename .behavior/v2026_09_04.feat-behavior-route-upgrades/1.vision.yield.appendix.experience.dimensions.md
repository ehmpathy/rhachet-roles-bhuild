# 1.vision.yield.appendix.experience.dimensions

the experience space for the queued route upgrades, factored into three orthogonal axes and walked
as a product. the inventory (`1.vision.yield.appendix.experience.case=_.md`) verdicts every cell.

## the axes

### axis I — actor (who encounters it)

| value | who |
|-------|-----|
| `driver` | the clone that drives a behavior route, stone to stone |
| `wisher` | the human who approves stones and rules the fulcrum council |
| `maintainer` | the human or clone that edits bhuild templates, or re-inits an extant route |
| `lane` | the reviewer process itself — a system actor, legitimate per `howto.experience.decompose` |

**a system actor earns an axis value when this wish changes what it encounters.** `lane` qualifies:
#349 changes the subject a lane loads, and #354 adds a rubric it runs. `cicd` does not: it runs the
same unscoped sweep before and after (`.github/workflows/.test.yml` runs `THOROUGH=true` on every
push), and #355 edits `5.3.verification.stone` only. cicd is a precondition of the `sweep` lens —
the backstop `case=009` `[t2]` leans on — never a subject of it.

### axis II — lens (which delivered change the actor meets)

| value | concern | issues |
|-------|---------|--------|
| `yagni` | what is not needed; how this could be simpler | #354 |
| `fulcrum` | is this fork real, and is it still owed | #352 #353 |
| `scope` | what a reviewer lane loads as its subject | #349 #365 |
| `audience` | what the vision declares so a reader can orient — who a demo is for, where each fact lives | #346 #362 |
| `sweep` | which tests the driver runs locally | #355 #366 |
| `dispatch` | where an authored dispatch file lands | #342 |
| `scouts-honor` | was an adjacent fix done now, or deferred to a dream | wisher steer 2026-09-14 |
| `tally` | what a route's peer lanes actually found, counted by lane or by rule | #351 #356 |

the prose cluster (#347 #350 #351 #356 #357 #358 #360) is no lens: the wisher dropped it (F22).
its measurement half survived as `rhx tally.reviews.peer`, which counts what extant lanes found, so
it earns the `tally` lens. it surfaced at execution and was cataloged at verification
(`rule.require.experience-catalog-evolution`).

### axis III — feel (what the actor feels)

| value | what |
|-------|------|
| `happy` | glides to the outcome |
| `sharp` | hits an edge that can cut |

## the product

`4 actors × 8 lenses × 2 feels = 64 coordinates`

six coordinates hold more than one cell — three `scouts-honor` ones and `lane × fulcrum × happy`
hold two, one per instrument; `maintainer × yagni × sharp` holds two, one per kept file (the guard
and the stone); and `lane × scope × sharp` holds three, one per bound (too wide, empty, and
under-reach). the last three surfaced at verification, so the inventory verdicts 71 cells. every
cell is **demoed** / **itemized** /
**forbidden** / **impossible**.

## why these axes are orthogonal

- **actor ⟂ lens** — each lens changes shared machinery, so any actor can meet any lens, with one
  two whole-row exceptions: `lane × sweep` and `lane × tally`. zero of the peer lanes at 5.3 grade
  test **selection**, and zero run or read the tally — it reads what lanes wrote. each is impossible
  **by the shipped design**, not by nature — the cells go live if a later route ships such a lane.
- **feel ⟂ (actor × lens)** — every live pair has both a glide and an edge. a gate that can only
  pass is not a gate.

🟡 the one covariance: **self vs peer review is already the actor axis.** a self review is lived by
`actor = driver`; a peer lane by `actor = lane`. so a lens that ships self-only has no `lane` row by
construction — that is why `sweep` has none and `fulcrum` (self reviews plus a close lane) has one.

## the candidates considered

| candidate | disposition | why |
|-----------|-------------|-----|
| actor | ✅ axis I | |
| feel-trigger | ✅ axis III, as `feel` | the trigger is the cell's own edge |
| goal | ⛔ covaries with `actor × lens` | each lens fixes the actor's goal within it; `case=008` `[t2]` split a case **file** that spanned two cells, not a cell |
| path-variant | ⛔ folds into `feel` | a lens's two variants are its happy and sharp cells |
| resource-state | ⛔ folds into `feel` | `present` maps to happy, `absent` to sharp, cell for cell |
| concurrency / time | ⛔ zero cells | one actor per stone, one turn at a time |
| stage | ⛔ covaries with lens | `audience` lives at vision, `sweep` at verification |

**the rule: a candidate whose values relabel an extant axis is an attribute, never a dimension** —
it would multiply the space and fill the new cells with `impossible`.
