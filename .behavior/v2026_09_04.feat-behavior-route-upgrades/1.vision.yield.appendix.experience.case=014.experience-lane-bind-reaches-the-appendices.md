# case=014 — the experience lanes bind the appendices, never the yield alone

> **who** — a lane: the `dimensional-decomposition` and `experience-coverage` peer reviewers on `1.vision`
> **came for** — to grade the experience catalog the vision wrote, before the stone passes
>
> cell — `actor=lane · lens=scope · feel=sharp · care=critipath`, the under-reach bound
> — *(no workable fallback: the review renders clean over a subject it never loaded, and naught
> downstream re-reads the appendices it was owed)*

## the narrative

the vision writes its experience contract in three appendix files — the dimensions, the inventory,
and one `case=NNN` demo per critipath. two lanes exist to grade them.

a bind of `$route/1.vision.*.md` reads as though it reaches all three. it does not. bhrain drops a
route file from a review's targets unless it is a yield, a seed, or a fulcrum summary, so that bind
loads `1.vision.yield.md` alone.

the lane still runs. its scope is not empty, so no zero-scope refusal fires, and it renders a
verdict — over the yield's one-line pointer to a catalog it never read. `0 blockers` arrives, and
it reads exactly like a catalog that passed.

**that is the whole hazard: the too-wide bound overflows loud, the empty bound malfunctions loud,
and this one is silent.** so the guard names the appendices outright:

```
--paths-with '$route/1.vision.yield.appendix.experience.*.md'
```

and a clamp reads each stamped variant, so a later edit that narrows the bind back goes red.

## the demo

```gherkin
given a vision whose experience contract lives in three appendix files

[t0] the lane binds a bare route glob
  given the bind is $route/1.vision.*.md
  when bhrain resolves the review's targets
  then it keeps 1.vision.yield.md and drops the appendices
   and the lane renders a verdict over the yield alone
   and the verdict reads clean, with no error — the silent edge

[t1] the template binds the appendices outright
  given initBehaviorDir stamps 1.vision.guard, light or heavy
  when the two experience lanes are read
  then each binds '$route/1.vision.yield.appendix.experience.*.md'
   and the catalog the lane grades is the catalog the vision wrote

[t2] a later edit narrows the bind back
  when a template drops the appendix glob from either lane
  then initBehaviorDir.integration [case8] fails, per variant, and names the slug
```

## what this proves — the fail-safe

- the fail-safe is the explicit appendix glob plus the `[case8]` clamp, which reads every stamped
  variant rather than trusts one
- 🟡 a route stamped before the fix keeps its narrow bind, since re-init is a findsert (`case=010`).
  `rhx route.guard.upgrade --stone 1.vision` adopts the template's bind
- the root sits in bhrain's target filter (bhuild#496); this guard binds around it, and the clamp
  holds the bind until that filter changes
