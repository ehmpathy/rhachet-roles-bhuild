# case=010 — re-init keeps the old guard, so an extant route gets no new gate

> **who** — a maintainer: a human or clone who upgrades bhuild, then re-inits a route already in flight
> **came for** — to pick up the new prose, yagni, and fulcrum gates on work already underway
>
> cell — `actor=maintainer · lens=prose · feel=sharp · care=critipath`
> plus `actor=maintainer · lens=prose · feel=happy · care=alterpath` at `[t0]` — the fresh stamp
> — *(**fallback:** the new route ships ungated, which is the status quo. demoed as the contrast
> that makes the sharp cell sharp)*

## the narrative

**the happy path first, because it is the contrast that makes the sharp one sharp.** when i stamp a
**new** route, findsert finds no target extant, so it writes every template — the prose lane, the
yagni lane, the fulcrum slugs all arrive with the guard, and i opt in to none of them by hand. that
is the whole delivery mechanism of this wish: a template change reaches a new route for free.

**then the sharp path.** i bump bhuild and re-init an **extant** route, and i expect the same gates
to arrive. they do not.

`initBehaviorDir` is a **findsert**: it skips any target that already exists
(`initBehaviorDir.ts` — *"findsert: skip if extant"*). my route already has `1.vision.guard`, so
the new guard is never copied. i get no prose lane, no yagni lane, no fulcrum slugs — and **no
error**, because a findsert that skips is a success.

this is not a defect in findsert. it is the property that protects me from a silent downgrade of a
`heavy` guard to a `light` one mid-route. the same property that guards me also strands me.

so the answer is not to change findsert. it is to make the skip **visible** and to name the move:

```
🐢 findserted .behavior/v2026_09_04.my-route
   ├─ created  0 files
   └─ kept     8 files
      └─ 🟡 1.vision.guard is older than the template it was stamped from
         3 reviews are in the template and absent from your guard:
           self: has-terse-prose · peer: prose-density · peer: yagni-and-simplicity
         to adopt them, run:
           rhx route.guard.upgrade --stone 1.vision --route .behavior/v2026_09_04.my-route
```

now i know. and i can choose: adopt them mid-route, or finish on the guard i started under.

## the demo

```gherkin
given a bhuild version whose 1.vision template holds 6 self reviews and 4 peer lanes

# --- happy: maintainer × prose × happy ---
[t0] the maintainer stamps a NEW route
  given no $route dir exists yet
  when initBehaviorDir walks the templates
  then findsert finds no target extant and writes every file
   and $route/1.vision.guard arrives with all 6 self reviews and all 4 peer lanes
   and the prose, yagni and fulcrum gates are present with no opt-in step
   and the maintainer took no action beyond the init

# --- sharp: maintainer × prose × sharp ---
[t1] the maintainer re-inits an EXTANT route
  given $route/1.vision.guard holds 5 self reviews and 2 peer lanes, from a prior version
  when initBehaviorDir walks the templates
  then it finds 1.vision.guard extant and skips it, per findsert
   and no new review reaches the route
   and no downgrade of a heavy guard to a light one can occur

[t2] the skip is reported rather than silent
  when init emits its result
  then the kept file is named
   and the reviews present in the template and absent from the route guard are listed
   and the exact command to adopt them is printed

[t3] the maintainer chooses
  when the maintainer runs the named upgrade command
  then the new reviews are added to the route guard
   and the guard variant (light or heavy) is preserved
   and the driver meets the new gates on its next arrival
```

## what this proves — the fail-safe

- the sharp edge is a silent no-op, and the fail-safe is to make it loud. today the skip is
  reported as `kept`, which reads as success; it cannot distinguish *"identical to the template"*
  from *"eight versions behind it"*.
- 🟡 **do not change findsert.** the skip exists so a re-init cannot downgrade a `heavy` guard to a
  `light` one mid-route. a fix that replaced guards on re-init would trade a visible gap for an
  invisible regression.
- this cell is why every gate in this wish is a template change with a migration story. a
  route in flight is the common case, not the exception.
