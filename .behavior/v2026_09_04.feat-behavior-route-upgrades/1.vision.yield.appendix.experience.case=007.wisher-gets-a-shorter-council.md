# case=007 — the wisher gets a shorter, higher-signal council

> **who** — the wisher, a human called to the fulcrum council at the close of a route
> **came for** — to settle the forks that only they can settle, and no others
>
> cell — `actor=wisher · lens=fulcrum · feel=happy · care=alterpath`
> — *(the two fulcrum slugs are new, so the **fallback** is the status quo: the wisher rules four
> forks instead of one. more attention spent, never a wrong outcome. demoed anyway, because the
> saved attention is the lens's entire payoff)*

## the narrative

i am handed the fulcrum inventory. one row.

before these two self reviews, i was handed four, and three of them were not mine to settle. one was
a read the driver owed and skipped — i would have had to say *"go read the file"*. one traced to no
criterion i ever asked for, so i would have been asked to rule on work i never requested. one had two
branches that shipped the identical artifact, so either answer was the same answer.

each of those three cost me a full context load: read the entry, reconstruct the fork, judge it.
three of my four decisions would have produced no change to what ships.

now the one row i receive is a genuine taste fork — two defensible options, no fact settles it, and
the branches ship different artifacts. i read it, i rule, and the route closes.

the three closed rows are still in `.fulcrums/`, each with its verdict and the reason as it stood.
if i disagree with a closure, i can reopen it. i did not have to be present for it to be made.

## the demo

```gherkin
given a route whose driver registered four fulcrums across the drive
  and the two fulcrum self reviews have run before the council

[t0] the wisher opens $route/.fulcrums/inventory.of=fulcrums._.md
  when the wisher reads the summary table
  then exactly one row carries status = open
   and three rows carry status = closed, each with a verdict and a reason

[t1] the wisher rules on the open row
  when the wisher reads the entry: the fork stated fairly, both branches,
       the rework judged clean, and the driver's best guess with its confidence
  then the wisher can settle it without a read of the source
   and the wisher spends one decision rather than four

[t2] the wisher audits a closed row
  when the wisher opens the entry for the row the driver closed by research
  then the entry names the fact that settled it and cites path + line
   and the wisher can reopen the row if it disagrees with the closure
   and no closed row was erased
```

## what this proves

- the council shrinks by **closure**, never by suppression. `[t2]` is the guarantee that makes the
  shrink safe: an audit trail per closed row, so a wrong closure is recoverable.
- the reviews run **before** the council, which is the only moment they can pay off — a row
  questioned after the wisher has read it has already cost what it would cost.
