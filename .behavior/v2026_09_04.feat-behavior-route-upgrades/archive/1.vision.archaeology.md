## the rejected design — a yield SIZE BAR, at 1,400 words

it shipped as `bar-of-yields` in both vision guard variants, plus a skill, a CLI, four operation
files, and six clamps. **the wisher struck it in three sentences** — *"what the fuck is 1,400 bar"*
· *"who said there's a bar?"* · *"thats assenine"*.

three reasons it was the wrong instrument, and the third is the one that generalizes:

| # | the defect |
|---|---|
| 1 | it measures SIZE where the ask was SIGNAL. a 1,400-word yield of pure archaeology passes; a 4,000-word yield of decisions alone fails |
| 2 | it was anchored to the corpus it exists to correct — my own `--stat` verb printed *"do NOT anchor the bar at the p75; this corpus is the state the bar exists to correct"*, and I anchored at a different percentile of the same corpus |
| 3 | 🔴 it recreates the `.taken` loophole the yield itself criticizes — a bounded yield beside an unbounded appendix pressures a driver to hide DECISIONS where no rubric grades them |

⇒ **the number was mine, and nobody asked for one.** the ask was *"crisp, dense, terse, high signal
yields"*, and I converted it into a word count, filed it as fulcrum `F15`, best-guessed it, and
shipped it.

the archaeology lint that replaced it is strictly better on all three: it names the line at fault,
it carries no invented number, and it cannot be satisfied by a relocation of material that belongs
in the yield.

### what the bar's own run taught, before it was struck

the tool did produce one real result. I had recorded that ejection could not close the gap — 11
ejections of archaeology had bought 13%. a second pass against `forbid.ejectable-overzoom` took the
yield 6,338 → 4,719 words in four moves.

⇒ **the bulk was never archaeology.** it was blueprint-grade lane detail a reader of a vision never
needs, and two of the four moves were `declare-once` violations — the experience tables existed
verbatim in `case=_.md` already.

⇒ so `forbid.ejectable-overzoom` and `require.archaeology-in-notes` find **different material**, and
only the overzoom rule finds this class. that claim survives the bar's removal and is what the
`overzoom-of-yields` lane rests on.

⇒ the corpus walks, the unit reversal from lines to words, and the `bar`-versus-`budget` collision
sweep are at `$route/.fulcrums/inventory.of=fulcrums.case=F15-the-yield-size-bar.md`.
