## evaluation

| goal | met by |
|------|--------|
| each issue closed with a note of where it landed | ten land in a lens; seven close on the F22 verdict |
| the route still drives end to end | the gates removed were reviews, never stones; no stone loses its guard |

**pros.** fewer lanes per stone, so fewer rounds and less budget. each kept lane grades a decision,
not a sentence.

**cons.** no lane now catches a bloated yield; a reader pays for it on read. that is the cost the
wisher chose.

**edges, and how each fails safe.**
- a route in flight never sees a new gate — init reports the skip and names `route.guard.upgrade`
  (`case=010`)
- a lane that still overflows is a malfunction, and a malfunction blocks (`case=004` `[t6]`)
- an orphan dispatch flags rather than blocks, since its driver holds no lever (`case=012`)
