# 1.vision.yield.appendix.experience.case=_ — the inventory

the walked product of `actor × lens × feel` — 64 coordinates, 71 cells, each verdicted. six
coordinates hold more than one cell: three `scouts-honor` ones and `lane × fulcrum × happy` hold
two, one per instrument (the peer lane and the self gate; the per-stage lane and the terminal one);
`maintainer × yagni × sharp` holds two, one per kept file (the guard and the stone); and
`lane × scope × sharp` holds three, one per bound (too wide, empty, and under-reach). this is the coverage contract: a critipath here with no demo is a gap.

```
🌴 happy — smooth passage; no edge to guard  ⭐ critipath — failure INTOLERABLE (`freq × cost`)
🌵 sharp — an edge the behavior must guard   🌙 alterpath — failure DEFERRABLE
```

a bullet that ends in a `case=…md` is **demoed**. `— itemized` is a real experience deliberately not
demoed; an itemized `[🌵,·]` cell names its fallback. `— impossible` means no attempt exists, so no
guard is owed. care = `freq × cost`: *if this path broke, is there a workable route to the goal?*

## the six critipaths, and the property they share

**no workable route survives the break** — it is silent, it accrues, or the actor holds no lever.

| cell | why no workable route |
|------|----------------------|
| `maintainer × yagni × sharp` | **silent**: findsert keeps the old guard and reports success |
| `lane × scope × happy` | **accrues**: the workaround is a manual re-run per lane per round |
| `driver × scope × sharp` | **silent**: `--paths-without` parses into an unread key, no error |
| `lane × scope × sharp`, under-reach | **silent**: the lane grades the yield alone and renders clean |
| `driver × dispatch × sharp` | **accrues**: a leak into `briefs/` is paid by every future session boot |
| `maintainer × dispatch × sharp` | **no lever**: an unattributable orphan blocks a route whose driver cannot fix it |

every critipath names its own demo; none points at another cell's.

## forbidden = 0, and the one barred act

act, and its refusal fills a sharp cell rather than empties one:

> **INV-1 — a dispatch file may not land under `.agent/**/briefs/`.** the shared briefs dir boots
> into every session, so a one-shot dispatch there is a permanent cost for a task that closes.
> refused by the lint with the `mvsafe` into `$route/.dispatch/` named · demoed at `case=011`
> `[t1]` and `case=012`.

the four `impossible` cells are no barred act: with no sweep lane and no lane that reads the tally,
there is no attempt to refuse.
