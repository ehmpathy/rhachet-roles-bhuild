# 1.vision.yield.appendix.experience.case=_ — the inventory

the walked product of `actor × lens × feel` — 64 coordinates, 67 cells, each verdicted. three
`scouts-honor` coordinates hold one cell per instrument: the peer lane and the self gate. this is
the coverage contract: a critipath here with no demo is a gap.

**each bullet says what the experience IS**, tagged `[feel, care]` and suffixed with its demo.

```
🌴 happy — smooth passage; no edge to guard  ⭐ critipath — failure INTOLERABLE (`freq × cost`)
🌵 sharp — an edge the behavior must guard   🌙 alterpath — failure DEFERRABLE
```

⇒ why these four glyphs, what six were rejected, and the byte test that barred one:
`term=behavior.experience._.choice.reason.md` §*the glyphs*.

🔴 **`feel` is set by the presence of an edge, never by the outcome.** a sharp path routinely
**succeeds** — the brief's `limit` case is *"the successful edge"*: grab the **last** open slot and
you get exactly what you came for, on a boundary where a double-book was one step away. sharp
names the edge and the obligation to fail safe; it does not name a cut that landed.

a bullet that ends in a `case=…md` is **demoed**. one that ends in `— itemized` is a real
experience deliberately not demoed. `— impossible` means no attempt is possible, so no guard is
owed.

🟡 **an itemized bullet names its fallback only where a break would cost the actor.** most itemized
cells are `[🌴,🌙]` — a happy alterpath, where naught broke — so a fallback clause on each would be
one sentence per cell that carries no information. the clause is owed on a `[🌵,·]` itemized cell,
where an edge exists and the reader must know what absorbs it.

🟡 **`impossible` is about the absence of an attempt, never about the absence of a choice.** both
impossible cells are barred by the **shipped design** rather than by nature — no law prevents a peer
lane that grades test selection; we did not ship one. with no lane there is no attempt to reject.
each names the contingency that would make it live. the `lane × fulcrum` pair is a live itemized
pair: the `fulcrums-self-sufficient` peer lane grades `.fulcrums/`, so a lane meets a fulcrum.

care = `freq × cost`.

## the prune pass — `rule.always.prune-the-experience-space`

run over the 64 cells before the verdicts are read. **it found no new prune, and it reclassified
four cells that were already one.**

### the two `impossible` cells are PRUNES, and they were recorded as descriptions

| subset | cells | lever | the cost — what is now unguarded |
|---|---|---|---|
| `lane × sweep × {happy,sharp}` | **2** | **guard** — no peer lane at 5.3 grades test *selection* | an under-scoped sweep is caught by cicd, one stage later and at full cost, or not at all |

⇒ **the reclassification is what this pass bought.** `impossible` says *no attempt exists*; it says
naught about **why we chose that**, and naught about what the choice costs. the cell was a design
decision — we shipped no lane with that subject — and rested on an unstated cost until now.

🔴 **the `lane × fulcrum` pair is live.** the driver-owned close lane `fulcrums-self-sufficient`
runs at vision + execution and grades the `.fulcrums/` inventory, so a lane meets a fulcrum. the
contract holds the pair inside the walk, per `rule.require.experience-catalog-evolution`.

🟡 and the rule's own bound applies to itself here: neither is a **critipath** pruned to avoid a
demo. both were absent before this wish and stay absent after it; the prune records a state the
design already held rather than one it created.

### the live cue that fired, and why it is a MERGE rather than a prune

> *"an axis has a value that only ever appears in itemized cells — does that value need to exist?"*

**`actor = wisher` is 16 of 64 cells, every one an alterpath 🌙, and one demoed.** the cue fires.

the answer is **no prune**, and the reason is the precondition:

- the 7 `wisher × * × happy` cells all carry one claim — *"receives a smaller, terser artifact"*
  - that is a **merge**: one concept at seven coordinates, a defect in the model
  - ⇒ the notes are the merge's record; the cells stay, because the lens still parts their *sharp* twins
- the actor itself is **not** prunable — the wisher is the approval authority, so to drop the row is
  to stop the question of what a change costs the party who must approve it
  - ⇒ a thin row is a **cheap** row, never an absent one

### why the space was already minimal — stated, per the rule

the prune happened **before the walk**, which is the right order and is why the pass found little:
the wish's twelve issues collapsed to **seven changes** because four of them (#347 #350 #351 #356)
request one reviewer pair at four scopes. delivered literally that is four lenses; delivered as one
it is `prose`.

⇒ **a collapse performed at design time is a prune the walk never has to make.** had the twelve
shipped as twelve lenses, the product would be `4 × 12 × 2 = 96` cells — **40 more**, and not one of
them a new experience.

## lens = `scouts-honor` — was an adjacent fix done now, or deferred to a dream

🔴 **this lens is a driver-owned close discipline: was an adjacent fix done now, or deferred?** a
peer lane at vision + execution grades `$route/dreams/*`, so a dream that reads as a small safe +
clean + adjacent fix is a blocker (*"if its a fix thats adjacent to the work, it should be fixed

code open**, so the unoverridable instrument is the one that belongs there (`F19`).

| `driver × dispatch × sharp` | **silent + permanent**: a leak into `.agent/**/briefs/` is paid by every future session boot, forever. #342 records three already | per dispatch × high |
| `maintainer × dispatch × sharp` | an unattributable orphan blocks a route whose driver holds **no lever** to fix it | rare × ∞ |

⇒ **each shape sets one factor of `freq × cost` past recovery.** a **silent** break sets `cost → ∞`
— you cannot recover what you cannot detect. a break that **accrues** sets `freq` high enough that a
fallback workable per incident still drowns in aggregate. **no lever** sets `cost → ∞` by a second
route: the break is detected, and the actor holds no command that would move it. every other cell in
the walk has a fallback that is both signalled and cheap.

🟡 **five sharp cells were re-verdicted out of this set**, each because a named, shipped fallback
reaches the same goal: `yagni` (the blueprint self slug), `fulcrum` (the wisher says "go read it"),
`lane × scope × sharp` (the printed scoped re-run), `audience` (infer from prose voice), `sweep`
(cicd, before release). all five stay demoed — a re-verdict of care changes what is **obligated**,
never what is **shown**.

### 🔴 every critipath names its own demo — no cross-cell pointer

**no bullet points at another cell's demo.** the check is one grep: a ⭐ bullet with no `case=…md`
suffix would be an absent critipath, and there are none.

a cross-cell pointer is forbidden because it hides a defect. a critipath with no demo is either a
cell that owes one, or a cell that was never a critipath; a pointer answers neither.

one principle sets care on the happy cells:

- **for a newly added gate, the happy path's fallback is the world before the gate**
- ⇒ so the happy cell of a new gate is an alterpath — unless the status quo it replaces is itself
  broken

exactly one lens fails that exception test: **`scope`**, whose status quo is a *stalled stone* (a
lane renders no verdict, the guard cannot assume zero, and passage blocks indefinitely). so
`lane×scope×happy` stays critipath. the eighth row, `lane×scope×sharp`, earned a real demo at
`case=004` `[t6]`.

### the care axis is categorical, so a subset repair under-fills it

🟡 **a lane samples; a rule-shaped defect is categorical.** the happy-cell principle governs every
happy cell, so a repair that touches only the cells a lane named leaves the rest of the axis wrong.
the honest repair is a full re-walk of the axis, never the subset.

the check that catches this before a lane does: *"what is the most general statement of what i just
wrote, and does the artifact obey it everywhere?"* — asked of every cell the principle governs.

the happy and sharp halves need different tests. the happy-cell principle does not apply to sharp
cells — those turn on the raw `freq × cost` product. so a principle that settles one half of an axis
is not a principle for the axis.

### `forbidden` = 0, and the boundary that makes it true

this wish does bar a path by nurture, so the `0` needs its bound stated rather than asserted.
the two are different objects:

| object | question | this wish |
|--------|----------|-----------|
| a forbidden **cell** | can this `actor × lens × feel` occur at all? | none is barred — a cell either occurs, or has no actor to attempt it (the 2 impossible cells) |
| a forbidden **act** | may the actor perform this act? | **yes, one is barred** — see the invariant below |

a nurture-barred act does not empty its cell; it **fills** the sharp one, because the rejection is
itself an experience the actor lives (`howto.experience.decompose` — a nurture-forbidden path
"yields a sharp rejection experience to demo"). so the act is forbidden and the cell is `demoed`.

⇒ the invariant this wish owes, recorded here rather than left implicit in a demo:

> **INV-1 — a dispatch file may not land under `.agent/**/briefs/`.**
> **why:** the shared briefs dir boots into every session, so a one-shot dispatch there is permanent
> context cost paid by every future clone for a task that closes (#342).
> **the rejection:** flagged as a blocker, with the `mvsafe` into `$route/.dispatch/` named.
> **demoed at:** `case=011` `[t1]` (the driver's own leak) and `case=012` (the attribution split —
> an orphan the driver cannot attribute is a **flag**, never a blocker, since no lever exists).

🟡 **INV-1 is the only one.** the other seven lenses are review or placement changes that reject no
attempt: `case=011` `[t2]` (a dispatch with no bound route) is a sharp critipath rather than a
rejection — the attempt is legitimate and the answer names the fix. the `scouts-honor` lens rejects
no attempt either: a deferred adjacent fix is **flagged** as a dream that should have been done now,
never refused — the driver answers in a `.taken`.

INV-1 survives the council's convention-only verdict, because convention-only is not convention with
no lint: #342 point 4 asks for the lint outright, and the F7 entry records it as owed under either
branch. so the verdict removes the code and keeps the guard — INV-1 is live, enforced by the lint.

the 2 `impossible` cells are not further forbidden acts, and the test is the attempt.
`lane × sweep` is impossible **by the shipped design**, not by nature — no law prevents a peer lane
that grades test selection; we did not ship one. but with no lane there is **no attempt**, so there
is no rejection to demonstrate and no fail-loud guard owed. that is the whole difference from INV-1,
where a driver genuinely can write a file into `briefs/` and the behavior must refuse it. the two
cells go live if a later route ships such a lane — `dimensions.md` records the contingency.

🔴 **`lane × fulcrum` shows that contingency in practice.** this route ships the
`fulcrums-self-sufficient` peer lane, so a lane meets a fulcrum and both cells are live.

## boundary density — where to center

| rank | cell | care | boundaries it exercises |
|------|------|------|-------------------------|
| 1 | `driver × prose × sharp` | ⭐ | the gate fires (`case=001`) · it over-fires (`case=003`) · the recursion — exercised by this route's own yields, not by a `[tn]` |
| 2 | `lane × scope × happy` | ⭐ | context gate · verdict rendered · coverage preserved |
| 3 | `driver × fulcrum × sharp` | 🌙 | research-vs-taste · still-a-fork · traces-to-acceptance |
| 4 | `maintainer × dispatch × sharp` | ⭐ | the lint fires on a real leak · it cannot attribute an orphan · its glob reaches across routes |

these four carry the demos that matter most; the other fourteen demoed cells are single-boundary.

rank 3 is an alterpath, and that is the axes at work rather than a contradiction. density and care
are independent: `driver × fulcrum × sharp` exercises three boundaries and has a cheap fallback (the
wisher says "go read it"). density says *demo this one richly*; care says *its absence would be a
nitpick, not a blocker*. `define.experience._.metric=boundary-density` names density a prioritization
metric for exactly this reason — it is not a third axis and it does not set care.

rank 4 reads as single-boundary and is not. `maintainer × dispatch × sharp` carries three
boundaries — the most of any cell outside the top three.

🟡 ⇒ **density is a prioritization aid, never a filter on what earns a demo.** a cell's density is
only knowable after its demo is written, so a cell skipped because it reads thin is a cell never
measured.
