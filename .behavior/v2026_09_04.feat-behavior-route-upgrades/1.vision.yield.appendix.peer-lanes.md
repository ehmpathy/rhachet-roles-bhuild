## the lanes are bound in all 12 guard templates, and the allocation is by what each stage WRITES

**18 lanes across 12 templates** (`grepsafe 'slug: density-of-'` over `templates/`). two tiers, and the
tier is set by the subject the stage authors rather than by taste:

| tier | guards | lanes each |
|---|---|---|
| **prose is the whole output** — execution, vision | `5.1.execution.{from_vision,phase0_to_phaseN}` · `1.vision.guard.{light,heavy}` | 2–3: yields + briefs, plus `density-of-skills` at execution |
| **a yield alone** — criteria, distill, blueprint, evaluation, verification, playtest | `2.1.criteria.blackbox.heavy` · `3.2.distill.{domain,repros.experience}` · `3.3.1.blueprint.product.{light,heavy}` · `5.2.evaluation` · `5.3.verification` · `5.5.playtest` | 1: `density-of-yields` |

🔴 **every lane binds its OWN stage's yield, never `$route/*.yield.md`.** a route-wide glob re-grades
each prior stage's yield — already approved, already answered — and the guard cannot part that
re-litigation from a fresh defect.

⇒ heavy and light bind the same peer set, and the prior split was a defect. the variant axis is
self-review depth (`define.guard-variants-heavy-light`), so a peer lane carried by heavy alone is a
detection hole keyed to a flag rather than to what the stage wrote.

⇒ the briefs subject rode heavy alone while it cost 3 extra lanes; at 1 lane that cost no longer
buys the split.

one allocation call, with its full reason in the lane's own comment: `density-of-skills` is the
`code comments` half of the wisher's hard requirement, and rides execution alone.

## `ejectable-overzoom` earns its membership on possibility, not worth

it grades PLACE where the condensation rules grade a ratio and a count — *could this detail have
been ejected to a zoom?*

| the condensation rules ask | it asks |
|---|---|
| *"is this 95%+ important?"* — a judgment of worth, made once already by the author who wrote it | *"CAN this be ejected to a zoom?"* — possibility, which the author does not get to weigh |

### the `archaeology-of-yields` lint spec

the MOVE verdict is the one a driver can argue with, so a lint names the file and the line — the
repair becomes an edit rather than an argument. it ships in both vision guard variants at `$0` and
1ms:

- **seven markers, five blockers** — a retraction, an attempt trail, a confidence that moved, a
  review round, a deliberation — and **two nitpicks** for words whose referent is a moment rather
  than a state
- it grades PLACE, never prose — a well-written retraction is still a retraction, so it does not
  replace the `dense` lane and a clean run says naught about density
- it reads `$route/*.yield.md` alone: the appendix is where archaeology BELONGS, so a lint over it
  would fine the driver for the ejection the lane exists to demand
- a fenced block and a blockquote are exempt — a verbatim quote may never be tidied

🟡 it catches two marker shapes no brain lane grades — a retraction and a confidence that moved —
each true, each present in a yield a brain reviewer would approve, and neither a claim a reader can
act on.
