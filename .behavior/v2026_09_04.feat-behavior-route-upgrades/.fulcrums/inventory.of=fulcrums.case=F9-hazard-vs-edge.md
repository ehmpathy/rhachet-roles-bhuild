# F9 — the feel discriminator: `hazard` or `edge`

**rework** = clean · **status** = **closed — settled at source, no dispute owed** · **confidence** = 91%

## the fork

while i edited `define.experience._.axis=feel.path=happy-vs-sharp`, my gloss for the sharp pole
drifted from *"an **edge** the behavior must guard"* to *"a **hazard** the behavior must guard"*, and
both words then stood across ~20 sites.

| candidate | the case for it |
|-----------|-----------------|
| `hazard` | names the **obligation** — a hazard demands a guard, where an edge is a mere geometric feature |
| `edge` | extant, ~14 uses across the behaver briefs, and already the word the axis brief's own test uses |

## what settled it — two facts, not a preference

1. 🔴 **`hazard` is already canonical elsewhere, twice over.** mhedic declares it as *"a potential
   adverse effect an exposure can cause"*. bhuild already uses it as *"a class of defect"* —
   `rule.forbid.behavior-hazards`, `.friction-hazards`, `.maintenance-hazards`. a third sense on the
   feel axis is not `rule.forbid.domain-term-inconsistency` (one concept, two words) but
   `rule.forbid.domain-term-ambiguity` (**one word, three concepts**), which is the worse of the pair.
2. **`edge` is canonical by weight of use** — ~14 sites, and it predates the drive.

⇒ **no dispute was opened, because the claim `hazard` was reached for is served in full by the
extant word.** the obligation rides the phrase *"an edge the behavior must guard"*, not the noun. a
dispute is owed when the newcomer names a concept the canonical word cannot; this one names no new
concept.

## why 91% and not higher

**a small real loss.** `edge` alone is geometric; it carries the guard obligation only because the
surrounding phrase supplies it. so every future use must carry the phrase, where `hazard` would have
carried it in one word. i judge that cheaper than a third sense on an already-overloaded word, but it
is a cost rather than a free win.

🟡 **the residue is a discipline, not a defect: the gloss must stay a phrase.** a later author who
shortens *"an edge the behavior must guard"* to *"an edge"* re-opens exactly the gap `hazard` was
reaching for.

## where

`define.experience._.axis=feel.path=happy-vs-sharp`, the three demos (`surf-school`, `camp-beaver`,
`ship-sailor`), `1.vision.stone`, and `1.vision.yield.appendix.experience.case=_.md` — ~20 sites conformed.

`define.experience.md:143` says *"maintenance hazard"* and is **untouched** — that is `hazard` in its
own correct sense.

## the record

`hazard` is on record under `term.synonyms.forbidden` in
`domain.terms/term=experience._.choice._.md`, with a dated `## .disputes` entry in the `.reason` that
names **my own gloss** as its source — so a reader does not mistake it for a candidate that was ever
argued for on the record.

## the lesson

🟡 **i listed both words in my own anti-synonym table** while the drift stood in the prose above it.
a table that forbids synonyms is not a check; the check is a grep.
