## how to read this

- **rework = clean** — a rename, a swapped default, a re-scoped glob. reversal does not ripple.
- **rework = dirty** — callers harden against it, or later work builds upon it, so reversal is a
  teardown. the two dirty rows are the ones worth the wisher's attention first.
- **confidence** — how well i would defend the call. any call under 93% earns a row, even where i
  weighed no alternative.
