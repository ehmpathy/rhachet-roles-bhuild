## .taken, and why

keep the glossary and the required sections inline. cut every passage that is a derivation and
not a declaration.

- the overzoom rule's own false-positive list exempts *"a passage the reader must hold at THIS zoom
  level to act on the claim beside it"*. a term a later claim uses is that passage
- an ejected glossary re-opens every hermeticity point the glossary closed. those points were
  raised in rounds i044 to i046
- a section the stone requires is not an overzoom of the yield. it is the yield's contract

## .why confidence is not higher

the glossary is 31 lines at the head of the yield, and the overzoom lane is right that every reader
pays for it. a middle path exists: declare each term inline at its first use and drop the block.
that costs more prose and spreads the definitions, so i did not take it.
