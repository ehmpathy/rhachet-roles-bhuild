## the fork

the behaver's practice dirs are **stage-scoped**: `behavior.wish/`, `behavior.vision/`,
`behavior.criteria/`, `behavior.blueprint/`, `behavior.roadmap/`, `behavior.execution/`,
`behavior.verification/`.

the new rules are **artifact-scoped** — terseness, yagni, and fulcrum discipline bind every stage
deliverable regardless of stage. they fit no extant dir.

| branch | for | against |
|--------|-----|---------|
| a new `behavior.artifacts/` dir | honest: the rules are cross-stage, and a stage dir would lie about their reach | a new top-level practice cluster is a real addition to the taxonomy |
| duplicate into each stage dir | no taxonomy change | duplicates one rule N times; drift is then certain |
| put them in `behavior.vision/` | the vision is where most of them first fire | false: they fire at blueprint, execution, and verification too |

## what i took, and why at the time

**a new `behavior.artifacts/` dir.** the alternative that avoids a taxonomy change also guarantees
drift, and the rules genuinely are cross-stage. an honest name for the cluster is cheaper than a lie
maintained in four copies.

## why 61%

   is "does this earn its place", which yields no good dir name.
