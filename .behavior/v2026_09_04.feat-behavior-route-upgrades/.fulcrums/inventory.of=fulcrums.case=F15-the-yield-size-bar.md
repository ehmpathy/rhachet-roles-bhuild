## .the fork

a stage yield must be concise, and 13 peer lanes over this one produced 8 density points against a
583-line file. the mechanism that would enforce it is a declared **size bar**, checked
deterministically. the fork is **what the number is**, and who sets it.

| the option | what it means |
|---|---|
| **a. one number, in the shared template** | every stage yield in every repo gets the same bar |
| **b. a per-stage number, declared in each stone** | the vision's bar differs from the criteria's |
| **c. a per-route number, the driver sets and the wisher approves** | maximal fit, and a negotiation every route |
| **d. no number — a ratio of yield : appendices** | scale-free, and it permits an unbounded yield beside an unbounded appendix |

## .taken, and why at the time

**b, at a default of 250 lines**, overridable per stone.

- **d is rejected on a measurement.** this route's ratio is already 583 : 1,960 = **23%**, which
  reads healthy, and the yield is the artifact the wisher called soft. **a ratio cannot catch the
  case where both halves grow together**, which is the case that actually occurred.
- **c is rejected as a cost.** a per-route negotiation spends the wisher's attention on a number,
  every route, which is the resource `rule.always.get-a-second-opinion-before-foreman` exists to
  protect.
- **a is rejected because the stages differ in kind.** a `1.vision` yield holds a design; a
  `5.3.verification` yield holds a proof table. one bar over both would be wrong for one of them.

⇒ **b, with a default, gets the enforcement without the negotiation** — the number is inherited
unless a stone declares otherwise, and a stone that declares otherwise is a reviewable decision
rather than a per-route ask.

## .the bound — what the budget must NOT become

- 🔴 **it must never justify a lossy cut.** the appendix is unbounded precisely so that ejection is
  free and cutting is not. `has-ejected-the-archaeology` already forbids the lossy cut; the budget
  is what finally makes the correct move the cheap one.
- **it does not replace the `dense` lane.** a bar is gameable by denser-but-worse prose — a
  riddle, a decode-friction sentence. the tool measures the SIZE; the brain measures the THOUGHT.
- 🔴 **it is a WORD count.** this entry first asserted the opposite — *"a table row is one line and
  carries more than a prose line, so a line count rewards the shape the canon already wants"* — and
  the reversal is below.
