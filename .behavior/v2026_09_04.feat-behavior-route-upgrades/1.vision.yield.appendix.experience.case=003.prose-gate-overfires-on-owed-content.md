# case=003 — the prose gate over-fires, and fails safe

> **who** — a driver clone at `1.vision`, with a caveat the design must keep in view
> **came for** — to pass the gate without a delete of content a reader must act on
>
> cell — `actor=driver · lens=prose · feel=sharp · care=critipath`

## the narrative

this is the trap the gate itself creates. a density reviewer can push me to cut content that
carries information, and a driver under gate pressure will take the cut rather than argue.

my yield holds three passages that *look* like archaeology:

1. a **dispute record** in a `term=` cluster — dated by contract, evidence mandatory.
2. *"P2 reverses a deliberate prior decision, and here is that decision's stated reason"* — the
   open question i owe the wisher.
3. an **assumption proven false that changes the contract** — a deadlock i caught in self review.

the prose lane flags all three as time-referenced prose. if i obey, i cut the wisher's open
question and ship a design whose reversal nobody notices.

i do not obey. the rule carries its own false-positive list, and i cite it in `.taken.by_self`:

> `[REFUTE]` — passage 2 is a reversal the reader must **act on**; `rule.forbid.chronological-accretion`
> exempts a correction a reader must act on, and `rule.require.brevity` states plainly that
> **terse is not thin**. the passage stays. cited: the rule's own `.caveat` section.

for passage 3 i split the difference, because the rule's line is precise: **the catch stays; the
narration of who caught it when goes.** i keep the false assumption and its consequence, and cut
"caught in self review r2". that is a real repair, not a refusal.

for passage 1 i refute outright — `rule.require.timeless-lessons` exempts a dispute record by name.

the lane re-reads with my `.taken` in context and drops all three points.

## what this proves — the fail-safe

a sharp critipath must fail **safe**: fast, loud, and it teaches the fix. this one does:

- **fast** — the lane raises at the gate, not after the design ships.
- **loud** — three named blockers with file and line, never a silent trim.
- **teaches the fix** — the rubric ships an explicit false-positive list, so the driver learns the
  boundary (*the catch stays; the narration of who caught it when goes*) rather than a guess.

🟡 **the escape hatch is the `.taken`, and it is load-central.** without a legal way to refute with
evidence, a blocker-grade density gate becomes a deletion mandate — and the reviewer would destroy
the exact signal it was built to protect. the convergence loop already provides this;
`rule.always.converge-with-reviewers.via-a-taken-per-point` requires a `[REFUTE]` to cite evidence.
