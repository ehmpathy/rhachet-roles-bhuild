# case=7 — contacts are granted at boot; a clone manages its own only once a human lifts the meter

cells: C1 · C2 · C3 · C6 · C7 · happy (C1, C2, C6) · sharp (C3, C7) · critipath — plus C4, C5, C8 (chosen alterpaths)

## .narrative

a director boots a new crew in the tree `svc-lessons.beav.feat-book-a-lesson`. as part of the boot it grants contacts
both ways: the crew may reach the director, and the director may reach the crew's mechanic and
foreman. contacts are per clone, so each grant runs in the boot of the clone it widens — the
mechanic's boot grants the mechanic, the foreman's boot grants the foreman, and the director runs
its own grant in its own shell. no flag points a grant at another clone or another tree (F11).

once the crew is up, its mechanic asks who it may reach and sees the director plus its own tree.
it cannot add a contact itself. `phone.contacts.uses` is blocked by default, always, for every
clone, so a clone's own `set` or `del` fails loud and names who may lift the meter: a human.

the director is no exception by role. it may manage its own contacts only because its boot hooks
ran `phone.contacts.uses allow` beneath it. so when it boots a second crew, it adds that crew
itself, and when a crew is torn down, it drops the stale contact with `del`.

## .timeline

```
given('[case7] a director tree, and a crew tree just booted by that director')

  when('[t0] the booter grants contacts both ways, each grant in the boot of the clone it widens')
    .note = in the boot of @:mechanic, and again in the boot of @:foreman, in the crew tree
    $ rhx phone.contacts set --address phone://grove=local/tree=notebook/clone=director
    .note = in the director's own shell, in the notebook tree
    $ rhx phone.contacts set \
        --address phone://grove=local/tree=svc-lessons.beav.feat-book-a-lesson/clone=mechanic \
        --address phone://grove=local/tree=svc-lessons.beav.feat-book-a-lesson/clone=foreman

    then('each clone holds its own contacts file, keyed by its serial, with the peers it may reach')
    then('each contact is stored in the full form, grove=local cast to bert-laptop')
    then('a crew clone left out of the boot grant cannot reach the director: the grant is per seat')
    then('a grant of an address whose grove is unregistered, or whose tree is absent on that grove, is refused — walked at [t6] and [t7]')

  when('[t1] a human reads the crew tree meter')
    $ rhx phone.contacts.uses get

    then('it reads blocked — unset is blocked, for every role')

  when('[t2] the crew mechanic asks who it may reach')
    $ rhx phone.contacts get

    then('it shows its own contacts and the clones of its own tree, each with reach state')
      💧 phone.contacts
         ├─ you: phone://grove=bert-laptop/tree=svc-lessons.beav.feat-book-a-lesson/clone=mechanic
         ├─ contacts
         │  └─ phone://grove=bert-laptop/tree=notebook/clone=director   (LIVE)
         └─ own tree
            └─ @:foreman   (LIVE)

  when('[t3] the crew mechanic tries to grant itself a contact')
    $ rhx phone.contacts set --address phone://grove=bert-laptop/tree=svc-forecast.beav.feat-tide-widget/clone=mechanic

    then('it fails loud, exit 2; a clone cannot widen its own reach while the meter is blocked')
    then('the refusal names who may lift it: a human, via rhx phone.contacts.uses allow')
    then('the mechanic may not lift the meter itself: phone.contacts.uses allow from a clone fails loud')

  when('[t4] the director, whose boot hooks lifted its meter, boots a second crew on grove A and adds it')
    .note = the director boot hook ran `phone.contacts.uses allow` beneath the clone
    $ rhx phone.contacts set --address phone://grove=grove-sandpine-v20260901/tree=coachbook.beav.feat-slot-shape/clone=mechanic

    then('the contact is added; the director may now text the second crew')

  when('[t5] the second crew is torn down, and the director drops it')
    $ rhx phone.contacts del --address phone://grove=grove-sandpine-v20260901/tree=coachbook.beav.feat-slot-shape/clone=mechanic

    then('the contact is gone; a text to it is refused as unknown')
    then('a second del of the same address is a no-op, not an error')

  when('[t6] the director grants an address on a grove this grove does not know')
    $ rhx phone.contacts set \
        --address phone://grove=grove-sandpine-v20261001/tree=coachbook.beav.feat-slot-shape/clone=mechanic

    then('it exits 2, a ConstraintError; no contact is written')
      🦫 bummer, that grove is not on the map

      ✋ ConstraintError: grove-sandpine-v20261001 is not a known grove
         ├─ a contact must name a grove rhx git.grove.list knows, or this grove
         ├─ known groves
         │  ├─ bert-laptop (this grove)
         │  └─ grove-sandpine-v20260901
         └─ to add a grove, register it, then grant again

  when('[t7] the director grants a tree that does not exist on a known grove')
    $ rhx phone.contacts set \
        --address phone://grove=bert-laptop/tree=svc-lessons.beav.feat-gone/clone=mechanic

    then('it exits 2 and names the absent tree and the grove it looked on; no contact is written')
```
