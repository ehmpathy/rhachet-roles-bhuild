# F8 — the phone's verb grammar

## .the fork

- **A**: one verb per act — `phone.text`, later `phone.call`, plus a stray `phone.get`
- **B**: `phone.send --via text`
- **C**: `phone.talk --via text|call`, plus `phone.get` for the thread
- **D (taken)**: three nouns, each with get · set · del — `phone.text`, `phone.contacts`, and,
  later, `phone.call`

## .taken, and why

the wisher walked B → C → D. D gives one grammar for every noun (the mechanic's get/set/gen/del
verbs), folds the thread into `phone.text get`, and adds a real act: `phone.text del` withdraws a
text still QUEUED. `phone.call` slots in beside `phone.text` as its own noun, no flag.
