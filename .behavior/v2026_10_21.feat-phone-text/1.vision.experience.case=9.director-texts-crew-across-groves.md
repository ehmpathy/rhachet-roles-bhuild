# case=9 — a director texts a crew on another grove, and the reply finds its way back

cells: S10 · G1 · G2 · G3 · G4 · G7 · happy (S10, G1, G2) · sharp (G3, G4, G7) · critipath

## .narrative

the director runs in the tree `notebook` on `bert-laptop`. a crew runs on the remote grove
`grove-sandpine-v20260901`, in the tree `coachbook.beav.feat-slot-shape`. `bert-laptop` holds the
tunnel to that grove; the grove holds none back.

the director texts the crew mechanic. the text is queued like any other, and the director's daemon
sees the peer sits on another grove. its grove holds the tunnel, so it pushes: the `in` copy lands
in the crew tree over the grove ssh alias, and the say runs there. the director gets a receipt.

the mechanic replies. its daemon cannot reach `bert-laptop` — no tunnel opens that way. so the reply
waits QUEUED in the crew tree, reason `awaits pull from bert-laptop`. on its next tick, the
director's daemon pulls each QUEUED text addressed to its tree from every grove it tunnels to, says
it into the director, and marks the remote copy DELIVERED. the reply carries the uuid of the text it
answers, so the link holds across both groves even though each tree numbered it differently.

later the tunnel drops. a new text to the crew stays QUEUED, reason `grove unreached`, and the
mailbox names the one command that wakes it. the mechanic, which has not yet seen the new text, follows up on the first one
while the tunnel is still down; its follow-up waits in the crew tree, and its own view says no pull has come and who can wake the
tunnel. once the grove wakes, the next tick pushes the text through and pulls the reply, each once.

last, the mechanic tries to text back with `grove=local` — the habit of a clone that has only ever
texted within its own grove. on the remote grove, `local` names `grove-sandpine-v20260901`, where
no tree `notebook` lives. the phone refuses and prints the contact it most likely meant, in the
full form, so the mechanic never routes a text to the wrong grove.
