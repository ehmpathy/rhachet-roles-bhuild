# case=5 — the peer cannot take the text yet; it waits, or fails loud, never drops

cells: P2 · P3 · P4 · P5 · P6 · P7 · P12 · P13 · P15 · sharp · critipath

## .narrative

a director texts a crew mechanic. what happens next depends on the mechanic, not the director.
each outcome maps to one verdict that `rhx clone say` already returns:

- the mechanic is **mid-turn**: `clone say` holds the text in the peer's own submit queue
  (verdict `enqueued`). that is a delivery. the mechanic reads it when its turn ends.
- the mechanic sits **at a modal** (a permission prompt): `clone say` withholds the write
  (`modal-holds-focus`), so the text cannot answer the modal by mistake. it stays QUEUED, its
  record names the reason, and the next tick tries again.
- the mechanic's screen is one rhachet **cannot recognize** (an unknown full-screen view):
  `clone say` withholds (`focus-unrecognized`), since it cannot prove the keys would reach the
  prompt. it stays QUEUED with that reason.
- a human has **half-typed text** in the mechanic's input box: `clone say` withholds
  (`input-region-dirty`). the phone never passes `--force`, so a text never fuses into a human's
  turn. it stays QUEUED with that reason.
- the mechanic is **dead** (or deaf): it stays QUEUED, reason `peer clone is dead`, and goes
  through once the clone is revived under the same slug.
- there is **no such clone** in that tree: a retry will not conjure one. the text settles
  REFUSED with the reason.
- the say **may have landed but rhachet could not confirm it** (verdicts `buffered`, `absent`,
  `unreadable`, or a wedged socket): a resend could say it twice. the text settles FAULTED with
  rhachet's reason and is never resent by the daemon. the sender reads it and decides.

a terminal failure (REFUSED or FAULTED) is not left for the director to stumble on. the daemon
says a short notice into the director's own clone — which text, to whom, why, and the next move —
so an async failure is as loud as a sync one.

in every case the director can ask `rhx phone.text get` and read exactly where each text stands
and why. once DELIVERED, a text is never said again, however many times the daemon ticks.

given('[case5] a director text 6b2d4e90 QUEUED for the crew mechanic; the daemon is up')

  when('[t0] the mechanic is at a modal')
    then('the tick leaves 6b2d4e90 QUEUED, reason "peer at a modal"')
    then('no keystroke reaches the modal')

  when('[t1] the modal is cleared and the daemon ticks')
    then('6b2d4e90 is said once; it settles DELIVERED')

  when('[t2] the director reads its mailbox')
    $ rhx phone.text get

    then('it shows 6b2d4e90 DELIVERED, and every other text with its status and reason')

  when('[t3] the daemon ticks again, and again')
    then('6b2d4e90 is not said a second time — delivered once')

given('[case5b] a text QUEUED for a dead peer')
  when('[t0] the daemon ticks')
    then('it stays QUEUED, reason "peer clone is dead"')
  when('[t1] the peer is revived under the same slug, and the daemon ticks')
    then('it is said once and settles DELIVERED')

    then('it is not resent — a FAULTED text waits on its sender')

given('[case5f] a text QUEUED while the peer screen is one rhachet cannot recognize')
  when('[t0] the daemon ticks')
    then('clone say withholds it (focus-unrecognized); it stays QUEUED, reason "peer screen unrecognized"')
    then('no keystroke reaches the peer; the phone never forces')
  when('[t1] the peer returns to its prompt, and the daemon ticks')
    then('it is said once and settles DELIVERED')
