# case=3 — a director texts a group of three crews

cells: S4 · P1 · happy · critipath — #491's group done-test

## .narrative

the director must tell three crews the same news: the `coachbook` api changed its slot shape,
pause any write to it until the new client ships. three crews, three trees.

one act reaches all three. the director names each address with a repeated `--to`. the phone
checks every address before it queues any; all three are contacts, so it queues one text per
peer, all under one group id, so a reader sees they were one act.

the daemon delivers each copy to its peer. each crew replies on its own; each reply comes back to
the director as its own message. the director now holds three answers, and the mailbox shows
which crew said what.
