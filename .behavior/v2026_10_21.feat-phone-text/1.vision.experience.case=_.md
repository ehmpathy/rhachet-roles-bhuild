# 1.vision.experience.case=_ — the inventory

the product of `1.vision.experience.dimensions.md`, walked as its five closed projections. every
cell carries a verdict: **demoed** (a `case=N` file) · **itemized** (on record, not demoed) ·
**forbidden** (attemptable; the phone must refuse it — an invariant) · **impossible** (no attempt
can occur). each real experience names its **feel** (happy · sharp) and **care** (critipath ·
alterpath, care = freq × cost).

cells are named by projection — `S` send · `P` delivery · `G` across groves · `C` contacts · `A`
audit and upkeep — never `D`, which names an axis.
