# case=11 — a revived clone keeps its seat: its contacts and its thread carry over

cells: C9 · C10 · P6 · happy · critipath (C9) · sharp · alterpath (C10)

## .narrative

a crew's mechanic dies mid-route, which is routine: a reboot, a crash, a cap. the booter enrolls
it again under the same slug. rhachet reuses a slug only while it names a LIVE clone, so the dead
one's slug is rebound to a fresh serial.

the phone keys a mailbox and a contacts file by seat — the slug, or the serial where a clone has
none (F24, ruled per seat). so the revived mechanic sits in the seat `mechanic`, and finds the
seat as the dead clone left it: the director in its contacts, the thread in its mailbox. it texts
the director first, with no re-grant, and the conversation resumes from either side.

the director's side holds as well. it addresses the crew as `clone=mechanic`, a slug resolves at
delivery, and each `in` copy lands in the seat's mailbox, so a text sent before the revive and one
sent after sit in one thread.

a clone enrolled with no slug has no seat beyond its serial: its address is its serial, so a revive
gives it a new address, a new mailbox, and no contacts. a peer that held the old one texts a DEAD
serial, and the phone settles that REFUSED with the reason, since no revive can reach that serial
again (P6).

## .timeline

```
given('[case11] a crew mechanic with the director in its contacts, mid-thread with the director')

  when('[t0] the mechanic dies and the booter enrolls it again under the slug mechanic')
    then('rhachet binds the slug to a fresh serial; the seat mechanic keeps its dirs')

  when('[t1] the revived mechanic asks who it may reach')
    $ rhx phone.contacts get

    then('its contacts still hold the director')
      💧 phone.contacts
         ├─ you: phone://grove=bert-laptop/tree=svc-lessons.beav.feat-book-a-lesson/clone=mechanic
         ├─ contacts
         │  └─ phone://grove=bert-laptop/tree=notebook/clone=director   (LIVE)
         └─ own tree
            └─ @:foreman   (LIVE)

  when('[t2] the revived mechanic texts the director first')
    $ rhx phone.text set --to phone://grove=local/tree=notebook/clone=director --what 'back up, mid route'

    then('it is admitted, with no re-grant, and returns QUEUED')

  when('[t3] the director texts the mechanic by slug')
    $ rhx phone.text set --to phone://grove=local/tree=svc-lessons.beav.feat-book-a-lesson/clone=mechanic --what 'status?'

    then('the slug resolves to the fresh serial at delivery, and the text is DELIVERED')
    then('its in copy lands in the seat mailbox, beside the texts from before the revive')

  when('[t5] a peer texts a dead clone that had no slug, by its old serial')
    then('the text settles REFUSED: peer clone <serial> is DEAD and has no slug to revive under')
```
