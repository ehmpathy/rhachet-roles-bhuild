# case=10 — two writers at once: the thread stays ordered, and each text is said once

cells: S26 · P9 · sharp · critipath — the ordered and delivered-once invariants under concurrency

## .narrative

the director fires two texts into its one mailbox in the same instant: one to the svc-lessons mechanic,
one to the coachbook mechanic, each from its own shell. each mints its own uuid, so the two can
never share a name. both reach for the next storage ordinal at once; each claims it by an exclusive
create of the record file, so only one can win, and the other takes the next. the thread holds
both, in the order they were written, and no record is overwritten.

later, the director and the svc-forecast mechanic both text the svc-lessons mechanic at the same
moment, from two trees. two daemons, one per sender tree, each call `clone say` on the same peer.
rhachet's say serializes them into the peer's own queue. the peer reads two messages, one after
the other, never fused, never twice. each sender's record settles DELIVERED once.

## .timeline

```
given('[case10] a director tree, and three crew trees, all on bert-laptop; contacts granted')

  when('[t0] two phone.text set run as the director, from two shells, in the same instant')
    $ rhx phone.text set --to phone://grove=bert-laptop/tree=svc-lessons.beav.feat-book-a-lesson/clone=mechanic --what 'slot race: first write wins'
    $ rhx phone.text set --to phone://grove=bert-laptop/tree=coachbook.beav.feat-slot-shape/clone=mechanic --what 'slot shape: lesson grid'

    then('each mints its own uuid; each claims a distinct storage ordinal by exclusive create')
    then('neither record overwrites the other; both are QUEUED')
    then('rhx phone.text get lists both, in the order they were written, each by its uuid alias')

  when('[t1] the director and the svc-forecast mechanic text the svc-lessons mechanic at the same moment')
    .note = two sender trees, so two daemons, each with its own tick
    then('each daemon calls clone say on the one peer; rhachet serializes the two into its queue')
    then('the peer sees two messages, one after the other, each signed by its own sender')

  when('[t2] both daemons tick again')
    then('each record is DELIVERED once; neither text is said a second time')
    then('the peer mailbox holds two in copies, each with its own uuid')
```
