wish =

wish: ship `phone.text` + `phone.contacts` in the bhuild dispatcher, so a crew and its director exchange plain directed text, both ways, with no pane injection. ships ehmpathy/rhachet-roles-bhuild#491; folds the overlap from #490.

⭐ sponsored, commander order, ready by EOD 2026-10-21.

the why: every director-crew exchange today goes through `git.crew.send`, which types into a pane, and the commander will not keep it. and a crew cannot reach its director at all (one crew had to write an asks file). the phone fixes both directions.

read in full first: `rhx radio.task.pull --via gh.issues --from ehmpathy/rhachet-roles-bhuild --exid 491` and `--exid 490`. #491 is the spec for this delivery. from #490 fold only what overlaps text: the address shape (`<org>/<repo>` + `@:<clone>`) and cross-repo reach on one box. #490's synchronous call stays a caught dream, as #491's future section says.

the want, from #491:
- `phone.text` (async; one to one and group) and `phone.contacts` (who a clone may reach)
- a clone texts only a contact or a clone in its own tree; an unknown address is refused loud, and the refusal names how to add a contact
- contacts supplied at boot by whoever boots the tree: a crew's mechanic and foreman can reach its director; a director can reach its crews
- every text a file on disk under `.agent/.cache/repo=bhuild/role=dispatcher/skill=phone.*/`: durable, ordered, delivered once, readable as the audit trail
- a daemon delivers each text over `rhx clone say`; a reply lands the same way; the sender is notified
- a text the peer cannot take yet (absent, dead, mid-turn, at a modal) stays queued or fails loud with the reason; never dropped
- no keystroke into a peer's terminal; no broadcast; no `phone.call`

critical path, demoed in a test: a director tree's director texts a crew's mechanic in another repo on the same box, and the mechanic texts back; both land as files, both are delivered once.

done means: route through its stones, tests green (integration, no mocks), a commit staged for the 20:00 CT push window, the pr body drafted. no push before that window, no merge, no `--apply`.
